FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS.
CAN I RELOAD OR REUSE LAST YEAR'S SEASON PASS?

Yes. Help us reduce waste and reload last year's Alta Season Pass online. Reloading your current pass allows you to skip the ticket window and head straight for the lift.

Reload your Alta Season Pass >

WHAT BENEFITS COME WITH AN ALTA SEASON PASS?

Enjoy no blackout dates, discounts for family and friends, an Ikon Pass add-on or an Ikon Base Pass add-on option, free skiing at other Wasatch ski destinations, half-priced skiing at Mountain Collective partner resorts, free parking reservations and discounts on services at Alta-owned ski shops.

Season Pass Benefits Fine Print >

ARE PARKING RESERVATIONS REQUIRED?

Parking reservations will be required to ski Alta during the 2026–27 season, Friday–Sunday & holiday periods from 8am–1pm.
