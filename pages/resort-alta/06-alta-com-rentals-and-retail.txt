RENTALS AND RETAIL AT ALTA

Rent, demo or buy gear at one of Alta's ski shops. Even if you brought your own, there’s no better time to try out the latest gear and retail offerings.

Filter by Services
ALTA SKI SHOP

The Alta Ski Shop has three convenient locations—Albion, Wildcat and Alf's.

Check out this year's hottest new gear, pick up rental equipment, or test out some demo skis at either base area location. Stop mid-day at Alf's on-mountain shop for those forgotten items, mid-day essentials or load up on Alta-branded swag. The Alta Gear Store is a great place to find Alta-branded everything, all year round.

ALTA SKI SHOP - ALBION

Meet Alta's full-service ski shop in the Albion Day Lodge, conveniently located in our family-friendly base area near the Sunnyside six-pack lift.

Closed for the Season >

ALTA SKI SHOP - WILDCAT

Stop by the Alta Ski Shop in the Wildcat Ticket Office next to Collins and Wildcat lifts. Don't miss out on our exclusive line of Alta-branded apparel and swag.

Closed for the Season >

ALTA SKI SHOP - ALF'S

The Alta Ski Shop - Alf's is Alta's mid-mountain location in the newly renovated Alf's Restaurant—located at the base of Supreme and Sugarloaf lifts.
