Age group regulations 2026/​2027

 

CHILDREN:

Born 2011 - 2020
Up to the 6th birthday children ski for free when accompanied by a parent. (The person accompanying the child requires a ski pass appropriate to their age group!).
ATTENTION: It is not permitted for an accompanying adult with a regular ski pass to supervise a group of children under 6 years of as part of privately organised ski courses, for example, by ski instructors who have travelled to the resort themselves.
In this case, a separate children ticket or point card must be purchased for each child under the age of 6 too!
When attending a course at a ski school, a ski pass is required. Children under the age of 6 are provided with a free ski pass during their training with the following local ski schools: Skischule CSA, Skischule Top, Skischule Koch, Skischule Frau Holle, Skischule Krallinger. 
Children born 2011 and later requires a valid ski pass or point card to participate in ski school training.

YOUTH:

Born 2008 - 2010
Proof of age with photo identification is required when purchasing the ski pass and may be checked at random throughout the ski area.

REDUCED:

Disabled persons with a degree of disability of at least 70%. An identity card proving at least 70% disability is required.
Seniors: Persons born 1962 or earlier.

Point Cards 2026/2027

Point cards are only valid in the ski area Obertauern. 400 and 250 point-cards are valid for two consecutive winter seasons. 
Variable point-cards (max 150 points) are valid for one season. 

Points cards are NOT valid in the summer season!

The points can only be used by one person and are not transferable.

BUS GROUPS – GROUP TRAVEL 2026/2027

In order to be considered for group discounts a group must count at least 20 adults or youths. 
When purchase at least 20 tickets of the same type (same start date, same period of validity, same group of people, same ski pool), we will provide you with
every 21st ticket as a free ticket for tour guides.
All ski passes must be collected and paid for by the tour guide at the same time! Bus drivers receive a free ski pass when transporting at least 30 people.

ATTENTION! In order to receive the group discount for ski passes with a validity of 4 days or more, the tour operator/organizer must obtain a written price confirmation from the Lift Association in Obertauern (Liftgemeinschaft Obertauern). The price confirmation must include the number of participants, first day of validity of the ski passes, duration of validity of the ski passes, and place of accommodation during the stay. SCHOOL GROUPS 2026/2027

A school group is a closed group of students who are taking a ski course in Obertauern as part of their school-organised sports training.
A school group of 20 or more paying students is entitled to the school group rates. Students are defined as those born in 2007 and younger.
For every 10 paid school tickets, one free ticket is provided for an accompanying person.
