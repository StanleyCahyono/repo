A LARGE 400KM SKI AREA FACING MONT BLANC IN AN AREA OF OUTSTANDING NATURAL BEAUTY
Enjoy the 220 slopes of the Evasion Mont Blanc ski area. A varried skiing for every skiers. A large and beautifull landscape facing Mont Blanc and Megève village between chalet and fir trees. Megève gives you a gourmet skiing – more than 60 restaurants on the slopes. 100% nature and 100% pleasure to enjoy a lovely stay in Megève with friends and family!

The Mont Blanc Evasion area: 227 slopes welcome you young and old on 400km of slopes.

IMPORTANT

Evasion packages, whatever their duration, give access to the Valléen gondola during their validity period. Please note: note that year passes (winter + summer) give access to the Valléen gondola during the winter and summer seasons (during the opening dates of Domaine Evasion Mont Blanc)
