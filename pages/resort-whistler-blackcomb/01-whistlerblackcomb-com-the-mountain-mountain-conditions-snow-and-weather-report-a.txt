ALPINE FORECAST SYNOPSIS

Alpine forecast for today: Cloudy with sunny periods. High 4 °C. Wind west: 20 km/h gusting to 55 km/h. Freezing level: 2500 metres.

Alpine forecast for the week: Mixed sun and cloud conditions through the week, with only a slight chance of isolated showers on Friday. Temperatures will warm from around 3 to 5°C midweek to a high of 11°C on Sunday before cooling slightly early next week.
