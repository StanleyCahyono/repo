Welcome to
LAAX Mountains

LAAX – where freestyle is celebrated. Where unique nature meets an unmistakable lifestyle. LAAX is not just a place in Switzerland, but also a feeling. Here you get everything from a single source: from hotel accommodation and a table at the restaurant to numerous activities, lift tickets, and shopping opportunities.

We invite you to immerse yourself in this world and discover everything LAAX Mountains has to offer. You will love it!
