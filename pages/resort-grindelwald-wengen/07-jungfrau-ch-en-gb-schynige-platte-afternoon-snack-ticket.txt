Afternoon snack on the Schynige Platte
Schynige Platte – Top of Swiss Tradition
1967 m

Look forward to a hearty snack platter with regional specialities such as bacon, dried meat, venison sausage and mountain cheese as well as bread and butter. Plus the best view of the Eiger, Mönch and Jungfrau - whether inside the restaurant or outside on the sun terrace.
