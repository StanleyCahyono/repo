Breakfast ticket Schynige Platte
Schynige Platte – Top of Swiss Tradition
1967 m

Enjoy the railway journey including a breakfast buffet and a glass of sparkling wine. An ideal start to the day – in the middle of the mountains.

Please note: The breakfast can only be booked on the first train connection from Wilderswil at 07:25. The buffet is available until 09:30.
