The Jungfrau-Marathon spectator day pass is valid on 5 September 2026 on the following transport routes:

Interlaken Ost - Eigergletscher (via Grindelwald or Lauterbrunnen and Kleine Scheidegg)

Grindelwald Terminal - Eigergletscher (with the Eiger Express)

Lauterbrunnen BLM - Mürren BLM (via Grütschalp - Winteregg)
