Mt Hutt
CANTERBURY'S LOCAL

Kia ora and welcome to Mt Hutt. Just a hop from Christchurch, you’ll find wide-open terrain, monster snowfalls and that classic Kiwi hospitality we’re known for. Whether you’re chasing freshies, carving corduroy or kicking back at Opuke Kai with a flat white in hand, Hutt’s the place to be.
