New York’s Olympic Legacy Resort Becomes 27th Destination in Global Pass Alliance.

The Mountain Collective is thrilled to welcome Whiteface Mountain to its global roster of iconic ski destinations for the 2025–26 season. Nestled in Wilmington, New York, and renowned for its Olympic heritage and the greatest vertical drop east of the Rockies, Whiteface brings world-class pedigree to the Collective’s Northeast lineup.

With this addition, the Mountain Collective now offers access to 27 legendary resorts across five continents. Passholders can enjoy two days at each destination, plus unlimited 50% off lift tickets—with no blackout dates. Whiteface’s inclusion further strengthens the Collective’s Northeast presence, joining Sunday River and Sugarloaf in Maine, and Quebec’s Le Massif and Bromont.

“We are excited to be included in the Mountain Collective Pass, which will bring new visitors to Whiteface Mountain through this global community of skiers and riders,” said Tait Wardlaw, VP of Sales and Marketing at the New York State Olympic Regional Development Authority. “At the same time, this partnership gives our SKI3 passholders an incredible opportunity to travel the continent and the world in pursuit of more unforgettable skiing and snowboarding experiences.”

“Whiteface not only brings Olympic history and serious vertical to the table, but it also strengthens our presence in the Northeast and increases the value of the pass for skiers worldwide,” said Todd Burnette, CEO of Mountain Collective.

From the Adirondacks to the Alps, from Chile to Japan, the Mountain Collective connects passionate skiers and riders with a global network of world-class terrain. With the addition of Whiteface, that network just got even stronger.
