Direct to Lift Access

All about your Mountain Collective physical pass

If you already have a physical pass from a prior season, continue to use your physical pass in the current season and in any future season in which you are a Mountain Collective pass holder.

New pass holders or pass holders who didn’t have a pass in a prior season, we will mail your physical Mountain Collective pass as long as you already signed your waiver and uploaded your pass holder photo (log into your Mountain Collective account to complete those tasks). Please also make sure your shipping address is correct.

HOW TO GET AND USE YOUR PHYSICAL PASS

Mountain Collective Direct-to-lift Physical Pass
GET YOUR PHYSICAL PASS
Getting your physical pass from Mountain Collective is easy. Simply upload your photo & sign waivers online before your next ski trip and we’ll mail it to you!
USE YOUR PHYSICAL PASS: DIRECT TO LIFT
The biggest benefit of having your Mountain Collective physical pass is skipping the lines for the ticket office and going straight to the lift. Make sure you know where and when your physical pass works.
50% OFF DAYS – ACTION REQUIRED!
Your physical pass is not automatically valid for 50% off lift ticket days. In most cases, discounted lift tickets can be purchased directly from the resorts (online or in-person) and loaded onto your physical pass, after which you can go direct to the lifts. Learn how to use your pass to claim 50% off lift tickets at various Mountain Collective resorts. 
PHYSICAL PASS FAQs & BEST PRACTICES
Have questions about your physical pass? We’ve compiled some FAQs and best practices to give you the best experience with your Mountain Collective pass. If you still need help with your pass or have additional questions about Mountain Collective, you can check out our FAQ page or contact us. We’re here to help!
DON’T HAVE/WANT A PHYSICAL PASS?
No worries! As part of the Mountain Collective, you can still pick up a daily pass at any of our resort destinations. Learn how to use your pass and view resort-specific pick-up instructions.
