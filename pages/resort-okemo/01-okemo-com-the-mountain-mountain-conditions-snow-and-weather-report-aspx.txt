MOUNTAIN UPDATES

 

Saturday, September 26, 2026

Good morning from Okemo!

Fall is making itself right at home in the Green Mountains, and it's a beautiful weekend to get outside and enjoy the season. With cooler temperatures, fresh mountain air, and more color beginning to appear across the landscape, there's no better time to experience the mountain.

Whether you're taking a scenic ride to the summit, exploring with family and friends, or simply looking for a peaceful escape, today offers the perfect opportunity to slow down and enjoy the beauty of Vermont in the fall. Bring your camera, take in the views, and enjoy the sights and sounds of the season from some of the best vantage points around.

Make sure to stop by the Summit Café for lunch, snacks, and refreshments while enjoying the incredible mountain scenery. A warm drink and a mountain view are hard to beat this time of year.
