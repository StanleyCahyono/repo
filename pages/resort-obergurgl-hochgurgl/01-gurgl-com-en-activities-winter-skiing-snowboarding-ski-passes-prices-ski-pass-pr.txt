SKI PASS PRICES

The ski pass for Obergurgl-Hochgurgl gives access to pristine slopes and outstanding skiing. Whether a day pass, multi-day pass or season ticket, the options can be flexibly tailored to your stay. For the biggest variety, the Ötztal Super Ski pass is valid in all Ötztal ski areas for 3 days or more.

GROUP RATES

Groups of 20+ benefit from particularly attractive ski pass rates in the Obergurgl-Hochgurgl ski area. The following group discounts are available:

Pre-season until 18.12.26 & winter season from 01.04.27

12% group discount for adult, child and youth groups

Winter season from 19.12.26 to 31.03.27

2% group discount for adult, child and youth groups
There are no group discounts for Snowkids, senior citizens, or people with disabilities.
The tour guide/bus driver must make the purchase and payment for all participants together.

Important information:

From November 19, 2026 - April 18, 2027, ski passes valid for 3 to 14 days can be used across all open ski resorts in the Ötztal. Ski passes valid for 1 or 2 days can only be used in the Obergurgl-Hochgurgl ski area. 
Photo identification is required without exception to purchase discounted ski tickets.
Discounted tickets for Snowkids, and people with disabilities are only available at the lift ticket office on site.
