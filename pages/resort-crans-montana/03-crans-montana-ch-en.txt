Buy your 2026/27 Skipass

1 May 2026

The Crans-Montana ski lifts have officially launched sales of their season tickets valid from this summer until the end of the 2026/27 winter season.

Buy your ticket

Advance purchase offer

The advance purchase sale for season tickets giving access to the Crans-Montana ski lifts is now underway and will run until 5 July 2026. During this period, season tickets are available at a discounted rate. After this period, they will be available online or at the ticket offices at the standard price.

The different price categories
Epic Pass: a global pass providing access to over 90 ski areas worldwide, including Crans-Montana and Andermatt-Sedrun-Disentis.
Pass One: standard pass.
Pass One R2: preferential rates for second-home owners, valid when you present your tourist tax invoice.
Pass One Crans-Montana: preferential rates for residents of Crans-Montana, Lens, Icogne and Noble-Contrée municipalities.
Day, multi-day and weekly passes: more information coming soon.

Note: all season passes are available with a ‘Fastlane’ option, which allows you to skip the queues on busy days. Epic Pass holders can purchase the ‘Fastlane’ add-on on a daily or seasonal basis.

Validity of season tickets

All season tickets provide access to the ski lifts in Crans-Montana:

in summer 2026, from Saturday 13 June 2026 to Sunday 25 October 2026
in winter 2026/27

Note: opening dates for other Epic Pass destinations may vary.

FAQ
Children up to 5 years old: free if a parent or grandparent holds a pass of the same type. The age of the child on 1 June 2026 applies.
Pass One R2: upon presentation of your tourist tax invoice from Crans-Montana, Lens or Icogne municipalities when collecting the pass. Invoice is expected to be sent out early May 2026.
Pass One R2: valid for a maximum of 2 adults per second home: owner, spouse, cohabiting partner living under the same roof, or adult children. Children (under 21) of the owner(s) are also eligible for the preferential rate, no maximum number.
Residents of Noble-Contrée municipality: provide an official certificate issued by the municipality when collecting your pass.
Epic Pass: several categories of Epic Pass are available and are valid for the 2026 summer season and the 2026/27 winter season. Only the standard ‘Epic Pass’ offers unlimited access to the Crans-Montana ski area.
