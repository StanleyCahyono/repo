About Pass Dynamic 1 Day Lift Ticket

Details

This product corresponds to a package for 1 day in Crans Montana. Please choose the age category on the date of the first day of the visit and not at the time of purchase. Checks are carried out randomly at boxes but also on the domain.

This ticket works for all lifts at Crans Montana.This product is valid for the specified date only, it is personal, non-transferable and non-refundable. If you purchase your ticket shortly before or on your planned ski day, please check the weather conditions and any ski area closures. Also, insurance is available to cover you in case of an accident, illness, or ski area closure (this can be added later during the booking process).

This discount product is for advance purchase only. Online rates are not available at any of Crans Montana's cash desks.

At Crans-Montana, we believe that the love of the great outdoors is passed on through the family. This is why children up to the age of 8 have free access to the facilities (the free lift ticket is only valid for the same period as the parent’s ski pass). To take advantage of this offer, go to the ski lift ticket offices with your child and his piece of ID.

Mobile Friendly

You can redeem your order using your mobile device or a printed voucher.

Pick-up Instructions

If you have provided your Keycard number, the ticket will automatically be transferred to your card when passing the first gate - just go straight through the gate and up the mountain! If you do not own a Keycard or if it says “Needs Card” on your booking confirmation, you can pick up your ticket at one of the points of sale of the Crans-Montana ski area. Bring your mobile device or a printout of this page to the pickup location on the order arrival date. You will be asked a proof of age for children.
