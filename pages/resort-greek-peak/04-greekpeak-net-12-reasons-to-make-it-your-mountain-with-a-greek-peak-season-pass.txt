12 Reasons to Make It YOUR Mountain With a Greek Peak Season Pass

We’re still in the middle of the season, and I’ve already had a few “just one more run” days that turned into… how is it 4:00 already?

That’s what Make it YOUR Mountain is about. It’s leaning into the kind of winter you don’t want to waste.

Greek Peak has been putting real muscle behind the guest experience, especially where it matters most: snow. With about $4 million in recent snowmaking equipment upgrades (and improvements continuing), the goal is pretty simple: more consistency, better coverage, and more days where the mountain looks the way a winter mountain should.

If you’re feeling this season the way I am, here are my best reasons to lock in next season.

1) Because this is YOUR Mountain now

A season pass flips a switch. You’re not “planning a ski day.” You’re just… going skiing. Early laps, midday cruisers, quick sessions.  Whatever fits your life.

2) You’re buying confidence in the season

When a mountain prioritizes snow and keeps investing in it, that’s not just behind-the-scenes stuff. That’s peace of mind. Fewer maybes… more yep, we’re going.

3) New terrain where passholders already love to lap: Chair #5

We’re beginning an East-side expansion with 7–8 new snowmaking trails around Chair #5, and if you know, you know. That’s one of the most-loved zones on the mountain for a reason. More snowmaking terrain over there is the kind of upgrade passholders can actually get excited about.

4) Snowmaking investment you can actually feel

Greek Peak has put about $4 million into snowmaking equipment upgrades, with improvements continuing. You don’t need to know what any piece of equipment is called to notice the difference. You just notice when winter shows up and sticks around.

5) More days feel “worth it”

It’s not just about the big weekends. It’s the random Wednesday, the quick morning session, the “let’s just get a few runs” afternoon. Those are the days that quietly become your favorites and they’re exactly what a season pass is made for.

6) Unlimited access makes the whole season yours

Powder days, groomer days, sunny days, cold days… with a pass, you don’t have to treat every visit like a major event. You can just take what winter gives you and enjoy it.

7) Five-ish days and you’re playing with house money

With day tickets around $120, most passholders hit the “worth it” point in about 5–6 visits (depending on when they bought their pass). After that? You’re skiing on house money and you stop counting dollars and start counting turns.

8) You stop doing the math every time you go

A day ticket makes you ask, “Is it worth it today?”
A season pass makes you ask, “What time are we getting there?”
That mindset shift is bigger than people expect, and it’s one of the best parts of being a passholder.

9) Bring-a-friend days are more fun (and cheaper)

One of the most-used passholder perks: 20% off a ski lift ticket for a friend, with one redemption per day. It’s a great way to build your ski crew… or finally get that friend who “totally wants to try skiing” to actually show up.

10) Partner perks that make your pass feel bigger

Greek Peak passholders are eligible for:

You can go shell (and build warmth underneath) or insulated jacket (easy mode)
Bonus points for: good cuffs, good hood, vents

To redeem, just email the partner at least 48 hours before your visit with a photo of your Greek Peak season pass and license/photo ID, and they’ll send you the online discount details.

11) Midweek lodging at Hope Lake Lodge for 40% less

This one’s sneaky valuable. Passholders can stay midweek at Hope Lake Lodge for 40% less than a regular stay, which makes it way easier to turn “let’s ski tomorrow” into “want to just stay the night?”

12) Your pass can keep paying off after winter ends

If you’re the type who hates putting the mountain away in March… this matters: full access to the Bike Park lifts all summer long in 2026.

Important note: Access for the 2027 Bike Park is not included with the 2026/27 Ski Season Pass.

Insider tip: passholders know the calendar. Here’s the thing: passes go on sale before the season ends, and pricing climbs in stages. If you’re already loving this winter, keep an eye out for the season pass sale — that’s when you can lock in next season and still squeeze more skiing out of this one.

The Bottom Line

If this season has already given you a few of those days where everything clicks — good snow, good turns, good mood — don’t leave next season to chance.

Make it YOUR Mountain. Become a Greek Peak season passholder.
