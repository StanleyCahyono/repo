Bike Park Closing Weekend Info

Closing weekend will look a little different this year, with three days of mountain fun leading up to the end of the 2026 Bike Park season.

Saturday, October 10 – Fall Fest

Bike Park: CLOSED

The Bike Park will be closed to all riders during Fall Fest.

Sunday, October 11 – Normal Operations

Bike Park: 10am–5pm

Regular Bike Park operations return Sunday.

Monday, October 12 – 607 Day

Bike Park: 10am–3pm

Lift Tickets: $6.07

We’re sending off the Bike Park season with one last day of riding – and a special local-style price. Bike Park Season Pass holders are welcome, too.

One Last Ride

If you remember Fall Fest as the traditional end of Bike Park season, that’s no longer the case. The riding continues through Monday!
The Greek Peak Bike Park will officially close for the 2026 season at 3pm on Monday, October 12.
