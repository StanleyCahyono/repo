WELCOME TO GREEK PEAK MOUNTAIN RESORT

Greek Peak Mountain Resort is Central New York’s home mountain and largest ski resort, offering four-season adventure in the scenic Finger Lakes region just minutes from I-81. From skiing and snow tubing to lodging at Hope Lake Lodge, Cascades Indoor Waterpark, Waterfalls Spa, dining, and year-round outdoor activities, Greek Peak brings together mountain fun for every season.

Champions of Winter: Greek Peak Snowmakers Snowmaking Champions

At Greek Peak, we don’t just make snow, we make your winter dreams come true. Our snowmaking team, crowned 2024 champions in SAM Magazine’s “I Am A Snowmaker” Contest, is the reason every turn, glide, and jump feels perfect. They work tirelessly, night and day, ensuring our slopes are ready for you, no matter what the weather brings. When you ski Greek Peak, you’re skiing on the passion and dedication of the snowmakers you helped crown champions. From all of us, thank you for being part of the Greek Peak family. Here’s to more unforgettable days on the slopes, brought to life by the snowmakers and supporters who make winter magic possible.
