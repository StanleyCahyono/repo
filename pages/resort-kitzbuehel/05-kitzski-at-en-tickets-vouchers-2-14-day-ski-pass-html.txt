2-14 day ski pass

Your benefits of the red KitzSki Keycard & the KitzSki Pure Card

> Suitable for one-day and multi-day tickets, season and annual tickets from KitzSki. You can top up your KitzSki Pure Card with any lift ticket in summer as well as in winter. Topping up your card is effortless and can be done from home at any time. For an overview of our current tickets, see the webshop at shop.kitzski.at.

> The KitzSki KeyCard and KitzSki Pure Card are personalised for you when you buy them from our webshop. (Please note that we cannot personalise key cards at the ticket offices or ticket points.)

The personalised KitzSki Keycard and the sustainable wooden KitzSki Pure Card are available for €5.00 each. Please allow 5–7 working days for delivery by post.
