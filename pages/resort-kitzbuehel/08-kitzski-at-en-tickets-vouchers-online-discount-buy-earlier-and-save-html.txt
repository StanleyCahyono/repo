Discount for ski tickets

From now on, purchase ski tickets online at a discount at shop.kitzski.at. The earlier you buy, the bigger the discount – save up to 20%!

Please note the regulations for tickets with online discount:

The tickets are personalised (first name and surname).
Tickets are not transferable.
Tickets cannot be refunded, exchanged or cancelled.

You can find more details in our FAQ and in our GTC.

The earlier you buy, the higher your discount will be:
Save up to 20% on the regular ticket price if you buy your ski ticket early at shop.kitzski.at

Get an online discount for the following ski tickets: 1-day ski tickets as well as 2-14 day ski tickets for any age group (children/youth/adults).

Important: There are only a limited number of discounted tickets available per day, so we recommend you buy your ticket early. Get more details at shop.kitzski.at
