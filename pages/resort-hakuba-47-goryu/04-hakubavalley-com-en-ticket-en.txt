Hakuba Valley Day Pass

Ski and ride all the resorts of the valley on just 1 pass!

Valid at resorts
Jigatake, Kashimayari, White Resort HAKUBA SANOSAKA, ABLE Hakuba Goryu, Hakuba 47, Hakuba Happo-one, Hakuba Iwatake, Tsugaike, Hakuba Norikura and Hakuba Cortina．

The Hakuba Valley All-Mountain Day Pass for the 2026–27 season is scheduled to go on sale around November through the Hakuba Valley Official Webshop.
