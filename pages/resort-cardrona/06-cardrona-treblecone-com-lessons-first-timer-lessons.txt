Who is a First Timer Package for?

A First Timer Package is designed specifically for those who have never skied or snowboarded before. You’ll learn alongside others at the same level and progress step by step, giving you all the time you need to build confidence and skill.

What does a First Timer Package day look like?

A full, structured learning experience.

Arrive on mountain at 8.30am to check in with guest services and collect your learners lift pass

Head to rentals to get fitted with all your gear for the day

Meet on snow for morning lessons starting at 10.30am

Have a 45-minute lunch break at 1pm

Back on snow at 1.45pm for afternoon coaching

Finish at 3.45pm, feeling confident on beginner terrain

Cardrona First Timer Package

Perfect if you’re new to snow and want a lively, purpose-built learning zone. Cardrona’s Learner area is designed to help you progress smoothly while soaking up the full mountain atmosphere.

Your First Timer Package includes:

4.5 hour first timer group lesson at Cardrona

Rental set of skis, boots and poles or snowboard and boots

Rental helmet

Learner lift pass, including conveyor belt access and one uplift on McDougall’s Chondola

Need clothing and accessories?

Upgrade to the First Timer Complete Package at checkout to include jacket, pants and goggles and be ready to step straight onto snow.

Treble Cone First Timer Package

Ideal if you’d prefer a calm, quiet setting while you learn. Treble Cone offers space to focus, connect with nature, and build your skills at your own pace.

Your First Timer Package includes:

4.5 hour first timer group lesson at Treble Cone

Rental set of skis, boots and poles or snowboard and boots

Rental helmet

Lift pass with access to the Learner Slope via the Surface Platter
