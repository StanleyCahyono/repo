From wide open bowls and cruisy trails to steep chutes and playful features, The Remarkables is Queenstown’s iconic mountain. There’s room to push your limits, try something new, and keep progressing. Whether you’re chasing your first big line or stepping things up run by run, you’ll Find Your Freedom here at Remarks.

From perfectly pitched learner slopes to world-renowned black diamond chutes, progression is what it's all about here at The Remarks. Designed to challenge and inspire at every level, the mountain offers terrain that grows with you. Whether you are taking your first turns, refining your technique or seeking out steep technical lines with a view to match.

THE DOOLANS EXPANSION
 a whole new side of freedom

The proposed Doolans expansion at The Remarkables will add 262 hectares of skiable terrain, bringing the ski area to 711 hectares and making The Remarkables the largest ski area in New Zealand by skiable terrain.

‍

The expansion will include a 2.7km gondola connecting Rastusburn to Doolans Basin. A second base lodge will also be added into the Doolans basin.
