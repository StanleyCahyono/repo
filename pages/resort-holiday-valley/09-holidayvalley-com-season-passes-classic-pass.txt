Classic Passes

Holiday Valley Classic season passes offer an entire season of winter excitement plus the luxury of flexibility! Never worry about missing out on a powder day or pristine bluebird conditions ever again.

Ski or ride just 11 times and your Combo pass is paid for.  Plus your season pass gets you access to an array of great passholder benefits.

Do you have someone age 6 and under in your house? When you purchase your pass, they ski/ride for free!

Classic Day Pass valid from opening to 4:30 PM. Classic Night Pass valid from 3:30 PM to 9PM Mondays through Wednesdays, or 10:00 PM Thursdays through Sundays. Classic Combo Pass valid any time during operating hours, opening to close.

Download a Classic Season Pass Application and fax to 716-699-5204.

Fee Free Installment Plans are not available to apply for after August 1.

If your child is enrolled in a Training Center program, please download and fill out the Training Center Acknowledgement.

Grandparents can put their grandkids (up to age 18) on their pass! Family maximum does not apply.
