Learn to Ski or Ride
Activity: Ski
Ability Level: Beginner
Location: Creekside Lodge

For first time skiers or snowboarders ages 12 and above. Daytime lessons include School Haus area lift ticket, rental equipment and first time 1-1/2 hour group ski or snowboard lesson. Register and receive rental equipment at the Creekside Lodge.

Day Lessons offered daily at 10:30AM and 1:30PM.

Nighttime lessons include School Haus area lift ticket, rental equipment and first time 50 minute ski or snowboard lesson. Register and receive rental equipment at the Rental Shop in the Holiday Valley Lodge.

Monday through Saturday Night lessons offered daily at 5:30, 6:30 and 7:30PM.

Sunday Nights lesson times are 3:30, 4:30 and 5:30PM.

Please note that night lessons are not available until Dec. 20, 2025. 

Please arrive at the Creekside Lodge at least 30 minutes prior to your lesson to get your rental equipment and get set up. Lessons leave promptly from night lesson lineup area at the base of the boardwalk conveyor.
