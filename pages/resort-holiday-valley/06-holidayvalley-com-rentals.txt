More Rental Options

Demos, helmets, and more! The High Performance Shop carries a selection of all-mountain, freeriding, women's, mogul and race shaped skis including Atomic, Elan, Rossignol,  Head, Salomon and Blizzard.

The High Performance Shop also is the site of our Tuning and Repair Shop. We can perform binding, boot and ski or snowboard repairs, often on the spot. Leave your skis or board overnight for a fresh tune-up for the morning! (If the repair or tune-up is major, we might need to keep your equipment a bit longer) Helmets, ski poles and tuning accessories are on sale in the High Performance Shop. You can rent helmets in both the Rental Shop and the High Performance Shop.
