Niseko Opening Dates 2026-27: Season Begins 28th November

Niseko United‘s 2026-27 ski season kicks off on 28th November 2026! Here is the full schedule and Niseko opening dates:

Resort Name	Planned Operational Dates	Planned Night Skiing Dates
Niseko Annupuri International Ski Area	28 Nov 2026 – 5 May 2027	12 Dec 2026 – 22 March 2027
Niseko Village Ski Resort	28 Nov 2026 – 4 April 2027	12 Dec 2026 – 22 March 2027
Niseko Tokyu Grand HIRAFU	28 Nov 2026 – 5 May 2027	12 Dec 2026 – 22 March 2027
Niseko Hanazono Resort	28 Nov 2026 – 11 April 2027	12 Dec 2026 – 22 March 2027

 

Operating dates are dependent on weather and snow conditions and are subject to change.

Planning a trip to Niseko? Our comprehensive guide to the Japan ski season will help you determine the best time to visit.

As always, Japan Ski Experience guests can have their pre-arranged Niseko United lift pass waiting for them in their accommodation upon arrival.

 

 

Niseko Opening Dates FAQ
When does Niseko ski resort open?

The Niseko 2026-27 ski season will run from 28th November 2026 – 5th May 2027, weather and conditions permitting.

Which ski areas make up the resort and what are the Niseko opening dates?

Niseko United includes four ski areas:

Niseko Annupuri (28 Nov 2026 – 5 May 2027)
Niseko Village (28 Nov 2026 – 4 April 2027)
Niseko Tokyu Grand HIRAFU (28 Nov 2026 – 5 May 2027)
Niseko Hanazono (28 Nov 2026 – 11 April 2027)
When does night skiing run in Niseko?

Night skiing is scheduled from 12th December 2026 – 22nd March 2027 across all four ski areas.

When is the best time to visit Niseko?

The best time to visit Niseko for peak powder is typically from late December to mid-February. Snow quality remains excellent through February and into March, with fewer crowds later in the season. April is great for skiing groomed runs.

Which Niseko resort stays open the longest?

Niseko Tokyu Grand HIRAFU and Niseko Annupuri usually have the longest seasons, staying open until 5th May 2027, weather permitting.
