The Summer Gondola In September, it operates only on weekends and holidays. From October 3 through 12, it operates daily.

NISEKO VILLAGE

Niseko Village Ski Resort has concluded operations for this season as of today, April 12. We sincerely thank all our guests for visiting us this season. From April 13, all slopes will be completely closed due to the reconstruction of the lift facilities. Skiing from Annupuri Ski Area or Hirafu Ski Area is extremely dangerous, so please refrain from doing so. The newly rebuilt gondola is scheduled to be completed for the 2026-27 season. All our staff look forward to welcoming you again.

GRAND HIRAFU

Our winter season has come to an end as of May 6. 

We would like to express our sincere gratitude to all our guests for visiting us this season.

HANAZONO

Thank you for joining us in the 2025-26 winter season! We look forward to another fantastic winter season with you in 2026-27.
