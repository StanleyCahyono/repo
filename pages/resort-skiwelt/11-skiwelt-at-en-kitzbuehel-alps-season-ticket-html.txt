KITZBÜHEL ALPS - SEASON TICKET
SUMMER

With the Kitzbühel Alps Summercard, you can conquer 40 summer mountain railways and their mountain adventure worlds in the Kitzbüheler Alps. In addition to the Mountain World Wilder Kaiser-Brixental, you can also explore the surrounding region. With this card, you also benefit from over 40 bonus partners! The Kitzbühel Alps Summercard is available at all ticket offices of the participating mountain railways.

Find points of sale
Inclusive services Kitzbüheler Alps Summer Card – season ticket
Validity period: 18.04. - 08.11.2026
Unlimited use of up to 40 lifts and cable cars in the Kitzbühel Alps region
Public transportation ticket for the local buses (in connection with the Kitzbüheler Alpen guest cards, various train connections are also included)
Up to 50% discount at bonus partners such as wildlife parks, swimming pools, museums, zoos and much more.

FAMILY BONUS

If the 2 oldest children/young people in a family have a season ticket, additional children/young people (born 2008 and younger) receive their season tickets free of charge. All you need to do is provide proof of this: Family allowance confirmation (Ö), child benefit notification (D).

Tip

Holders of the Kitzbühel Alps summer season ticket receive a 10% discount when purchasing a mountain and valley ticket in Zell am See, Kaprun or Saalbach-Hinterglemm-Leogang!
