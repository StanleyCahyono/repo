All year round on the mountain

The SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser – Brixental region is one of the largest ski area in the world. With nine access points, modern infrastructure and short journey times, the region is uniquely accessible. In winter, visitors can enjoy state-of-the-art lifts, Austria's largest night skiing area, a variety of toboggan runs, winter hiking trails, fun parks and adventure slopes. In summer, seven mountain theme parks, over 700 km of marked hiking trails and themed trails invite you to explore, as do the panoramic views of more than 70 three-thousand-metre peaks. Family-friendly activities, events and guided tours, as well as over 80 privately run huts serving regional cuisine, make the SkiWelt a year-round meeting point for sports enthusiasts, nature lovers and foodies.

In winter, you can enjoy 275 kilometres of pistes, 81 modern lifts and 80 family-run huts. You can also enjoy fun parks, the SkiWelt Tour route, toboggan runs as well asver 20 valley runs. With the largest night skiing area in Austria, variety is guaranteed! And all of this is just an hour's drive from Salzburg, Innsbruck and Munich! As a 5-star ski resort, SkiWelt offers easy access, a variety of exciting experiences both on and off the slopes, and modern lifts alongside perfectly groomed pistes.
