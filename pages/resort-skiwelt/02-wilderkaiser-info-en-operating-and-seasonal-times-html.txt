SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser-Brixental season times winter 2026/27

In winter, the cable cars in the Wilder Kaiser open the door to almost limitless skiing fun. The SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser - Brixental delights with 275 kilometres of pistes and 81 lifts. In the four Kaiser villages of Ellmau, Going, Scheffau and Söll, you can enjoy direct access to the ski area with three gondola lifts and the Astberg chairlift.

05. December 2026 until 29. March 2027
