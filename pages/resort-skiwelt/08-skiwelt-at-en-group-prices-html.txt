PRE-ORDER TICKETS

Group tickets are not available to purchase online, but can be purchased directly from the on-site ticket office. To pre-order tickets, please call the ticket office in the morning on the day of your arrival. Our on-site team will then prepare your lift tickets. It is not necessary to call us the day before, as experience has shown that the number of participants usually changes at short notice. If you have any further questions, for example about payment methods or parking, please contact the ticket office by telephone or email.
