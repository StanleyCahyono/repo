Operating hours & timetables
All information at a glance

For optimal travel planning: all information on the opening times of all cable cars and timetable of the cogwheel train.
Please note: In spring and autumn, regular maintenance work is carried out. During these times, the cable cars might be out of operation.

Information on the cable cars to  Zugspitze

All opening times and timetables for the cable cars and cogwheel train on Zugspitze. The times listed also apply to public holidays (e.g. Christmas or New Year).

During the listed maintenance times the corresponding cable car is completely closed (the first and last day of maintenance is stated). Inspection times may be subject to change at short notice (e.g. due to bad weather).

Zugspitze will be completely closed during the following periods: 09.–13.11., 16.–20.11. and 23.–26.11.2026.

The ski season at Zugspitze is expected to run from 27.11.2026 to 02.05.2027.

Gletscherbahn	Sep to June 8.45 a.m. – 4.25 p.m. | Jul to Aug 8.15 a.m. – 5.25 p.m.	4 min. | runs every 10 minutes; additional trips as required	with end of operation	09.–13.11. | 16.–20.11. | 23.–26.11.2026
Cogwheel train	see timetable	75 min. from Garmisch-Partenkirchen	2.15 p.m. from Garmisch-Partenkirchen	Mountain route (Grainau- Zugspitze) 09.–13.11. | 16.–26.11.2026
Construction work
Cogwheel train valley route
09.11.–13.11.2026 | 16.11.–20.11.2026

The cogwheel train valley route between Garmisch-Partenkirchen and Grainau is closed. A rail replacement service will been set up along the route. The bus does not stop at the Hausberg and Kreuzeck-/Alpspitzbahn stops. For the Hammersbach stop, a temporary stop has been set up at the intersection of Zugspitzstraße and Höllentalstraße.

Information on the cable cars in the Garmisch-Classic area

All opening times and timetables for the cable cars in the Garmisch-Classic area. The times listed also apply to public holidays (e.g. Christmas or New Year).

During the listed maintenance times the corresponding cable car is completely closed (the first and last day of maintenance is stated). Inspection times may be subject to change at short notice (e.g. due to bad weather).

The Garmisch-Classic ski season is expected to run from mid-December until 29.03.2027.
