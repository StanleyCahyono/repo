Summer tickets
for the Garmisch-Classic area
The Garmisch-Classic Ticket includes one ascent and one descent. It is possible to use the Alpspitzbahn or Kreuzeckbahn cable car. 
The one-way ticket can be used for an ascent or descent either with the Kreuzeckbahn or the Alpspitzbahn cable car.
The 2 peak pass includes a Ticket Zugspitze and a Garmisch-Classic ticket. The day of purchase is the first day of validity. The second day can be chosen freely in the current summer season.
Family tariffs are valid for children from 6 to 18 years accompanied by at least one parent.
Discount for disabled persons with a degree of disability from 60 % and more or „G“. Same discount for registered accompanying persons. Only upon presentation of the disability card.

Important information
Tickets & mountain railway travel

Please note:

Children under the age of 6 travel free when accompanied by a parent or legal guardian. Children from 6 to 15 years pay the child rate, youths from 16 to 18 years pay the youth rate. Family tickets or the “child in the family“ rate: valid for families (children aged 6 to 18 accompanied by at least one parent). ID required for reduced rates.
Note: In the context of skiing lessons, children under 6 also need a valid ticket. 
You are welcome to take your dog with you on our cable cars. The one-off charge for the ascent and descent is 8,00 €.
With a degree of disability from 60 % or “G” we grant discount on ascents and descents as well as round trips with the cable cars and the cogwheel train upon presentation of the disability card. No free transport for people with disabilities in accordance with § 228 SGB IX on the Grainau – Zugspitze cogwheel train line and with the cable cars, chairlifts and surface lifts of Bayerische Zugspitzbahn Bergbahn AG.
No transport of bicycles.
Tickets cannot be transferred.
We would like to point out that when a mountain railway ticket is used, personal data (especially on the readers) is processed for control purposes and to prevent misuse of the ticket. The data will be deleted in accordance with the legal requirements. For more information on the processing of your personal data, please visit https://zugspitze.de/privacy-policy and have a look at the relevant notices on site.
