Proof of age

・When purchasing a “Student” ticket, you are required to present a valid student ID (student card or school ID booklet) that verifies school enrollment. (Proof of age alone is not acceptable.)
・When purchasing a “Senior” ticket, you are required to present a valid ID that verifies your age (passport, driver’s license, health insurance card, My Number card, etc.).
・When purchasing “Student” or “Senior” tickets online, you must upload a photo of the above-mentioned identification. If the documents are incomplete or invalid, your purchase may be canceled.

Birthday Gift

Guests who purchase the above ticket and visit during their birthday month will receive a “Happy Birthday Magnet” as a gift!
Please inform the staff that it is your birthday when purchasing your ticket at the ticket counter.

Remarks

・The Night Amusement Park for the 2026 season will be held on the following limited dates : 25/07/2026(Sat.), 01/08/2026(Sat.), 08/08/2026(Sat.) – 15/08/2026(Sat.), 22/08/2026(Sat.).
・The Night Amusement Park can be enjoyed with a Season Pass・a Resort Ticket (1 Day Ticket・Twilight Ticket・Night Ticket・Disabled 1 Day Ticket・Group Discount 1 Day Ticket)・an Amusement Park 1 Day Ticket.
・The Outdoor Super Jumbo Pool can be enjoyed with a Season Pass・a Resort Ticket (1 Day Ticket・Twilight Ticket・Disabled 1 Day Ticket・Group Discount 1 Day Ticket)・an Outdoor Super Jumbo Pool Ticket.
・The Yotei Gondola can be ridden with a Season Pass・a Resort Ticket (1 Day Ticket・Twilight Ticket・Disabled 1 Day Ticket・Group Discount 1 Day Ticket)・ a Yotei Gondola Round-Trip Ticket.
・If you are accompanying a disabled person, up to one accompanying person per disabled person may purchase tickets with the disability discount. (If the disabled person is in elementary school, the accompanying adult will receive the disability discount for adults.)
・Please note that height and age restrictions apply per play facility.
・Some play facilities may suspend operations due to weather or other circumstances.
・Tickets eligible for discounts may not be used in combination with other discount coupons and services.

Attention

At Rusutsu Resort, tickets obtained through illegal means or involved in resale transactions will be deemed invalid. Therefore, tickets sold on ticket resale sites, auction sites, flea markets, and similar platforms may have been invalidated. Please be aware that invalidated tickets will not grant admission, and we cannot provide compensation such as refunds. We kindly ask you to purchase tickets from authorized ticket sales sites. Thank you for your understanding.

PICK-UP BOXES

With the introduction of the automatic ticketing machines (Pick－up Boxes), tickets can now be purchased online in advance using a credit card through the official website. Simply hold the QR code received after the online purchase over the Pick－up Box scanner, and the ticket will be issued.

How to purchase tickets online

Click on the “Buy Pass” button to purchase your ticket. QR code for ticket redemption will be sent to your registered email address.

Please prepare the QR code for ticket redemption on your smartphone or as a printed copy, and scan it at the automatic ticketing machines (Pick－up Box).

Please collect your ticket. Go to the amusement park!
