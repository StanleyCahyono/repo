Intro

Rusutsu Resort (ルスツ) is considered one of the best ski resorts in Hokkaido. It has a large ski area that covers three mountains, each having a variety of long runs with a good mix of groomed trails, great powder and tree runs. It is located close to Lake Toya (Toyako) and is just on the other side of Mount Yotei from Niseko.

At the base of the ski slopes stand two large hotels, the Rusutsu Resort Hotel and the highrise Westin Rusutsu Resort. Several small pensions can be found in the nearby town center of Rusutsu. Besides skiing, Rusutsu Resort offers numerous other attractions, including restaurants and shops, pool facilities and hot spring baths. Summer activities include golf and an amusement park with over 50 attractions, including seven roller coasters.

Rusutsu Resort
A foreigner-friendly ski resort with good powder and a variety of long runs makes Rusutsu one of the best ski resorts in Hokkaido. Lift ticket prices are lowered during the shoulder seasons and if purchased online.

Getting there and around
Show Google Maps
From Sapporo

A free daily shuttle bus operates between Sapporo (how to get to Sapporo) and the resort during the ski season. Reservations are required by 3pm of the previous day.

During most of the ski season, Donan Bus and Hokkaido Access Network operate multiple buses daily between Sapporo and Rusutsu Resort. Buses continue on to Lake Toya (Donan Bus) and Niseko (Hokkaido Access Network). The fares are drastically different between the two companies, with Donan Bus charging 2600 yen (book online) for the one way trip, while Hokkaido Access Network charges 5500 yen. Reservations are required.

Outside of winter, only Donan Bus operates buses between Sapporo and Lake Toya, which stop at Rusutsu along the way.

From New Chitose Airport

During the ski season, Hokkaido Access Network operates buses between Sapporo's New Chitose Airport and Niseko, which stop at Rusutsu along the way. The one way trip takes about two hours and cost 5500 yen. Reservations are required.

From Niseko

Donan Bus operates buses roughly every 2-3 hours between Rusutsu and Kutchan Station near Niseko around the year. The one way trip takes about one hour and costs 1100 yen. Note that ski buses operating between New Chitose Airport and Niseko via Rusutsu cannot be used for travel just between Rusutsu and Niseko.

From Lake Toya (Toyako)

Around the year, Donan Bus operates buses every 2-4 hours between Lake Toya and Sapporo which stop in Rusutsu along the way. The one way trip between Lake Toya and Rusutsu takes 45 minutes and costs 1400 yen. Reservations are required and can be made via Willer.
