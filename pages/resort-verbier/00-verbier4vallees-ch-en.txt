WELCOME TO
VERBIER 4VALLÉES

Welcome to the largest ski area entirely located in Switzerland! Open for 10 months every year, the Verbier 4Vallées area is paradise for skiers and mountain bikers. Escape to the summits and explore the beautiful scenery around Verbier.

MOUNTAIN BIKING

In the summer, Verbier is a giant playground that attracts many mountain bikers. Downhill, mountain biking, enduro or e-biking: the choice is yours! You can use the lifts to get to the top of the mountains more easily.
