YOUR WINTER STARTS HERE

A 100% pedestrian resort, snow-covered streets and ski-in, ski-out accommodation… Discover what makes Avoriaz unique and book your winter holiday now.

A UNIQUE DESTINATION

Resort opening period:

11th December to 18th April

The only 100% pedestrian resort where the streets are snow-covered, and all residences have ski-in/ski-out access.

Sixty years ago, Avoriaz brought a bold idea to life: a new way to experience the mountains. A completely car-free resort, architecture that follows the contours of the landscape, streets you can explore on skis… At an altitude of 1,800 metres, that vision is still what makes Avoriaz unique. In 2027, Avoriaz celebrates its 60th anniversary and invites you to share in a special year. It’s a chance to revisit the milestones in its history, celebrate the people who bring it to life, and imagine what comes next together.
