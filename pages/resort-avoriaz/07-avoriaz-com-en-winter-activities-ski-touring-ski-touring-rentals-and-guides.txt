Our professionals are here to accompany you:

Cross-country skiing and splitboarding are mountain sports. They require suitable equipment, knowledge of the mountain environment, and skiing/snowboarding techniques of at least intermediate level. If in doubt, ask a professional to accompany you in order to ensure your safety.

Top Ski Shop, Avoriaz
Roland - ESF instructor - and his team offer a range of modern, well maintained equipment from leading brands. Top Ski means more space, more comfort and better service for its customers!
Magasin Antoine Sports
Summer and winter alike, Antoine Sports supports mountain enthusiasts in Avoriaz. Skiing, snowboarding and ski touring in winter; e-MTB, climbing, sales and equipment maintenance in summer, with advice from a team of passionate professionals.
Intersport Centre Shop
Intersport Centre is open every day in the winter season. It offers a wide range of sports products, available to buy or rent. The shop, which extends over some 450m2, has all the equipment you need so you can really make the most of your time in the snow.
Hervé Gaillard
Hervé offers fun and modern teaching techniques, whether you are a beginner or an advanced skier, for children & adults!
Burton Avoriaz Shop
The Burton Store: a space entirely dedicated to products from the Burton Snowboard brand: outfits and snowboard equipment, accessories and the unique THE STASH clothing line. Discover the entire range in store - to buy or hire!
Avoriaz Sports Shop
At Avoriaz Sports, benefit from expert advice and a wide range of equipment. Ski and snowboard rentals in winter, mountain bike rentals in summer, as well as a dedicated retail area offering sports equipment to meet all your mountain needs.
Absolu Sports Shop
Absolu Sport is a shop specializing in the hire of ski equipment, located in the centre of Avoriaz. Our ski-shop will endeavour to meet all your equipment needs whether you're a beginner or experienced skier.
Avoriaz Alpine Ski School
The Avoriaz Alpine Ski School is a school located on the Avoriaz plateau. We have been offering the best in skiing and snowboarding since 1992. We provide lessons suited to all ages and skill levels, in private sessions or small groups.
Skishop Avoriaz Store
We have selected a wide range of skis and boots, guaranteed to meet all needs and requirements. Whether you're a novice, improver or expert skier, you will find all the high quality equipment you need to ensure you fully enjoy the delights of the slopes.
L'ESI d'Avoriaz
The ESI 360° ski and snowboard school and its state-qualified instructors offer you a range of courses adapted to your expectations and needs, for all ages, all levels, and all forms of skiing. Quality and tailor-made teaching.
Easy2Ride "Ski et Snowboard Academy" Avoriaz
Easy2Ride means exclusive welcome, top-quality instruction, and personalized services. Our goal is to share our passion for the mountains and offer a wide range of experiences.
Skiland Shop
Our Skiland team will advise you on the best equipment so you can enjoy the full delights of Avoriaz's fantastic slopes, in complete safety. An experienced, approachable, friendly team who will help you make the right choices.
Superski Shop
Avoriaz's Skiset stores - the leading providers of ski hire equipment - are ideally, and centrally, located throughout the resort. Specialists in equipment sales and hire, providing a high quality service.
Evolution 2
Welcome to Evolution 2, your ski and adventure school in the mountains, both in summer and winter. Our team of instructors guides and supports you in all your activities, ensuring safety and providing personalized teaching.
École du Ski Français ESF
The French Ski School has 180 instructors to teach you downhill skiing, snowboarding, fon skiing, monoskiing, telemark. the majority of instructors speak English, Italian, German, Russian, etc.
Mirfamose Shop
Mir Famose offers you the widest choice in the resort to equip you from head to toe: rental and sale of ski or snowboard equipment, sale of textile articles and accessories.
Bureau des Guides de Morzine Avoriaz
Skiing, snowshoeing, with crampons, ice axes, or at the end of a rope... the Morzine-Avoriaz Guide Office makes you experience unforgettable days!
