RATES
SKI PASSES
in Avoriaz

Check here all the rates to make the most of the ski area, whether for a day, a weekend or a full stay. Two types of passes are available: one dedicated exclusively to the Avoriaz area, and the other giving access to the entire Portes du Soleil, which of course includes Avoriaz. Choose the one that best matches your wishes and the length of your stay.

First, you will find the rates for the Avoriaz sector. Then, further down, you will find the rates for the Portes du Soleil.

These are cash desk rates only.

Ski area and maps

Avoriaz 1800 is located in the heart of Portes du Soleil, one of the largest cross-border ski areas in the world, where 12 French and Swiss resorts are interconnected.

SKI AREA
Lift passes ski/snow

Avoriaz 1800 proposes lift passes for the entire Portes du Soleil ski area, as well as passes for individual sectors of the resort. A whole host of solutions to satisfy the needs and wishes of every wintersports fan.

SKI PASSES
Find the ski area maps

Find the ski area maps for Avoriaz as well as those for the Portes du Soleil ski area. Avoriaz is an integral part of two ski areas: The Avoriaz ski area: 35 ski lifts, 51 runs, 4 snowparks, 4 snowcross zones, The Portes du Soleil ski area: spanning 2 countries, 12 resorts, 196 ski lifts, 286 runs.
