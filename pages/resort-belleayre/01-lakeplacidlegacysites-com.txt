DISCOVER THE LEGACY SITES

 

Two-time Olympic host. Four legendary venues. One unforgettable destination. From Whiteface Mountain to Mt Van Hoevenberg, the Olympic Center, and the Olympic Jumping Complex, the Lake Placid Legacy Sites offer year-round adventure, events, and family fun. They are ready to host your next adventure, so explore the website and start planning your visit today!

Plan your spring visit to the Lake Placid Legacy Sites using our online calendar, and discover all the events and activities we have to offer.
