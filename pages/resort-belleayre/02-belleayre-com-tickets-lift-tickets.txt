Lift Tickets (Area Use Tickets)
2026/27 Lift Tickets On Sale Now!

Daily tickets are your everyday passport to snow-laden sliding, granting access to our lifts and terrain for alpine skiing and snowboarding! The highest skiable peak and longest season in the Catskills means every winter day is a snow day – at a great value!

If you still have your RFID lift ticket from the last time you visited us, hold on to it! New for 2026, we are tossing in a $5 discount for those re-loading their ticket for their next adventure. Just enter the code on the back of the card at checkout, and $5 will automatically be taken off. From there, toss that RFID card in your jacket, revel in your savings, and head straight to the lift!

Lift tickets are date specific and ticket availability is limited. All weekend and holiday period tickets & rentals must be pre-purchased online and in advance of your visit.  If they are sold out online, they will not be available to be purchased here at the mountain or over the phone. Lock in your spot now and guarantee your day on the slopes—plan ahead and make it happen!  Prices vary and are subject to change without notice.

Lift Tickets (Area Use Tickets) along with ski and snowboard equipment are required to be on snow, whether you are riding one of our aerial lifts,  riding one of our surface carpets, or learning to ski or ride in our learning areas. “Viewing Areas” are unavailable.

LIFT TICKETS

Purchase Tickets Online:

Lock in your day on the slopes by purchasing your lift ticket online! If tickets sell out online, they will not be sold in-person at the window, or over the phone.
The earlier you buy your tickets – the better the pricing.
Beware of fraud. It is best to purchase tickets directly from our website.

Save $5 when you reload your RFID Card. Already have one? Reload it online, save a few bucks, and head directly to the lift!  Don’t have an RFID card? No worries, you can purchase your ticket online and bring your email confirmation with the QR code to one of our contactless Ticket Pick-Up Boxes to receive your lift ticket loaded onto a new SKI3 Card. Save your new SKI3 Card to reload online for future visits!

Lift Tickets are not transferable, and your SKI3 Card is associated exclusively with your guest profile.
