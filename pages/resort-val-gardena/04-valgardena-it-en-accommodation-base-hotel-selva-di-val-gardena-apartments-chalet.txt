Description

Chalet Pratesi is the perfect setting for an unforgettable vacation in an idyllic location. The Chalet is unique: it combines being just in he heart of Selva Gardena (in the Dolomites) and offering ski-in and ski-out.

This small and lively village at bottom of Val Gardena is directly connected to the "Sellaronda" ski carousel and welcomes its visitors with an enticing combination of sport and entertainment. The imposing Dolomites surround Selva di Val Gardena, the highest village in the valley, offering a wide range of sports and leisure activities.

UNIQUE EXPERIENCE
One of the undisputed benefits of Chalet Pratesi is its location in the centre of Selva di Val Gardena. In winter you can leave the chalet with your skis on and enjoy the slopes of Val Gardena and the Sella Ronda. The chalet is immersed within a large private garden (for the exclusive use of guests) and in summer the scents of the surrounding meadows will accompany you on the many excursions that this wonderful area has to offer.
An unforgettable stay, far away from the noise and chaos of everyday life.
You can access the Chalet by car, park it in one of the 3 private parking spaces at the property and pick it up on your day of departure. Sip an aperitif during the evening sunset on the large terrace, and feel at home amid the pink-tinted mountains that are unique in the world.

THE CHALET
The spacious, comfortable bedrooms are characterised by a unique atmosphere. The double rooms on the first floor are entirely panelled in original 20th century wood and are the perfect place to relax in complete silence
