Skischool Rental Zentrum
Description

The ski rental of the Ortisei Ski and Snowboard School is located at Piazza Rezia 75/A, right in the heart of Ortisei, next to the ski school office and just a few steps from the pharmacy – perfect for those staying in the center. We also offer a fast and precise ski service to keep your equipment in perfect condition. We are the rental trusted by ski instructors: professionalism, service, and passion at your side on the slopes!
