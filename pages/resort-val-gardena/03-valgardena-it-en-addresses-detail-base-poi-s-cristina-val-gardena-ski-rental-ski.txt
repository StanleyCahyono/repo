Ski Rental Ski School S. Cristina
Description

Do you dream of hitting the slopes on your next holiday with the best skiing equipment? At our ski rental shop you’ll find modern and well-maintained equipment from well-known manufacturers. Our ski instructors and highly qualified staff will be happy to offer advice and recommendations so you can find the right skiing and snowboarding gear.
Whether you’re a beginner or an expert, you can be sure you’ll find the equipment you need for the perfect winter holiday. Expertise, passion, and fair prices are included in our ski rental prices!
