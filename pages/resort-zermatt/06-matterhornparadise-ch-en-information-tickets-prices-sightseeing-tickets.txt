Afternoon Tickets

Afternoon tickets to Schwarzsee or Matterhorn Glacier Paradise can be purchased at our sales points on the day of validity from the specified time. Enjoy the wonderful afternoon mood at the summits and take advantage of discount fares (cannot be combined with other discounts such as the GA, half-fare, Swiss Travel Pass).

Validities & reductions
Bambini up to the age of 8.99 years: free
Children 9 - 15.99 years: 50%
Children with JJunior Travelcard/Children’s Co-Travelcard (accompanied): free on cable cars in Switzerland
GA, half-fare, Swiss Travel Pass: 50% on cable cars in Switzerland
Bike transport included (bikes are not transported between Trockener Steg and Matterhorn Glacier Paradise)
Dogs are carried for free

The amount of the reduction depends on the date of birth. Official ID showing date of birth required.

Access to the Italian area will be sold in the name and for the account of Cervino Spa and Cime Bianche Spa. The exact details of the Italian and Swiss shares will be announced to the clientele by posting at the sales outlets of Zermatt Bergbahnen AG and on the receipt given to the users.

Tickets between Zermatt and Testa Grigia: all prices incl. 8.1% VAT
Tickets between Cervinia and Testa Grigia: all prices incl. 10% VAT

Prices are subject to change. For payments in foreign currencies the exchange rate of the day will be applied.
