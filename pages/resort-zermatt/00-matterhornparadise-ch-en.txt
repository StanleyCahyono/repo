Hiking, Alpinism and Climbing

The Matterhorn Summer Paradise is a vast and varied hiking area with 250 kilometres of signposted trails. There are many opportunities here for walking enthusiasts of all ability levels, from gentle rambles to challenging tours high in the mountains, steep via ferratas and spellbinding Alpine passages. The scenery of the region is breathtakingly beautiful.

Family-friendly paths allow even the youngest hikers the chance to discover the beauty of Zermatt’s natural environment. On your tour of discovery among idyllic Alpine meadows and clear mountain lakes, the sun terraces of the local mountain restaurants provide an opportunity to relax and enjoy the beauty of the mountains and savour the culinary delights of the region. The landscape is also home to marmots, chamois and rare flowers.

Hiking tickets
The Peak Pass offers unlimited travel (not valid for skiers) on the following routes: Zermatt - Matterhorn Glacier Paradise, Zermatt - Rothorn, Zermatt - Gornergrat and Furi - Riffelberg (according to timetable).
Zermatt hiking experiences
The Zermatt hiking experiences offer a wide range of possibilities, including the breathtaking sunrise at Stellisee, various themed hiking trails, matching hiking tickets, the fascinating Zermatt nature and wildlife, refreshing mountain lakes and always the majestic Matterhorn in view.
Zermatt themed hiking trails
There is something special for everyone to discover in Zermatt - be it rare flowers, breathtaking views or art on the Rothorn. Here you can be within reach of the glacier and experience the variety of adventures or simply enjoy yourself.

The Matterhorn
The Matterhorn rises proudly and majestically among the Valais Alps, almost as if it is stretching up to watch over the idyllic village of Zermatt. Anyone who has ever been to Zermatt will understand how that view of the Matterhorn works its magic – each and every day.
