Hiking tickets

The Peak Pass offers you unlimited travel (not valid for skiing) on the following lifts/trains: Zermatt - Matterhorn Glacier Paradise, Zermatt - Rothorn, Zermatt - Gornergrat and Furi - Riffelberg (as timetabled). Admission to the Glacier Palace, the use of the Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn between Randa, Täsch and Zermatt as well as the use of the local bus are all included in the Peak Pass.

Summer option for snow sport:

Reductions:

Bambini up to 8.99 years: free
Children: 9 - 15.99 years, with Junior Card / Grandchild Card (accompanied by a parent / grandparent) free
Dogs are carried for free
Bike transport included (bikes are not transported between Trockener Steg and Matterhorn Glacier Paradise)

The amount of the reduction depends on the date of birth. Offical ID showing date of birth required.

All prices incl. VAT. Prices are subject to change. For payments in foreign currencies the exchange rate of the day will be applied.

Peak Pass

The Peak Pass offers you unlimited travel (as timetablet) on the following lifts/trains:

Zermatt - Furi - Schwarzsee - Trockener Steg - Matterhorn Glacier Paradise
Zermatt - Furi - Riffelberg (via Riffelberg-Express)
Zermatt - Sunnegga - Blauherd - Rothorn (incl. ascent from Findeln to Sunnegga) 
Zermatt - Gornergrat

Admission to the Glacier Palace, the use of the Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn between Randa, Täsch and Zermatt as well as the use of the local bus are all included in the Peak Pass. The Peak Pass is not valid on ski and chairlifts.
