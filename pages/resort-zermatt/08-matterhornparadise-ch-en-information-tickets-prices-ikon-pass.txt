IKON PASS

The Matterhorn Ski Paradise is part of the Ikon Pass since this winter season. The Ikon Pass opens the turnstiles in over 60 destinations worldwide. But also as an owner of the seasonal or annual pass Zermatt or International, you can profit from extra ski days in the Ikon Pass destinations.

Ikon Pass
The Ikon Pass is your opportunity to access unique terrain at 60 of the most iconic winter destinations around the world, join a like-minded community, and seek out new adventures as you explore all that the mountains have to offer.
Ikon Base Pass
The Ikon Base Pass offers the best value with limited restrictions and blackout dates. The Ikon Base Pass is the ideal pass for skiers and riders with more flexibility in their travel schedule, who frequently hit their home mountain and enjoy select days at other legendary Ikon Pass destinations a few times a year.
Destinations
There are over 50 destinations available worldwide with the Ikon Pass and the Ikon Base Pass. You can find more details on the official Ikon Pass website.

As a seasonal or annual ski pass holder of the Zermatt and/or International ski pass (guest tariff) you benefit from additional extra ski days in selected ski areas of the Ikon Pass destinations.

SEASON & ANNUAL PASSES
Seasonal pass winter

The winter season pass is always valid from 1st October until the end of the winter season for the ski area Zermatt. With the Zermatt seasonal pass you get unlimited use of all Zermatt Bergbahnen AG lifts and the Gornergrat railway during the winter season. You can also add the ski area Breuil-Cervinia and Valtournenche (IT). In addition, you will receive “even more Zermatt” vouchers when you buy your pass. 

from CHF 1’750.00

SEASON & ANNUAL PASSES
Annual pass

With the Zermatt annual pass you get unlimited use of all Zermatt Bergbahnen AG and Gornergrat railway lifts/trains for one year. The lifts and facilities of Cervino Spa (Cervinia & Valtournenche) are included in the annual pass during the winter season. In addition, you will receive “even more Zermatt” vouchers when you buy your pass. 

from CHF 2’290.00
