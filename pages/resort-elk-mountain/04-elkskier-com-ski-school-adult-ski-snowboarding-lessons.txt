SKI & SNOWBOARDING LESSONS FOR ADULTS AND CHILDREN

Lessons are helpful to skiers of all levels. We recommend that all first-time skiers, as well as adults that want to brush up on their skills take a lesson.

Elk Mountain strongly recommends that new skiers take at least three group or private lessons.

Advance reservations can be made for 1 or 2 hour private lessons on weekends based on availability. Any cancellation of a reserved lesson must happen no less than 48 hours in advance of the lesson to avoid a canellation fee of 50% the cost of the reserved lesson. Call 570 679 4400, option 4 for details.

Lift tickets may be purchased at the ski school desk when purchasing lessons.

PARENT/CHILD LESSON CLINIC

For parent/guardian and one child age 3-5. 90 minute lesson. Parent/Guardian must be at at least level 3. Lesson times based on instructor availability. Objective: The instructor's goal is to teach the parent/guardian how to work with the child confidently and creatively and safely introducing them to the joy of skiing. Lesson plan will utilize PSIA techniques.

PROGRAM DETAILS

This weekend program is designed for skiers age 6-12 as a "learn to ski (better) opportunity. Participants will enjoy three hours of professional ski instruction with safe and structured lesson plans to improve technique and build self-confidence. Participants will be assigned to their demonstrated individual levels. All levels of proficiency will be accommodated - Beginner (Green/Blue) trail skiers and those who are compfortable skiing all trails. This program is a recommended first step prior to pursuing Juniior Race Training programs (Cruisers).

FIRST-TIME SKIERS, ESTABLISHED SKIERS, OR SNOWBOARDERS LOOKING TO LEARN OR FINE-TUNE THEIR SKILLS CAN DO SO WITH A LESSON FROM A MEMBER OF THE ELK MOUNTAIN SKI SCHOOL.

Our school is a member of the Professional Ski Instructors of America (PSIA) and utilizes the American Teaching System. With a staff of over seventy-five trained instructors, Elk has a program and the staff to meet your individual needs. Elk Mountain takes great pride in its ski school programs and lessons. Class sizes are limited to ensure a quality experience. Lessons may be sold out during busy periods. Further details are available at the ski school desk or by calling 570-679-4400 Extension 4430 (or press Option 4).
