LIFT TICKETS (PRICES UPDATED OCTOBER 2025)

PLEASE READ THE INFORMATION BELOW IN ITS ENTIRETY BEFORE MAKING A PURCHASE.

Lift tickets may be purchased online up to 7 days in advance. Purchase lift tickets using the “Buy Now” button below. If you already have (non transferrable) ticket media from a previous visit to Elk Mountain,  use the “Reload Now” button below and save $5. Please be absolutely certain (check the weather forecast!) before finalizing a sale that you intend to make a final purchase. Items purchased online are not refundable or transferrable to a different date. TICKET DATES CHANGE AT MIDNIGHT. Pricing is the same online as at the ticket windows and we’re not limiting lift tickets so you can wait until the last minute. Check the date on your items before you finalize a purchase. Be sure that you’re purchasing tickets for the date that you want. If purchasing a 2-day ticket, select the date for the first day. 2-day tickets are for consecutive days.

IMPORTANT: When redeeming a QR code for a lift ticket on a cell phone, the dark mode setting cannot be used. Your QR code will not scan. Dark mode must be turned off.

READY TO VISIT ELK MOUNTAIN?

Lifts start loading at 8:30 a.m. each day. Ticket sales, rentals, ski school, and the cafeteria open at 8:00 a.m. each day. We close at 4:30 p.m. Sunday through Wednesday and at 9:00 p.m. Thursday through Saturday when night skiing and snowboarding is scheduled. (Please see schedule of hours on the home page). Come ski or snowboard at Elk Mountain, located in the beautiful Endless Mountains just north of the Poconos. We also invite beginners of all ages to enjoy the slopes at a discounted rate! All lift tickets are valid only for the day and time purchased and are not transferrable to another day or season. Tickets are not refundable if unredeemed/unused.

To purchase tickets online, select the appropriate button above (Buy Now for a first visit or Reload Now if you have an RFID lift ticket that has your name on it from a previous visit to Elk Mountain). Follow the steps and purchase or reload your ticket online. Once your purchase is completed, you will see a confirmation screen showing your sale number. We recommend that you take note of that sale number. If you have reloaded your media from a previous visit to Elk Mountain, you’re all set and can just go directly to the lifts. If you’re purchasing new tickets, you’ll receive a confirmation email containing a QR code to scan at a pickup box in one of our ticket kiosks. The pickup box will dispense your ticket(s) and then you can go directly to the lifts. Please note that the confirmation email may contain a “display images” option that you may need to click to display the QR code. For best results, carry the ticket in a zipped pocket on your left side with nothing else in the pocket. Tickets cannot be reloaded at the pickup boxes in our ticket kiosks but can be reloaded online for future visits.

If there is a QR code on the lift ticket that you have, you may scan it with your phone to reload online. That QR code will not scan at the pickup boxes.
