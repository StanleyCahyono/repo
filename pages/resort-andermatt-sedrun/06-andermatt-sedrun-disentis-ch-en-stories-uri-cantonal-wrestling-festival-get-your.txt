URI CANTONAL WRESTLING FESTIVAL - GET YOUR TICKETS NOW!
Andermatt, May 6, 2025

On Sunday, 15 June 2025, Andermatt Mitte will become the centre of traditional Swiss sport: the 102nd Uri Cantonal Wrestling Festival will bring exciting duels, living traditions and a festive atmosphere to the region. On Saturday, 14 June, young talents will take to the sawdust ring at the young wrestlers' day.

Now is the ideal time to secure your ticket: All seats offer the best view of the arena - perfect conditions for an eventful day in Andermatt! Anyone planning a whole weekend of wrestling can benefit from a 15% discount on overnight stays at the Radisson Blu Hotel Reussen from 13 to 15 June 2025.
