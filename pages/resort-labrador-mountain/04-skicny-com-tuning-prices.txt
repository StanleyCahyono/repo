At Labrador and Song, CNY’s most renowned tuning shops, we specialize in precision tuning and expert repairs for skis and snowboards of all kinds. From full base grinds and stone tunes to edge sharpening, waxing, and binding work, we treat every setup like it’s our own. Whether you’re chasing powder, carving groomers, or lapping the park, we make sure your gear performs exactly the way it should.

Our technicians combine 30-plus years of hands-on experience with top-tier equipment to deliver fast, reliable, and consistent results. We work on everything from race skis and splitboards to daily drivers that need some love after a long season. No upselling, no shortcuts — just honest advice and quality craftsmanship.

Bring us your gear, and we’ll get you back on snow smoother, faster, and more confident.
