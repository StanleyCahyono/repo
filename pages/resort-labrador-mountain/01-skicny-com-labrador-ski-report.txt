This report is effective 5:00 PM, Friday, April 10th.

One of the things you learn early in this business, is that when it comes to the weather, WE ARE NOT IN CHARGE!

We were all really hoping some April skiing and snowboarding BUT, as I just said, WE ARE NOT IN CHARGE 

Song & Labrador are now closed for the season.

Hours for this weekend are as follows:
• Song office and locker rooms OPEN Saturday, April 11 from 10 am to 3 pm, Sunday closed.
• Lab office closed for the weekend..
• Lab locker room OPEN 10 am to 3 pm.
• Clubhouse North OPEN by appointment. Please call the Song office.

2025-26 gave us a nice old fashioned winter; 95 days of just OUTSTANDING sking and snowboarding. We’re on a roll and next season is expected to be more of the same!

Going forward, MARK YOUR CALENDAR AS FOLLOWS:
• The spring Season Pass Sale ends Wednesday, April 15th.
• Heuga’s Alpine reopens for the summer with a new menu Thursday, April 30th.
• Don’t forget to make your reservations early for Mother’s Day Brunch, May 10th.
Visit Heuga’s Alpine dot for details.
• Please make sure you clean out your locker no later than Thursday, April 30th.

A great big THANK YOU from everyone here at Song and Labrador. We know there are choices and sincerely appreciate that you spend your winter time at Song and Labrador. All of us wish you and yours a happy, safe, healthy, and prosperous summer…

Operating hours at both mountains are posted at “HOURS OF OPERATION 7 DAYS OUT”.
