100% SKI PASS IN CHAMONIX

The CHAMONIX LePass pass opens the doors to all Chamonix ski areas (except the Grands Montets summit) for fun skiing on slopes between 1000 and 2765 m altitude

Chamonix ski areas
Brévent-Flégère, Grands Montets (Top closed), Balme - Le Tour - Vallorcine
Begginers skiing areas
La Vormaine, les Chosalets, Le Savoy, Plateau 2000 (Planpraz), Plateau de Lognan, Les Planards and La Poya*

ℹ️ : Our ski areas and sites are not linked, except for Brévent - Flégère areas.
The purchase of the CHAMONIX Le Pass skipass allows you to discover all or part of our territory (depending on the duration of your transport ticket).
