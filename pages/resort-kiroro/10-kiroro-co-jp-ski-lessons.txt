KIRORO SKI & SNOWBOARD ACADEMY
At Kiroro Ski & Snowboard Academy we have 2 keywords: Safety and Fun

We will help everyone to gain progress by using latest teaching methods, from beginners all the way to expert levels.

We ensure that all of our customers will have an enjoyable snow experience.

– Book Online! –
Enjoy 5% discount off regular price to book online!

* Some products are not available for online booking.
* If you cancel a lesson after 180 days from the date of completing a payment, you will be refunded either on-site or by bank transfer.
Please note that in the case of bank transfers, refund amount will be after deducting bank fee.

– All lessons are taught in Japanese (International guests are welcome to join if their Japanese language is sufficient).
– Please visit Kiroro International Academy Page for private lessons with English or Chinese speaking instructors.
Reservations are required in advance (Reservations on the day are subject to availability).
* We highly recommend you to make reservation as soon as possible due to the limited number of places.
※Cancellation policy : Bookings cancelled 7 days or less before the lesson date will be charged at 100%

Ski Theme Lesson Menu

Gate Challenge	10pax	For ages 13 years old and above and can ski with stability using parallel turns on slopes of intermediate difficulty or higher.	Involves challenging gate courses using gate poles. Mastering regulated courses also helps improve turning techniques.

Badge test strategy lesson

	10pax	JSBA Level 1 and 2 Exam Candidates	Lessons for JSBA Level 1 and 2 exam candidates.

Badge test strategy lesson (short version)

	10pax	JSBA Level 1 and 2 Exam Candidates	This lesson focuses on the “short turn” test, which is required for JSBA Level 1 and Level 2 certification. We will teach you tips for passing, such as turn size, speed control, and how to express your movements.

Badge test strategy lesson (long version)

	10pax	JSBA Level 1 and 2 Exam Candidates	This lesson focuses on the “long turn” test, which is required for JSBA Level 1 and Level 2 certification. It is a lesson that aims to improve your skills by teaching carving and basic exercises.
