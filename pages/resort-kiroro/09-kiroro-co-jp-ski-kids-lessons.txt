KIRORO KIDS ACADEMY

At Kiroro Kids Academy, we use our techniques, dedication and our own methods to inspire children to learn actively in a safe area how to ski or snowboard throughout our curriculum aiming to learn how to start parallel turning even from their first time on a slope. Our motivated teachers encourage children to explore, discover and experiment to build confidence and think independently while they’re having fun and solving problems.

※Our Japanese instructors basically do not speak English, therefore, very simple commands in English can be used for communication.

All Kiroro Kids Ski and Snowboard Lesson prices include lift passes and helmets, tax and accident insurance during the lesson time.

※Rental equipment is not included except a helmet.

Kiroro Kids Levels

※26-27 lesson calendar

 
Ski 3-12 Year Old
Lesson	Lesson Details
Pink	Classes for 3 and 4-year-old children. Get used to the snow and discover the joy of skiing by having fun at our Factory area. 
Green (for 5 years old and above)	Classes intended for children 5 years old and above who have never skied or are new to skiing. Time to have fun whilst skiing.
Red	Classes for children who can turn on the flat slopes and stop in the V-shape. Improve your skills on the Family Course.
Silver	Classes for children who can make stable turns on the flat slopes. Let’s practice parallel turns on the “Ridge” and beginners courses.
Purple	Classes for children who can do stable turns on the intermediate courses. The aim is to make a good balanced parallel turns and perfect horizontal skiing.
Gold	Classes for children who can make balanced and stabilized turns on the intermediate course.　Let’s make it a little bit faster!
Platinum	Classes for children who can make stable turns gaining speed on the intermediate courses. They will know the difference between short and long turns and practice skiing on the various steep slopes.
Diamond	Classes for children who can stably ski on the advanced ski slopes. You will have pole training and learn how to ski on every type of slope and every type of snow.
Snowboard 7-12 Year Old
Lesson	Lesson Details
First	Classes for the first timers and children who have not learned how to turn yet. You will learn basic rules through play.
Fun	Classes for children who can turn on the flat slopes. Practice on the Family course will strengthen their balance.
Free	Classes for children who can turn in control on intermediate slopes. Practicing easy tricks and faster turning skills.
Expert	Classes for children who can turn on the intermediate courses fast and smooth. Speed! Balance! Tricks! We aim for a professional level.
Kiroro Kids Academy Group Lesson Price

We will take care of your children for the whole day or a half day. While playing on the slopes children will make progress with our original and entertaining tutorial methods.
