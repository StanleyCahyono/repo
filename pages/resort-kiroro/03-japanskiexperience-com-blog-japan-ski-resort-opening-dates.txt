Japan Ski Resort Opening Dates 2026-27
By Stu Buckland
|
Updated 20 August 2026

Japan ski resort opening dates are being released for the 2026-27 winter season. We will update this page as new information becomes available

Japan Ski Resort Opening Dates 2026–27
Resort Area	Planned Opening Date	Planned Closing Date
Niseko United	28 November 2026	5 May 2027
Rusutsu Resort	28 November 2026	30 March 2027
Furano Ski Resort	28 November 2026	TBA
Hakuba Happo One	TBA	TBA

 

Niseko United will open all four ski areas on 28th November 2026. Annupuri and Grand Hirafu will remain open until 5th May 2027, and Niseko Village and Hanazono will close on the 4th and 11th April. Niseko’s popular night skiing will operate from 12th December 2026 until 22 March 2027 across all resorts, giving visitors the chance to ski under floodlit slopes well into the evening.

Rusutsu Resort is scheduled to open on 28th November 2026 until 30th March 2027.

Furano Ski Resort is scheduled to open on 28th November 2026, closing date TBA.

Hakuba opening dates for the 2026/27 ski season have not yet been announced..

All Japan ski resort opening dates and lift operations depend on weather and snow conditions.
