RFID

LEARN MORE ABOUT PK2’S NEW LIFT TICKET SYSTEM

Peek’n Peak Resort introduced an all-new access card containing an RFID chip during the  2024/25 season to replace paper tickets. Your Season Pass or Lift Ticket is loaded onto a card containing RFID (Radio Frequency Identification) microchip technology. With RFID cards, you are now able to make ticket purchases online. If it is your first time visiting us and you have already purchased tickets online and filled out your waiver, head directly to one of the pick-up kiosks (located at our ticket windows) when you arrive at the Resort. Now you can save time without waiting in line for ticket sales! Once you have an RFID card, you can reload additional Lift or Tubing tickets onto your card through your online account.

GOLF AT THE PEAK
PLAY WHERE THE PROS PLAY!

The Upper Course at Peek’n Peak has been a regular tour stop for the PGA Tour’s development league, the Korn Ferry Tour, for several years. Enjoy rolling hills, deep bunkers, and unique holes cut atop the slopes of Peek’n Peak. You’ll be greeted by stunning, photo-worthy views. Whether you’re a scratch handicapper looking to take on a challenge from the gold tees, which measure over 7,000 yards, or a 20 handicapper playing from the more manageable white tees at 5,600 yards, the course offers a playing experience that you’ll never forget. We can’t wait to see you swing your driver at the top of the Peak!
