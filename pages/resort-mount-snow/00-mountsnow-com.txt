Your Green Mountain Getaway

Located in southern Vermont, Mount Snow is the most accessible Green Mountain getaway from southern metropolitan areas. Found on Route 100, the resort is just 2.5 hours from Boston and 4 hours from New York City. Spend less driving, and more time on the mountain!
