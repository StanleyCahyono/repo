A lift pass for every type of skier or snowboarder in Méribel

Are you looking to ski in Méribel but unsure about the 3 Vallées pass? Unsure which pass duration to choose? Whether you’re coming with family or friends, a beginner or an expert skier, staying in the mountains for a weekend or a full week, we offer several options tailored to your real needs.

The Méribel ski pass to discover an easy-to-navigate ski area

The Méribel ski pass lets you ski freely on all Méribel slopes, including Méribel-Mottaret. Enjoy an authentic experience and the wide variety of landscapes across more than 70 high-altitude runs. You’re sure to have unforgettable moments on your skis!

The weekly pass (6 days) to save on lifts

Get a weekly ski pass and save with our great deals, such as family-friendly passes (Family Flex). Save valuable time hitting the slopes—no need to queue at the ticket office every morning. Enjoy both time and money savings with Méribel’s best ski pass rates!
