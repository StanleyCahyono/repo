Feel at home in a holiday apartment in Méribel

A family resort renowned for its warm and friendly atmosphere, Méribel has many attractions: its altitude (between 1,100 and 2,950 metres), the quality of its environment and its varied activities. Would you like to stay in a cosy place, just like at home, but in the Méribel valley? Try the experience of a private home! Each rental, imbued with a mountain atmosphere, is dedicated to welcoming you as it should be. Homemade fig jam, a hand-embroidered tea towel, a small dish concocted by the hostess, anecdotes about life in the valley, a little history…. It is the little details that make the difference.

The private renters offer you apartment or chalet rentals in Méribel, in the heart of the 3 Valleys. In the evening, after a day of skiing in the Méribel ski resort or after a day of hiking in the summer, meet your friends and family for dinner in complete privacy.

From the cocooning studio for 2 people to the large luxury serviced chalet: enjoy a stay in the Méribel valley, with all the comforts of home…. This is the unique advantage of renting directly from the owner!

Book your ski rental accommodation directly with a private owner

Méribel Tourisme will help you find the accommodation in the ski resort or in the mountains in summer that suits you best. Thanks to its selection of “visited and approved” offers in the whole Méribel valley, easily compare the offers of individuals among a large choice of flats, chalets and gites. By booking your flat rental in Méribel on our website, benefit from a discount on your ski pass thanks to the Happy Ski offer.
