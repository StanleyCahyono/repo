Skiing & Snowboarding in Zillertal
Skiing fun in the ski areas of: AHORN, PENKEN & HORBERG

If you have chosen the Mayrhofen-Hippach region in Tyrol for your skiing holiday, an astonishing 142 kilometres of impeccably groomed pistes and 61 state-of-the-art lifts await.

The AHORN and PENKEN ski areas offer endless opportunities to enjoy winter to the fullest. Mount Ahorn is a natural haven for leisure skiers, beginners and families alike, with its wide, sun-drenched carving slopes and the popular FunRide arena. Mount Penken, meanwhile, draws action lovers to its slopes with highlights such as the legendary Harakiri and the PenkenPark.

PENKEN

The expansive Penken ski area, including the Finkenberg, Rastkogel and Eggalm ski areas, boasts an exceptionally diverse array of pistes. For the ultimate test of skill, the legendary Harakiri Piste awaits with a dizzying 78% gradient. Meanwhile, the world-class PenkenPark offers tailored features and lines for snowboarders and freeskiers of all abilities, from rookies to seasoned pros.

Questions about skiing & snowboarding in Mayrhofen

What is the difference between the Ahorn and Penken ski areas?
The Ahorn is renowned for its wide, sun-drenched slopes, making it perfect for beginners, leisure skiers, and families, whilst still offering a challenging valley descent for expert skiers. In contrast, the Penken provides a vast, interconnected domain packed with sporty highlights such as the ultra-steep Harakiri run and the PenkenPark.

What does Mayrhofen have to offer snowboarders?
Snowboarders can head straight to the PenkenPark, which features bespoke areas designed for every skill level, from rookie to pro. For those looking to learn the sport or hone techniques, the local ski and snowboard schools across Mayrhofen-Hippach provide expert, professional tuition.

Which ski pass best suits my winter holiday in Mayrhofen?
The right choice depends on how many days you want to ski and which ski areas you’d like to visit. Before booking, compare the validity period and the areas covered by the available day and multi-day passes.

What can non-skiing companions do on the Ahorn and Penken?
Even without skis or a snowboard, visitors can immerse themselves in the alpine winter scenery via beautifully groomed winter walking trails and sun terraces boasting panoramic mountain vistas. On the Ahorn, unique experiences such as the PistenBock toboggan ride and the iconic WhiteLounge snow bar add a delightful touch to any mountain excursion.
