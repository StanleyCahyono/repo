Mayrhofen Ultraks FanTicket

Experience the 8th edition of Mayrhofen Ultraks on 5 September 2026 up close at the Ultraks FanZone powered by Mountopolis, right by the Penkenbahn mountain station. Get your Mayrhofen Ultraks FanTicket now and be there when the athletes perform at their best on the mountain.

The Ultraks race series brings the trend sport of trail running to the most beautiful event locations and along the most spectacular mountain peaks in the Alps. Would you like to get to the best trail running viewpoints with ease? Then grab your Mayrhofen Ultraks FanTicket and use the Penkenbahn to follow the action on the mountain live at the Ultraks FanZone powered by Mountopolis directly at the Penkenbahn mountain station.

DJ sounds and culinary delights await you at the Ultraks FanZone powered by Mountopolis while you watch the thrilling race.

Provisional timetable
from 9.30 am: Start of the Ultraks FanZone powered by Mountopolis, music and moderation by DJ Albin, arrival of the best athletes "MUZ30"
from 10.30 am: Finish line of the best athletes "Mountopolis Vertical"
from 11.50 am: Arrival of the best athletes "TUX70"
from 12.00 am: Arrival of the best athletes "RK50"
👉 MOUNTOPOLIS COMPETITION right at the FanZone!
THE ROUTES WITH THE COURSE ON THE PENKEN:

"MOUNTOPOLIS VERTICAL": Distance 8.5 km, 1,250 vertical metres - short and crisp.

"MUZ30": Distance 30 km, 2,000 vertical metres - Part of the Golden Trail National Series supported by Salomon and also the most popular route of the Mayrhofen Ultraks.

"RK50": Distance 50 km, 3,200 metres in altitude - A trail delicacy that leads up to the most beautiful panoramic mountain in the Zillertal - the Rastkogel.

"TUX070": Distance 70 km, 4,000 metres in altitude.

Here you can find an overview of all the routes at the Mayrhofen Ultraks.
