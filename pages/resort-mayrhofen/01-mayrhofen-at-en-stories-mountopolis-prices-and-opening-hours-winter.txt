Please note: The lifts in the ski resort in the ski season are generally open from 8.00 am until 4.00 pm; Last ascent of the cable cars at 4.30 pm; Last ascent Möslbahn and 6SB Schneekar (Return Möslbahn) at 4.00 pm. Due to the large scale of the ski resort, make sure you return to your gondola station in time.
However, this is subject to weather conditions (particularly after snow fall) and can be postponed. All slopes of the Mayrhofner Bergbahnen are closed outside of the operating times due to slope preparation for safety reasons!

OPENING TIMES CASH DESKS
Our cash desks are daily open from 8.00 am to 5.00 pm

Tip: Ski passes can also be purchased round-the-clock at our ticketmachines located at the Ahornbahn, Penkenbahn and Horbergbahn as well as via mayrhofen.at/tickets.

Key cards: To offer you a comfortable and stress-free skiing and snowboarding day, every lift ticket has been programmed to a chip card so that you can enter all our lifts without presentation of your ticket. You have to pay a deposit of € 2.00 which will be fully returned when giving back your undamaged chip card to one of our return counters. You can pay with cash card, VISA and Mastercard at all of our ticket counters. For information about special prices for groups and our terms, please contact us.

Reductions: Please note that you have to show your ID! Children: Children born after 01.01.2021, can travel free with an accompanying adult and adhere to the terms of transport. We offer discounts on certain tickets to persons with a disability rating of at least 60%. These tickets are only available from our ticket offices. Please note: Dogs must be kept on a leash and muzzled.

Information pursuant to § 24 DSG 2000 regarding “PhotoCompare”: Please note that in order to control access to the lifts, a photo of each lift pass holder will be taken every time they pass through one of the turnstiles that are equipped with a camera. The first such photo of a lift pass holder, considered the “reference photo,” will be compared by the lift staff to any subsequent photos of the lift pass holder that are taken in the same manner. As soon as the lift pass expires, the reference photo will be deleted; all other photos will be deleted no more than 30 minutes after a lift pass holder passes through a turnstile that is equipped with a camera. Because some lift passes are not compatible with the PhotoCompare technology, some lift pass holders may pass through a turnstile without having their picture taken. Therefore, please be advised that the lift staff will be carrying out spot checks.
