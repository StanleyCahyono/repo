Tickets and prices for summer use of the Mayrhofner Bergbahnen include single ascent and descent rides, Flexi Tickets, special experience offers such as SunRise and SunSet Gondolas, as well as combination tickets for families. For adventure seekers, there are also action tickets for bikers and paragliders, plus access to attractions such as the Eagle's Stage Ahorn and the FunSportStation Penken.

Important Notes and Information
Chip card, deposit fee, discounts and general benefits for your stay.
Chip Card and Deposit Fee
A deposit fee of € 2.00 is charged per chip card. The chip card grants you access to all operating cable cars without the hassle of inserting your ticket. You will receive the deposit fully refunded when you return the undamaged card at one of our return points (recognisable by the symbol).
Penkenbahn incl. Kombibahn Penken
Riding the Penkenbahn includes the Kombibahn Penken.
Discounts and Rates
ID required: In all cases, presentation of an ID is mandatory.
Groups and packages: Excursion packages and rates for groups of 10 or more persons available upon request at vertrieb@mayrhofner-bergbahnen.com.
Children born in 2020 or later: Children born after 01.01.2020 ride free of charge in accordance with our transport conditions and when accompanied by an adult. For children born after 01.01.2011, the children's rate applies.
Disability of at least 60 %: For a degree of disability of at least 60 %, discounts are offered on selected ticket types. These tickets are only available at our cash desks.
Dogs on the cable car
Leash and muzzle required for all dogs. No dogs allowed at the Eagle's Stage Ahorn during the show.
