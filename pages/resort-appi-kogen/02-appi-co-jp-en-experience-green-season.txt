THE GREEN SEASON

Discover the renowned nature of the Appi Highlands. Hike through forest trails, cycle across mountain landscapes, or enjoy a relaxing round of golf at the nearby courses.

SPRING TO AUTUMN SEASON

During the spring and autumn seasons, the picturesque Appi Highlands invite you to immerse yourself in the embrace of nature. Ride the gondola to take in breathtaking mountain views, or enjoy a peaceful hike through lush forests, breathing in the fresh air. You can also experience luxury camping under the starry sky for an unforgettable night, or spend quality time with your loved ones playing tennis in the refreshing outdoors. For dog lovers, the Appi Highlands’ dog park is the perfect place to share joyful moments with your pets. Whatever experience you choose, nature here offers romance and tranquility.

Appi Kogen Beech Forest Exploration Tour

Enjoy and learn about the wonderful nature of Appi Kogen!
The beech forest spreading across Appi Kogen is full of charm throughout the four seasons.
Our guide will introduce you to the pastures that once served as grazing lands and the second-growth beech forests selected among Japan's Top 100 Forest Bathing Locations, sharing stories of Appi Kogen's past and present.
From June to July, the beech forests and their surroundings come alive with diverse flora and fauna. Visitors can encounter the Ezoharuzemi cicada, the mysterious ghost plant, and vibrant meadow blooms such as the Japanese azalea and lily of the valley.

From Saturday, April 25, 2026 to Tuesday, November 3, 2026 (National Holiday) *Some dates are subject to exclusion

Heike and Genji Firefly Night Tour

The Appi Kogen area is home to three species of fireflies—Hime fireflies, Heike fireflies, and Genji fireflies.
From early July to mid-August, let’s go see the enchanting dance of light created by these three types of fireflies during this short season.
