APPI RESORT

Nestled at the foot of Maemori Mountain, APPI Resort is the perfect year-round destination for both relaxation and adventure. With over 20 slopes and 43km of skiable terrain, we are the largest Ski Resort in Japan’s Tohoku region and a top spot for winter sports. Named Japan’s Best Ski Resort for 2022 & 2023, APPI Resort combines fantastic skiing with great accommodations and dining. Whether you prefer a luxurious stay at the Michelin Key-awarded ANA InterContinental Appi Kogen Resort or a convenient Ski In & Out experience at the ANA Crowne Plaza Resort Appi Kogen, we have the right options for all – solo, group, or family travels.

Come for the skiing, stay for the experience—APPI Resort is waiting for you!
