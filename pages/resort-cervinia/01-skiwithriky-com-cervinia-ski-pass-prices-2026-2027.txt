Cervinia Ski Pass Prices 2026/27: Complete Guide to Lift Passes, Costs and the Best Choice for Your Ski Holiday

In this guide you’ll find the official 2026/2027 ski pass prices, the differences between the Italian and International ski pass, available discounts, and my personal advice as a local ski instructor to help you choose the best option for your holiday.

Breuil-Cervinia is one of the most famous ski resorts in the Alps, thanks to its incredible altitude, reliable snow conditions and the unique possibility of skiing all the way to Switzerland through the connection with Zermatt.

Whether you are a beginner looking for easy slopes, an intermediate skier wanting kilometres of perfectly prepared pistes, or an expert searching for challenging terrain and freeride opportunities, choosing the correct lift pass can make a big difference.

For the 2026/2027 winter season, Cervinia will officially open from:

24 October 2026 until 2 May 2027

The ski area offers different options:

Cervinia & Valtournenche ski pass
International Cervinia-Zermatt ski pass
Multi-day passes
Seasonal passes
Explore Pass for sightseeing
Freedom Pass for selected rides
Ikon Ski Pass

In this complete guide, I will explain all prices, the differences between the available ski passes and which option is the best depending on your type of holiday.

Which Ski Pass Should You Choose?
Choose the Cervinia & Valtournenche Ski Pass if:
you’re a beginner
you’re travelling with children
you prefer wide Italian slopes
you want to spend less
Choose the International Ski Pass if:
it’s your first time in Cervinia
you are intermediate/advance skier
you enjoy exploring large ski area
you don’t mind paying a little extra
Cervinia Ski Pass 2026/2027: Cervinia and Valtournenche Area

The first option is the local ski pass, covering:

Breuil-Cervinia
Valtournenche

This is the perfect choice for skiers who want to enjoy the Italian side of the Matterhorn without crossing into Switzerland.

The Cervinia-Valtournenche ski area offers more than enough terrain for several days of skiing, with long cruising slopes, excellent beginner areas and challenging pistes for advanced skiers.

A great advantage is that:

From 3 consecutive days of validity, the ski pass can also be used in another ski resort in the Aosta Valley during the purchased period.

These are not additional ski days, but included possibilities within your existing pass.

What Are the Differences Between Adult, Senior, Young and Junior Ski Passes?

Cervinia applies different prices depending on age categories.

Adult Ski Pass

The standard category applies to most skiers.

If you are travelling without age-based discounts, this will be your reference price.

Senior Ski Pass

Senior discounts are available for older skiers.

The exact age requirements can change depending on the season, so it is always recommended to check the official Cervinia website before purchasing.

Senior Tariffs for adults born before 31/05/1962 upon presentation of an identity document.

Young Ski Pass

The Young category is designed for teenagers and young adults.

It offers a reduced price compared to the adult ticket and is particularly convenient for families travelling with older children.

Young Tariffs for children born after 31/10/2002 upon presentation of an identity document.

Junior Ski Pass

The Junior category is dedicated to younger children.

Families with kids should always check the available reductions because the difference compared to an adult ticket can be significant over a full week.

Junior Tariffs for children born after 31/10/2010 upon presentation of an identity document.

Cervinia-Zermatt International Ski Pass 2026/27

For many visitors, the real attraction of Cervinia is the possibility of skiing across the border into Switzerland.

The Cervinia-Zermatt ski area is one of the most spectacular ski destinations in the world, offering:

Over 300 km of slopes
Skiing between Italy and Switzerland
Incredible views of the Matterhorn
High-altitude skiing above 3,000 metres
One of the longest ski seasons in Europe

The international pass allows access to:

Breuil-Cervinia
Valtournenche
Zermatt

This is the best choice for skiers who want the complete Matterhorn experience.
