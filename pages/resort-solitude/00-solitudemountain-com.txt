September 16, 2026
AMBER’S UPDATES: FINAL WEEKS OF FALL

Fall has arrived in Big Cottonwood Canyon. Crisp mornings, comfortable afternoons, and changing aspens are making September one of the best times to visit Solitude. And with cooler temperatures settling in, winter is starting to feel close. Snowmaking Improvements One of Solitude’s biggest summer projects is taking shape along Main Street, where crews have been making significant upgrades to the resort’s snowmaking system. The work builds on last summer’s new pipeline, which brought additional gravity-fed pressure to the system. This summer, crews are upgrading lines and valves, improving valve houses, and adding both fixed and portable snow guns. Three new fixed guns have already been installed along Main Street. Together, these improvements will help Solitude make better use of snowmaking windows and strengthen early-season coverage. State of Solitude State of Solitude returns Friday, September 25, at Moonbeam Lodge. The annual event is an opportunity to hear from Solitude leadership about what’s been happening across the mountain, the projects completed this summer, and what guests can expect this winter. Those who can’t make it up the canyon can tune in virtually. Solitude Bike Park Whip Off & Big Cottonwood Winter Welcome Saturday, September 26, brings a full afternoon and evening of biking, community, and getting excited for winter. Solitude Bike Park Whip Off will give riders another chance to send it before bike season comes to an end. The action takes place at the final jump on Pyrite Plunge, with the Moonbeam base area serving as the hub for the event. After the Whip Off, the celebration continues with Big Cottonwood Winter Welcome at Moonbeam. Expect live music, food and drinks, an outdoor movie, and plenty of excitement for the winter ahead. Make the Most of Fall There’s still time to enjoy fall on the mountain. Lift-served operations are now running Friday – Sunday and will wrap up on the first Sunday in October. Cooler temperatures and recent rain have created prime conditions for hiking and biking, while fall colors are beginning to fill Big Cottonwood Canyon. St. Bernard’s Dinner & Wine Pairing Series also continues into fall. Sunday evening dinners feature a five-course menu with thoughtfully selected wine pairings and offer another way to spend an autumn evening at Solitude. Winter Hiring Is Underway Solitude is actively hiring for Winter 26/27, with full-time and part-time opportunities available across the resort. Those looking for another way to spend the season on the mountain can also explore opportunities to become a volunteer mountain host. Open positions and additional information are available on Solitude’s website. Winter Is Getting Close With snowmaking improvements nearing completion, fall operations winding down, and winter hiring underway, preparations for the season ahead are in full swing. Solitude has announced its planned opening date, conditions permitting, but the team will be ready to open earlier if weather and snowmaking conditions allow. There’s still plenty of fall left to enjoy before then. Come up for the changing leaves, get in a few final bike laps, join the community for State of Solitude and Big Cottonwood Winter Welcome, and start counting down the weeks until ski season. August 7, 2026
AMBER’S UPDATES: FALL LIFT OPERATIONS

Happy August! While it’s hard to believe summer is already winding down, there’s still plenty of time to enjoy everything happening at Solitude. Thanks to a healthy monsoon season, Big Cottonwood Canyon is as green as it’s been in years, making it a great time to get outside before we start looking ahead to winter. Fall Lift Hours As we move into late summer, our lift operations will begin transitioning to our fall schedule. Through Sunday, August 17, the bike park and lifts will remain open daily. Beginning Monday, August 18, we’ll operate Thursday through Sunday, with Twilight Nights continuing on Thursdays from 10:00 a.m. – 8:00 p.m. and regular operations Friday through Sunday from 10:00 a.m. – 6:00 p.m. After Labor Day, we’ll transition to our fall schedule, operating Friday through Sunday through our planned closing day on October 4. Ikon Pass Mountain Credits If you’re an Ikon Pass holder who selected Mountain Credits as your renewal benefit, don’t forget to use them before they expire at the end of each month. Ikon Pass holders receive $25 in Mountain Credits each month, while Ikon Base Pass holders receive $10. Credits can be redeemed at most retail and food and beverage locations at Solitude through the Ikon Pass app. Summer Events Our summer events calendar is still in full swing throughout August. Sunday Night Concerts, Saturday Morning Yoga, and Thursday Sunset Cinema will continue for the next several weeks before movies wrap up near the end of the month. Yoga and live music will continue into September. Looking ahead, our next signature event is the Winter Welcome Party and Mountain Bike Whip-Off on Saturday, September 26. It’s one of the highlights of the fall and a great way to celebrate the transition from bike season to ski season. Around the same time, we’ll also host our annual State of Solitude, where we’ll share updates on this summer’s mountain improvements, preview new snowmaking investments, and provide a look at what’s ahead for Winter 26/27. We’ll announce the final date soon. We’re also excited about what’s coming this winter. Along with enhanced Solidaze rewards for Ikon Pass holders, we’re introducing additional value-focused offerings designed to make skiing and riding at Solitude more accessible for locals and visiting families. Our target opening date for Winter 26/27 is November 20, conditions permitting. As always, we’ll open as soon as conditions allow. Thank you to everyone who has visited Solitude this summer. It’s been one of our strongest summer seasons yet, and we’re grateful to everyone who has helped make it such a success. We look forward to finishing the season strong and welcoming everyone back this winter. July 13, 2026
AMBER’S UPDATES: WILDFIRE SEASON & PRO DOWNHILL SERIES

Summer is in full swing at Solitude, and while it’s been another incredible season on the mountain, it’s also been one that has kept wildfire top of mind across Utah and the West. Our hearts go out to everyone who has been impacted, especially our friends at Eagle Point Resort and those continuing to deal with active fires. We’re also incredibly grateful to the firefighters and first responders working tirelessly to protect our communities, including several Solitude team members who are serving this summer. Thank you for everything you do. As a precaution, Solitude is now operating under Stage 2 fire restrictions. For guests, the biggest change is that smoking is only permitted inside personal vehicles or in parking lots. Behind the scenes, we’ve also partnered with the U.S. Forest Service and Unified Fire Authority to provide wildfire awareness training for all team members as we continue to prioritize preparedness and safety. The mountain has remained remarkably green thanks to recent afternoon monsoons. Wildflower season is in full bloom, and with the Wasatch Wildflower Festival underway, now is one of the best times of year to visit. The trails are vibrant, the flowers are spectacular, and temperatures are typically about 20 degrees cooler than the valley. Next week, Solitude will once again host the Pro Downhill Series, a UCI World Cup qualifier that brings some of the world’s best downhill mountain bikers to the Wasatch. Racing begins with athlete check-in and practice midweek before the action ramps up throughout the weekend. The Moonbeam base area will transform into a festival atmosphere with vendors, big screens, food and drinks. There will also be designated viewing areas where spectators can catch the athletes in action. Alongside race weekend, our lineup of free summer events continues. Coca-Cola Sunset Cinema continues every Thursday evening at Moonbeam. Perfect for gathering with the crew under the stars to watch free movies beginning at dusk. Saturday mornings feature outdoor yoga in Solitude Village, and live music fills the Village every Sunday evening. If you’re looking to escape the summer heat, there’s no better place to spend a day. Ikon Pass holders also have plenty of reasons to visit this summer. Every 26/27 Ikon Pass includes one complimentary bike park day ticket that can be used at Solitude. Full Ikon Pass holders who selected Mountain Credits as their renewal benefit also receive $25 in monthly credits, while Ikon Base Pass holders receive $10 each month. Credits can be used throughout the month at Solitude. They can be used for retail purchases, rentals, Little Dollies, and Stone Haus, with additional redemption locations planned in the future. Mountain Credits expire at the end of each month, so be sure to use them before they’re gone. Looking ahead, our next signature summer event is Summer Fest during Homeowners Weekend in early August. It’s one of our favorite weekends of the season, bringing together live music, games, family activities, and great food as we celebrate another memorable summer. It’s already been an incredible season thanks to our hardworking team members who continue to bring the mountain to life every day. We’ve also been making great progress on several behind-the-scenes projects, including snowmaking improvements that will enhance the winter experience. Believe it or not, we’re also approaching the 100-day countdown to opening day. Winter will be here before we know it.
