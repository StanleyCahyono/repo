SOLITUDE SEASON PASS
Unlimited Access
No Blackout Dates

Ski or ride Solitude as much as you like with unlimited access and no blackout dates during winter 26/27 season.

15 Friends & Family Tickets
For 25% Off The Window Rate At Solitude

Discounted friends and family lift tickets: 25% off Adult, Senior, or Child window price. Only redeemable on dates pass is valid. Friends & Family tickets must be redeemed online 24-hours prior to visit. Child and 6 & under pass holders do not get Friends & Family benefit.

Exclusive Discounts
15% Off Retail And Select Dining Options

15% discount at Canyon Fever retail locations and select dining options, including Last Chance, Roundhouse, Stone Haus, and more. Child and 6 & under pass holders do not get discounts/other benefits.

Free UTA Ski Bus Access
Store Your Skis And Simplify Your UTA Ski Bus Ride

Simplify your UTA Ski Bus ride by leaving your gear with us. We'll store 1 pair of skis or 1 snowboard per guest, free for Ikon Pass and Solitude season pass holders. Limited to 7 consecutive days. Visit Solitude's website for more details on pass holder benefits.
