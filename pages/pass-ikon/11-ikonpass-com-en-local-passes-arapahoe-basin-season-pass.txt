ARAPAHOE BASIN SEASON PASS
Unlimited Access
No Blackout Dates

Ski or ride Colorado’s longest winter (typically October to June) with unlimited access and no blackout dates during the 26/27 winter season.

Legendary Benefits

A-Basin Season Pass Holders receive benefits offered by no other mountain. Why? Because A-Basin Season Pass Holders get the Legend, so the Legend gets them.

Buy Now, Ride Now: 26/27 A-Basin holders can start using their pass on April 6, 2026.

Unlimited Friends & Family Tickets
25% Off The Daily Window Rate At A-Basin

Discounted Friends & Family lift tickets: 25% off Senior, Adult, Young Adult, or Child window price. Tickets available for purchase online in advance and onsite.

Exclusive Discounts
15% Off Retail, Rental, Lessons, And Dining

Discounts valid in the Base N’ Edge Tune & Rental shop, Arapahoe Sports, Legends Cafe, the 6th Alley Bar & Grill, and Black Mountain Lodge. Alcohol and events not included. Valid for the active passholder to use in a group lesson.

Summer Benefits
A Year-Round Adventure

Unlimited free scenic lift access, 25% off Via Ferrata Guided Tours, and 2-for-1 Aerial Adventure Park access.

Partner Resort Access

To be used throughout the 27/26 winter season: 2 days at Silverton (unguided), one free heli-drop with booking of full guided tour (blackout dates apply). 3 days at Monarch with no blackouts.

Discounted Uphill Pass

Add one for just $49. The discounted add-on will go on sale in the coming weeks.

Easy Access
One Of The Closest Resorts To Denver With Multiple Public Transit Options

All vehicles will need to create a parking reservation on weekends during peak season. Need a ride? Check out TreadShare to be connected to like-minded skiers heading to A-Basin. The Summit Stage offers free transit all through Summit County and services A-Basin daily. Coming up from the Front Range? Check out Snowstang to sit back, relax, and enjoy the ride.
