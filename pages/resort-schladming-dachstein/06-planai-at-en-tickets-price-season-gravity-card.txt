Season pass bike
full driving pleasure all summer long

With the bike season pass, you can enjoy unlimited access to the Bikepark Schladming and the Reiteralm Trails throughout the summer season. Whether you enjoy riding uphill on the Uphill Flow Trail, flowing descents or downhill action, with just one ticket you can be flexible and experience the trails of both bike parks as often as you like.

Gravity Card Schladming
1 ticket - 30 bike parks - 7 countries

Experience biking fun in Austria, Germany, Switzerland, the Czech Republic, Slovenia, Italy and Poland with just one season ticket.

No more queuing at the ticket office and you can start biking straight away at no extra cost. Now all that remains is the agony of choice as to which bike park to explore first.

Valid from April 5 to November 9, 2025
