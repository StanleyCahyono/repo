Hiking on the Planai & Hochwurzen
There's something going on on the Planai all year round. With your season ticket for the summer, you'll never be bored.

You can use the hiking season ticket for your hike on the Planai & Hochwurzen as well as for a trip to Hopsiland. This ticket also includes the use of the Planai & Hochwurzen buses and the Schladming city buses.

These season tickets do not include the transportation of sports equipment such as bicycles or paragliders!
