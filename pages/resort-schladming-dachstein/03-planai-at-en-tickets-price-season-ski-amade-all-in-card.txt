Ski amadé - ALL-IN Card
ALL-IN Card White

Valid from 11 October 2025 to 3 May 2026

One ticket, endless possibilities – that's what the Ski amadé ALL-IN Card White offers you on the Planai, the Hochwurzen and throughout the entire Ski amadé ski association. Experience unlimited fun on perfectly groomed slopes, modern cable car facilities and a wide range of activities in the heart of the Schladming mountains.

The ALL-IN Card White is your key to Austria's greatest skiing pleasure and guarantees you access to all open facilities of the participating mountain railway companies from the start of the season to the end.

Validity

The card cannot be used before 11 October 2025 or after 3 May 2026. During the period of validity, cardholders are entitled to use all cable cars and lifts in operation during the respective opening hours. The general tariff conditions apply.

ALL-IN Card Gold

Valid from 11 October 2025 to 9 October 2026

With the ALL-IN Card Gold, you can enjoy unlimited mountain fun on the Planai, the Hochwurzen and throughout the Ski amadé ski association for a whole year. In winter, pure skiing pleasure awaits you on the legendary 4-mountain ski area, and in summer and autumn, countless sporting adventures open up for you in the breathtaking Schladming mountains.

The ALL-IN Card Gold is your key to 12 months of mountain and nature experiences. It gives you access to all open mountain railways throughout the Ski amadé area for unforgettable moments, whatever the season.

Validity

The card is valid from 11 October 2025 to 9 October 2026. During the validity period, cardholders are entitled to use all cable cars and lifts in operation during the respective opening hours. The general tariff conditions apply.

General ticket information
The ticket is personal and non-transferable.
No replacement for forgotten or lost tickets.
Multi-day tickets are only valid on consecutive days.
Advance sale and use of multi-day tickets is also possible from 3 p.m. on the day before.
Information about additional ski pass offers (e.g. hourly tickets) is available at the lift ticket offices.
Information on special rates (groups of 20 people or more, people with disabilities from 70%, etc.) is available on request!
In the early and late season and when there is insufficient snow, the range of lifts and pistes is limited.
The current (daily) availability can be found at the respective ticket offices, on the electronic panorama boards, on the respective information channels of the ski area and at the respective lifts as well as on the Internet.
The FIS safety rules must be observed.
A special evening ticket is required for tobogganing in the evening and night skiing.
The member companies of the respective ticket associations operate their cable car and lift facilities as well as pistes on their own responsibility and legally independently. The specific transportation contract is concluded with the member company whose facilities and pistes are currently being used.
