RENT: SKI & BOARD
Ski rental shops in Kaprun and at the Kitzsteinhorn
Ski rental at the Kitzsteinhorn

Ski rental, ski service, fashion and accessories can be found on various levels at the Kitzsteinhorn. The shops offer a wide range of products and professional advice as well as top services.

BRÜNDL SPORTS

The Bründl Sports service network is presented with a total of nine connected service stations in Zell am See-Kaprun. There is a shop in the middle of the Kitzsteinhorn glacier skiing area at the Alpincenter (2.450). Additionally a shop is offered at the valley station of the MK Maiskogelbahn at the Kaprun Center, a modern ski depot included.

INTERSPORT OBERSCHNEIDER

Intersport Oberschneider is represented at Ski Dome II as well as the heated glacier depot directly next to the lift on Langwied (1976 m /exit station: Gletscherjet 1). In addition, there are several service points in the town center of Kaprun. Tip: Upon request, Intersport Oberschneider offers  packages which also include a ski or snowboard course.
