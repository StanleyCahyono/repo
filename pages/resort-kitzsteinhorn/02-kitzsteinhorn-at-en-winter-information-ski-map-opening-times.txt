Currently Open

Daily operating hours for the cable cars and ski lifts depend on the current weather, snow, and operational conditions.

For sightseeing visitors, the lifts to Kitzsteinhorn are open year-round.
The ascent from Langwiedboden to Alpincenter is currently operated by the Langwiedbahn (chairlift).
MK Maiskogelbahn (until Oct. 4 & Oct. 24 - Nov. 1) open daily. 

Maisi Flitzer closed for maintenance, expected to open again on Oct. 10
Planned ski season start at Kitzsteinhorn: Oct. 10
