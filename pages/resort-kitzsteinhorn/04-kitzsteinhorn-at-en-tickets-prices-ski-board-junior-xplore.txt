JUNIOR XPLORE CARD
Super-Saturday ticket for kids & teens

With the Junior XPLORE CARD, children and teens* can enjoy the three premium ski regions of Skicircus Saalbach Hinterglemm Leogang Fieberbrunn, Schmittenhöhe in Zell am See, and Kitzsteinhorn Kaprun every Saturday throughout the winter season for just €15. Up to 408 kilometers of runs, 121 cable cars and lifts, and the glacier included.

Valid every Saturday from Nov. 28, 2026, through April 3, 2027, at Skicircus and Schmittenhöhe
Valid every Saturday from Nov. 28, 2026, through May 1, 2027, at Kitzsteinhorn and Maiskogel

www.alpincard.at

* Children: born 2011–2020; youth: born 2008–2010
