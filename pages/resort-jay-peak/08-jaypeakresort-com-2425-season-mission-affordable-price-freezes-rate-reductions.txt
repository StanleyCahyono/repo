24+25 SEASON - MISSION AFFORDABLE PRICE FREEZES & RATE REDUCTIONS
Jay Peak Resort Freezes or Reduces Lift Tickets and More for 2024-25 Winter Ski and Snowboard Season

Jay Peak, Vt. – [Nov. 11, 2024] – Jay Peak Resort recently announced it will freeze or reduce its pricing on lift tickets and other sales points throughout the resort for the 2024-25 winter ski and snowboard season as part of its Mission Affordable program.

“We are committed to continuing to create a genuine, community-oriented resort where the heart of the experience lies in our employees and the atmosphere they foster,” said General Manager Steve Wright. “It’s important for folks to have access to skiing and riding and to help guests navigate the ongoing inflationary pressures, we will freeze many of our prices for this season. We hope this, along with other programs we’ll roll out during the season, support both our employees and guests alike.”

Mission Affordability - Price Locks and Deals

Jay Peak’s Mission Affordable program aims to offer as wide a variety of price freezes, discounts and promotions as possible to cover as many guests’ financial needs as possible.
These price locks and reductions include:

Lift Tickets: Price reductions and freezes on all lift ticket window rates, including the Mission Affordable 4-Pack, offering four fully transferable lift tickets for just $279 (less than $70 per ticket when purchased before December 2, 2024).
Vermonter Rates: Jay Peak will continue to offer Vermonter Lift Tickets, providing local residents with nearly $20 off a lift ticket with a valid Vermont ID.
Admissions to the Pump House Waterpark: Like lift tickets, resort officials froze daily admission rates for the 2024-2025 winter season.
FREE Ski & Ride School 4-Pack: The first 50 guests to sign up to the Jay Peak email list will win a free 4-Pack of lessons. The Ski & Ride 4-Pack is a program designed to introduce beginners to skiing and riding at Jay Peak. After completing all four lessons, skiers and snowboarders earn a free season pass.
Discounted Lodging Rates: Jay Peak offered rotating discounts across various lodging properties and time periods this past summer and will continue similar discounts this winter.
Food & Beverage: New low-priced menu items will be available at resort dining locations to help guests enjoy a tasty yet affordable dining experience.
For more information on this and other deals this season, visit jaypeakresort.com.

About Jay Peak Resort
Jay Peak Resort is a four-season destination in Northern Vermont, close to the Canadian border and about 60 miles north of Burlington. The resort features 385 acres of skiing and snowboarding terrain serviced by nine lifts including Vermont’s only aerial Tram plus a year-round indoor waterpark, an indoor ice arena, an extensive recreation center, multi-purpose athletic fields, and an 18-hole championship golf course. The resort is best known for its 356 inches of average annual snowfall compliments of what is known as “The Jay Cloud,” which is the most natural annual snowfall East of the Rocky Mountains. The resort offers a wide range of accommodations, dining options, and unique events catering to all types of visitors. jaypeakresort.com
