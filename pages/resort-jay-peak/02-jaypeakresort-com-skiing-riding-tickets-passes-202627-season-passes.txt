SEASON PASS USE POLICY

Season passes are not transferable and are only to be used by the individual named on the pass.
If you share your season pass please be aware of the following;

All season passes are scanned at the lifts and Lift Attendants verify that the user and the Season Passholder match the photo.
If they do not match, the unauthorized user will be required to purchase a full adult lift ticket.
The Season Pass will be confiscated and held at Customer Service on Tramside to be collected by the authorized Season Passholder.
The Season Pass will not be released until a $200 fine is paid.
Once the fine is paid and the Season Pass is collected, there will be a 1-week waiting period until the Season Pass can be used again.
CANADIAN GUESTS PURCHASING AT PAR

As always, Canadian guests (with valid proof of current residency like a driver's license or Medicare card) can purchase at par by cash or check (all credit card transactions are processed in US funds).

You can purchase in person at Tramside Customer Service or mail a check with this APPLICATION (must be postmarked by the relevant tier deadline date).

If you decide to purchase online, remember that all online purchases are processed in US funds.
