JAY PEAK MILITARY DISCOUNTS

Season passes are not transferable and are only to be used by the individual named on the pass. If you share your season pass please be aware of the following;

All season passes are scanned at the lifts and Lift Attendants verify that the user matches the Season Passholder photo.
If they do not match, the unauthorized user will be required to purchase a full day Adult lift ticket and pay a $200 fine.
The Season Pass will be confiscated and voided.
The individual who shared, and if applicable the individual using the season pass, will be prohibited from participating in the free Military Season Pass program again in the future. If any dependent passes were issued, they will also be voided.
Active-Duty Military
SEASON PASSES	

For the 2025+26 Ski & Ride Season, Jay Peak will be offering Season Passes to Active-Duty Members and their dependents (spouse and dependent children 18 & under) for $199+tx.

Here’s how you can take advantage of this special rate:

Active Vermont military member must be present orders (or equivalent paperwork) showing active dates at a Vermont base across Winter 2024, current military ID, and Vermont ID. We need to be able to verify that you are both stationed and living in Vermont.
Eligible dependents also need to present ID confirming familial relationship. Dependent Military IDs are best, if available.
If you’d like to verify your paperwork before heading to the mountain, please share it to customerservice@jaypeakresort.com for review.
Present your information at Tramside Guest Services to obtain passes for the season.
Please Note

Military Season Passes are not available for pick up and will be blacked out across Christmas and Presidents’ Week.

Questions?

customerservice@jaypeakresort.com
