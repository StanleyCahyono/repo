SALES  OF  LIFT  TICKETS  AND  LESSONS  LIMITED 
25 June 2021

As at 15 July: Skiers and boarders are urged to book online to avoid disappointment.

From Saturday 26 June the Northside Express chairlift will operate for sightseeing and limited snowplay. 
Buy passes online before you arrive and simply collect at the Buller Sports Horse Hill store.

From Sunday 4 July the Village and Horse Hill Snow Play Parks are open for tobogganing for as long as snow cover permits.  Check the daily status update.  There is strictly no tobogganing in resort outside the Snow Play Parks.

Melburnians are free to travel to Mt Buller under the same guidelines as for the rest of regional Victoria, without the requirement for a negative COVID test following the easing of restrictions as of Friday 25 June.  

Thank you to everyone for continuing to follow all the guidelines. Wear masks inside, scan into venues and stay at home if unwell. 

 

At Mt Buller you can book everything online in one go – accommodation, lift tickets, parking, transport, rental, lessons and more – all on mtbuller.com.au
