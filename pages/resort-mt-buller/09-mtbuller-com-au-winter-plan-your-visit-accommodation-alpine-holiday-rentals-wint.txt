ABOUT THE PLACE

Selection of Mt Buller Apartment Accommodation suitable for families and groups. We are open all year for Skiing, Biking, Targa and Resting.
Choose from a wide variety of accommodation to suit most budgets.

Alpine Holiday Rentals accommodation is non smoking and caters for families and small groups. Properties are NOT suitable for noisy or party groups.

A Bond security is required for the length of your stay
