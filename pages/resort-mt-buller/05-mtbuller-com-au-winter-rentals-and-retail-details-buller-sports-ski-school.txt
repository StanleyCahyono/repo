BULLER SPORTS SKI SCHOOL

Your ultimate destination for all your snow adventure needs right at the heart of the mountain! Experience unparalleled convenience with everything housed under one roof.

Purchase lift tickets, toboggan tickets, lessons, hire equipment, and retail, all available at Buller Sports Ski School. Say goodbye to the hassle of multiple stops and enjoy the ease of finding everything you need in one place.

Buy online and hit the slopes faster! enjoy exclusive online discounts on your purchases. Whether you're flying solo or planning a group outing, our school and group specialists are here to cater to your specific needs and ensure a seamless experience for everyone.

Switch up your gear effortlessly with our Gear Swap offer. Hire for multiple days and enjoy one free swap, allowing you to transition from skis to snowboard or vice versa with ease.

Rest assured with our 100% equipment guarantee, if you rent from one of our Buller Sports Store locations, feel secure knowing we can fix any equipment issues in one of our many locations.

Get a head start on your snow-filled day with our complimentary afternoon-before pick-up service. Collect your gear the day before and hit the slopes bright and early the next morning, ready for an unforgettable day on the mountain.

Don't forget to purchase your lift ticket ahead of time. You'll need a valid lift ticket to be able to collect your gear.
