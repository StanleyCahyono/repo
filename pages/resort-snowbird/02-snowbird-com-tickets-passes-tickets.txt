It's one of the best times of year–peak Fall. (Maybe second only to one of those bottomless powder days). The groves of aspens are yellow, the apron of Superior is orange and under the Tram tower is red. So, come up for a hike, Tram ride, lunch, remote work day, whatever floats your boat. 

Oktoberfest is here for just 2 more weekends. There are more than 50 beer varieties and endless delicious food options. Plus, entertainment galore. Enjoy the classics like Halle Entertainment and Alphorns on Hidden Peak. Then, check out this week's artists, musicians and more. Andrew Pollard will be the live artist painting a set of wings, SheJumps will be the featured non-profit, and Mitokandrea and Sun Divide will be on the Chickadee Stage.

Summer Activities operate Saturday and Sunday, 11 am to 6 pm, while the Aerial Tram remains open daily, 11 am to 7 pm. Tip for those who are good at planning ahead: throughout the fall, when you purchase your All-Day Activity Pass 7 days or more in advance, you can save up to 15%.

Winter Lift Tickets

Your ticket to the mountains. 

Go Full-Day, Half-Day or Multi-Day. Whichever way, a Snowbird lift ticket is your access to a ride up the mountain so you can enjoy Utah's best terrain on the way down. With a lift ticket, you get access to 9 lifts, 2 conveyors and The Tram, unlocking treed runs, groomers, steep skiing, powder skiing and more. Plus, when you buy your winter lift tickets online, you're stacking up discounts compared to purchasing at the Ticket Window.

Get the Best Discounts on Snowbird Lift Tickets

Snowbird's lift ticket prices fluctuate based on demand and peak dates. Want to save on your ticket? Here are some tips:

01 / Buy Early

EARLY BIRD GETS THE BEST DEALS

Purchase your tickets online, 2 or more weeks in advance, and save up to 15% off what you'd pay at the Ticket Window.

02 / Visit Off Peak

THE GETTING IS ALWAYS GOOD

Prices vary by date. Visit weekdays and early or late season for the best rates.

Holiday: Dec. 24 - Jan. 1; Jan. 16 - 17; Feb. 13 - 15
Peak: Dec. 21 - 23; Feb. 16 - 17 & Fri - Sun from Dec. 11 - April 4

03 / Book Lodging

CASH IN WITH STAY & SAVE

When you book lodging at Snowbird, you qualify for 20%-36% off tickets with Stay & Save.
