September 28, 2026 - 09:00 am

It's one of the best times of year–peak Fall. (Maybe second only to one of those bottomless powder days). The groves of aspens are yellow, the apron of Superior is orange and under the Tram tower is red. So, come up for a hike, Tram ride, lunch, remote work day, whatever floats your boat. 

Oktoberfest is here for just 2 more weekends. There are more than 50 beer varieties and endless delicious food options. Plus, entertainment galore. Enjoy the classics like Halle Entertainment and Alphorns on Hidden Peak. Then, check out this week's artists, musicians and more. Andrew Pollard will be the live artist painting a set of wings, SheJumps will be the featured non-profit, and Mitokandrea and Sun Divide will be on the Chickadee Stage.

Summer Activities operate Saturday and Sunday, 11 am to 6 pm, while the Aerial Tram remains open daily, 11 am to 7 pm. Tip for those who are good at planning ahead: throughout the fall, when you purchase your All-Day Activity Pass 7 days or more in advance, you can save up to 15%. 

 

Fall Operations Schedule-Summer Activities

Beginning October 1: Open Saturday-Sunday, 11 am - 6 pm
October 11: Last day of the season for Summer Activities

Fall Operations Schedule-Scenic Tram

Beginning September 21: 11 am - 7 pm, daily
Beginning October 5: 11 am - 6:30 pm, daily
October 11: Last day of the season for Scenic Tram Rides
