Combiticket Madrisa Delight
Culinary delights on the mountain

A great mountain experience – enjoy a culinary excursion on Madrisa! This unforgettable day promises delicious food and a panoramic view of the alpine landscape.
 

Services
Ascent and descent Madrisa
Meals at the Madrisa-Alp, the Madrisa-Hof or the Pizzeria Madrisa-Mia (food coupon)
Price from 25,00 CHF
Booking
Details

With the Madrisa Combi-Ticket, you benefit from an attractive culinary offer which combines a cable car ticket with a meal of your taste in the restaurants Madrisa-Alp, Madrisa-Hof or Pizzeria Madrisa-Mia.

Booking note

After booking, you will receive the order confirmation with attached voucher. Please show the voucher at the Madrisa ticket office to receive your cable car ticket and the consumption coupon for the restaurants.

Please note: The voucher for meals in the restaurants is only usable on the selected day (day of validity).
