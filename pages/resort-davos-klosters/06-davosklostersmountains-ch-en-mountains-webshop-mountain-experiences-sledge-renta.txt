Sledge rental Rinerhorn

The 3.5-kilometre-long sledge run is the highlight on the Rinerhorn for little and grown-ups. Numerous curves are waiting for you!
 

Services
Sledge rental for the whole day
Day ticket for the Rinerhorn (optional)
Price from 15,00 CHF
Booking
Details

After renting the sledge at the sports shop Rinerhorn, you take the gondola up to the mountain station, where the curvy descent begins! A leisurely ride together on a sledge or a speedy ride alone to the valley? Either way, the 24 curves ensure adventurous sledging fun.

Gastronomy

Our huts and mountain restaurants on the Rinerhorn are open daily for you.

Booking note

By showing your voucher, you will receive a sledge in the Sportshop at the valley station Rinerhorn.
