Combiticket Gipfel delight
Culinary delights on the mountain

A very special mountain experience - enjoy a culinary excursion on Parsenn. This unforgettable day promises delicious food and a panoramic view of the alpine landscape.
 

Services
Ascent and descent Weissfluh summit Parsenn Davos
Enjoy meals at 2,844 meters above sea level in the Weissfluhgipfel mountain restaurant (food coupon)
Price from 30,00 CHF
Booking
Booking note

With the combined ticket, adults receive a restaurant voucher worth CHF 20. Young people and children receive a voucher worth CHF 15. The voucher can be redeemed on the same day at the Berghaus Weissfluhgipfel.

Please note: The voucher for meals in the restaurant can only be redeemed on the selected day (day of validity).
