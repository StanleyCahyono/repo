DAVOS KLOSTERS 2026/27
Keith Marshall

 A stunning winter scene in Davos showcasing snow-covered mountains, ski slopes, and winter sports activities, highlighting the region's appeal for winter tourism and outdoor adventures.

Friday 13th? Unlucky for some but not for skiers. Friday 13 November is opening day for downhill skiing at Davos Klosters. The Parsenn lifts will be in operation that weekend, then daily from 20 November. Other areas will open as snow conditions dictate. The resort stays open after Easter until 11 April 2027. https://www.davos.ch/en/activities/snowsports/ski-snowboard

Davos is always among the first Alpine resorts to open for winter sports. This season, thanks to its snowfarming practice, the four-kilometre Flüelastrasse cross-country ski trail is to open on Friday 30 October. As a pioneer of the technique (since 2008), Davos collects snow during the preceding season, stores it under a layer of insulating – and sustainable – sawdust which preserves 70-80 per cent of its volume for use in the forthcoming season. https://www.davos.ch/en/activities/snowsports/cross-country-skiing/snowfarming-in-davos

This winter, the resort will unveil the new-look Hilton Garden Inn Davos where all 146 rooms and suites have been updated in a contemporary-Alpine theme, and a new restaurant – Larch & Lemon – will offer guests and visitors breakfast and dinner (kids’ menus available). Situated in the heart of the town, opposite the Davos Congress Centre, it has a fitness and wellness area with sauna and steam room, four well-equipped meeting rooms and a 260m² event space. Doubles available from CHF 225 per room per night (2 sharing B&B). Children up to 18 sharing adult’s room stay free.   www.hilton.com/en/hotels/zdvgigi-hilton-garden-inn-davos

A less conventional hotel choice, located below Parsenn’s Weissfluhoch station at 2570m, is the Iglu-Dorf Davos Klosters. Built every year from fresh snow and ice, its 30 igloos offer overnight accommodation for up to 46 guests. Facilities include a hot tub, sauna and heated Kota hut, snowshoeing and a cool ice bar and restaurant – open from 11 am to 4 pm – for hot and cold drinks, snacks and a classic cheese fondue. Bookable for dinner and torchlit descent. Open from 26 December 2026 to 4 April 2027, igloos are priced from CHF538 per night (2 sharing ½-board). https://www.davos.ch/en/experience/culinary-nightlife/mountain-restaurants/iglu-dorf-davos-klosters
