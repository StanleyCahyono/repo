SPECIAL OFFERS
FAMILY VACATION

In Courmayeur, children under 8 years old ski for free with the purchase of an adult, youth, or senior ski pass.
Attractive discounts also for young under 16 years old, who benefit of a 30% discount, and youth under 24 years old, with a 20% discount.
Children who are too young to start skiing can accompany mom and dad on the slopes and have fun with the Snow White baby club nannies, just steps from the Plan Checrouit ski lifts, cable car, and gondola terminals.
