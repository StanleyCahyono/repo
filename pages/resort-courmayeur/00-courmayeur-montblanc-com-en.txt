WINTER ACTIVITIES

Set in the Alps, against the spectacular backdrop of Mont Blanc, Courmayeur offers a wealth of exciting activities throughout the entire winter season. Let yourself be swept away by the thrills of descending alpine skiing and freeriding slopes, and discover wonders, such as the local heliskiing services, to fully experience the mountain, or the snowpark, dedicated to all stunt lovers, or the Fun Park for the little ones, and the delights offered by the Skyway amidst a breathtaking landscape. Find out more, and immerse yourself in this unforgettable experience!
