Discounts available only on presentation of documents of identity. Self-certification is not accepted.

Persons with disabilities can access the 50% discount compared to the full price of ski passes.
The discount is granted to the person concerned upon presentation at the ski area ticket offices (not available online) of the Civil Disability Certification certifying a disability equal to/exceeding 67% or, alternatively, of the European Disability Card, called Disability Card (see all information here)

ADVANTAGES

Holders of an annual, season or multi-day consecutive passes (starting from 4 days) can use the Courmayeur cable car evening service free of charge. The cable car is open every day until midnight.

INSURANCE

Purchasable simultaneously with the ticket: adult rate € 3,50 – children <8 rate € 1,50.

All tickets must be loaded onto a Keycard (hard plastic card) that you already have or can purchase for € 2,00.

Mobile Flow: daily tickets can be reloaded on your smartphone.
