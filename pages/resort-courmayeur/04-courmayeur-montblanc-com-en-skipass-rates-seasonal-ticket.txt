ADVANTAGES ANNUAL AND SEASON PASS COURMAYEUR
COURMAYEUR CABLE CAR BY NIGHT **

Holders of annual or season passes can use the Courmayeur cable car evening service free of charge. The cable car is open every day until midnight.

TWO DAYS IN LA THUILE

Pure snow and fun in the Espace San Bernardo: use your season pass to enjoy winter on the slopes, or your annual pass to experience the mountains in summer as well (excluding major events in La Thuile).

SKYWAY MONTE BIANCO

Unlimited access to the Skyway Monte Bianco only during the winter opening period of the Courmayeur Mont Blanc ski resort. Reservations required on their website.

VERBIER 4 VALLEES

Possibility of skiing 6 days in Verbier 4 Vallées during the winter season (one day per week).

PROTECTION IS BORN IN THE MOUNTAINS – DOTTOR NICOLA FARMACISTA

At high altitude, sun, wind, and cold are all part of the experience. That’s why we have chosen to include a small gesture of care and protection with your annual/season skipass: Dottor Nicola Farmacista’s Face Sunscreen. A product that combines skincare with alpine nature, featuring Edelweiss grown in Val Ferret, a plant that has learned from the mountains how to thrive in harsh environmental conditions. Protecting your skin where the sun meets the mountains.

One free evening entrance (from 5 p.m.), with one full-paying entry to the QC SPA Pré Saint Didier, on presentation of a digital coupon and annual or season pass. Offer for people over 14.
Receive a special gift with the purchase of your ski pass.

INSURANCE

All annual and season passes skiers are offered free of charge the insurance policy that includes third-party liability that has become compulsory.
The insurance, that includes also rescue service on-piste, is only valid in the event of request and intervention by the rescue services at the scene of the claim on the ski runs (more information).
