INSURANCE
SNOWCARE DAILY SKI AND SNOWBOARD INSURANCE

The skier is required to have an insurance policy that covers liability for damage or injury caused to third parties according to art. 30 D.Lgs. 40/2021.
If you do not have it, ask for the ski and snowboard insurance at our ticket offices.
Snowcare is the most popular ski and snowboard insurance in Europe, the only accident and accident insurance (RC) on skis and snowboards that you can buy for just one day.
IMPORTANT! The insurance is only valid in the event of request and intervention by the rescue services at the scene of the claim on the ski runs.

DAILY INSURANCE MULTISPORT

Mountain biking, downhill, trekking, climbing, and all mountain sports. One policy, complete coverage.
