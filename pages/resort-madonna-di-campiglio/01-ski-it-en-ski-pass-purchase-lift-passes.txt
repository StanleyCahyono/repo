DAILY UPGRADES SKIAREA MADONNA DI CAMPIGLIO

Folgarida-Marilleva and Pinzolo ski pass holders can purchase a daily upgrade to access the entire SkiArea Madonna di Campiglio which can only be purchased at the Folgarida-Marilleva, Madonna di Campiglio or Pinzolo ticket offices.

Rates: 
Adults € 41,00
Juniors € 29,00
Children free of charge (if born after 2019) only if accompanied by a paying adult and if valid ID is provided.

From 1 January 2022 third-party liability insurance is mandatory according to the law. It can be bought online or at the ticket office with your ski pass.
DO YOU PREFER TO PURCHASE YOUR SKI PASS AT THE TICKET OFFICE AT A FIXED PRICE?

Discover our fixed price list and find the nearest ticket office. You can also use the automatic cashiers to quickly purchase your lift pass without having to queue, so you can head right to the slopes.
