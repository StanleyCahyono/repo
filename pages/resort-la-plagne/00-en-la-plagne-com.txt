From snow-covered slopes to the summer trails of the Vanoise National Park, every season transforms the scenery and the possibilities. In winter, skiers and snowboarders explore a diverse ski area, while non-skiers can enjoy snowshoe walks, mountain spas and breathtaking panoramas. When the snow gives way to alpine pastures, La Plagne becomes a playground for hikers, mountain bikers, families seeking fresh air, and those curious to discover the local heritage. Here, everyone can tailor their adventure to their own pace, desires, and encounters. The first step towards a dream holiday? Book your accommodation now!

La Plagne ski resort, linked to the Les Arcs and Peisey-Vallandry ski areas, is one of the largest ski domains in France! All the ski runs for all abilities across the three combined resorts make up Paradiski, a "super domain" that certainly lives up to its name. Because yes, Paradiski is a skier's paradise for all snow sports enthusiasts: 425 km of green, blue, red and black runs that will appeal to beginners and seasoned skiers alike, and even experts (downhill skiing, freestyle, freeride…). Good to know: 70% of the Paradiski area is located at an altitude of over 2,000 metres. Snow cover on the slopes is therefore guaranteed during your holidays in La Plagne in winter as well as in spring, along with fabulous dog sledding adventures.
