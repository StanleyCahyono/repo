Ski Passes

The Paradiski link is open from 12 December 2026 to 17 April 2027, inclusive.
The Paradiski pass provides access to all lifts in La Plagne and Les Arcs/Peisey-Vallandry.

If you already have a valid La Plagne pass, you can opt for a Paradiski day upgrade, only available in the lift pass offices.

Each pass is loaded onto a Ski card (which costs €1 when purchased for the first time and is rechargeable and non-refundable).

The ski pass is valid for a period from 4 hours to 8 consecutive days.

Passes are personal, non-transferable, and issued in the holder’s name. Random checks may be carried out.

The age taken into account for your purchase is the age on the first day of the pass’s validity.

For the winter season, Season and Annual passes are valid from 123 December 2026 to 24 April 2027.
Photo required for season, annual, 1 day out of 7 and 2 days out of 7 passes.

The prices shown are net prices in euros, including VAT, excluding insurance.
