How does the Basic Pass work?

Enjoy 2 consecutive days at Soldeu, Mont Màgic Family Park (Canillo) or Pal Arinsal.

The Basic Pass is very simple to use:

First day - activities + access: you will enjoy access to the sector of your choice, along with the activities and services included in the Basic Pass.

Important: included activities depend on the sector where you use the Basic Pass on the first day.

Second day - gondola access only: includes one ascent and one descent on the gondola lift in the sector of your choice. Activities and services from the first day are not included.

Prices 2026

Basic Pass - 2 consecutive days: €23
Basic Pass Afternoon - 2 consecutive days, from 2 p.m.: €16
Basic Pass Baby up to 2 years old: free

Free access with season pass: Andorra Pass, Nord Pass, Andorra Pass Sundays, After-school, University and Companion 2025–2026. Mountain Pass and Pedestrian Pass 2025–2026: includes free access to the Tristaina gondola (1 ascent and 1 descent per day).
