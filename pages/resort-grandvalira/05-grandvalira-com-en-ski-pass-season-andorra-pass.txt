What is the Andorra Pass Season Pass?

Andorra Pass is the season ski pass that gives you access to 308 skiable kilometres in Andorra, 216 slopes and 124 lifts. You can ski in Grandvalira, Pal Arinsal and Ordino Arcalís throughout the entire winter season.

In addition, ski at international resorts in Europe, America and even Asia, and enjoy exclusive benefits both inside and outside the Grandvalira Resorts ski areas.

With Andorra Pass, live the Infinite Winter, and keep enjoying it even when summer arrives: access lifts across the country and discover a new passion on more than 60 km at the Pal Arinsal Bike Park.

Ski as much as you want, wherever you want and whenever you want with Andorra Pass.
