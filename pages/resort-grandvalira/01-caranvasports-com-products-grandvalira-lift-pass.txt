GRANDVALIRA LIFT PASS — ANDORRA, 2025/2026 SEASON

Grandvalira is the largest ski area in the Pyrenees and in southern Europe, with over 200 km of slopes across the Pas de la Casa-Grau Roig, Soldeu-El Tarter and Canillo-Encamp sectors. Here you can buy your day or full-season lift pass.

LOOKING FOR THE FULL-SEASON PASS?

The widget on this page handles day and multi-day passes with real-time availability. The full-season pass is a personalised card that requires each passenger's details (name, passport or ID, date of birth and a photo of the document) and is processed manually — get in touch and we'll arrange it.

WHAT DOES THE GRANDVALIRA LIFT PASS INCLUDE?

Access to the lifts in all three sectors of the Grandvalira ski area: Pas de la Casa-Grau Roig, Soldeu-El Tarter and Canillo-Encamp, with over 200 km of skiable slopes between 1,710 and 2,640 metres of altitude.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
HOW LONG DOES THE SEASON PASS TAKE TO PROCESS?

As it is a personalised card, the timeframe depends on validating each passenger's details. We recommend booking well in advance, especially before bank holiday weekends and school holidays.

CAN I BUY JUST ONE DAY IF I DON'T YET KNOW HOW MANY I'LL SKI?

Yes, the day pass is available for adults, juniors and children directly in the widget on this page, with immediate confirmation.

DOES THE PASS INCLUDE ORDINO ARCALÍS OR PAL ARINSAL?

The Grandvalira lift pass gives access to Grandvalira's own sectors. If you also want to ski at Ordino Arcalís or Pal Arinsal, check the combined Grandvalira Resorts rates.

GRANDVALIRA LIFT PASS PRICES
How much is a ski pass in GrandValira?

The day pass costs adult €69, youth (12-17) €59 and child (6-11) €45.50. These are the prices on this page for the 2026/27 season.

What is a ski pass?

The ski pass is the ticket that gives you access to the resort's lifts for as long as you buy it for: without one you cannot go up the mountain, even with your own gear. In GrandValira, the adult day pass costs €69.

How much is a child's ski pass in GrandValira?

The child day pass costs €45.50 (ages 6-11). The youth pass, €59 (ages 12-17).

Is there a season pass in GrandValira?

Yes: adult €1365, youth (12-17) €1208 and child (6-11) €935.

Using your lift pass in GrandValira
Where do I collect the GrandValira lift pass?

You collect it in GrandValira, close to the lifts. Once the purchase is confirmed you get the voucher by email with the exact address; there is no need to print it, showing it on your phone is enough.

Purchase, payment and cancellation
What do I pay when I buy the lift pass?

You pay the full price of the pass online when you buy it; there is nothing else to pay when you collect it.

Can I cancel the lift pass and get my money back?

If you cancel more than 24 hours before the first day of the pass, 100% of what you paid is refunded, provided the resort allows it; within those 24 hours a refund is not guaranteed. It is all in the returns and cancellations policy.
