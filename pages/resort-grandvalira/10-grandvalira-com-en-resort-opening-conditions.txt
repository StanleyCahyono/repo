Opening Conditions Season
2024–2025 Opening conditions season
Update: 4/12/2024 12 pm

Grandvalira and Ordino Arcalís are working to partially open this Friday, December 6. The recent cold weather, coupled with light snowfalls and the efforts of the terrain teams, will enable the preparation of several slopes for skiing in the Pas de la Casa and Grau Roig sectors of Grandvalira, as well as in the Coma area of Ordino Arcalís.

Weather forecasts confirm the arrival of a cold front expected to bring snowfall across all elevations starting Saturday night and continuing at least through Monday. This would allow all three ski resorts in the country to open to the public starting next week.

Pal Arinsal and the Canillo sector of Grandvalira are preparing for a tourist-oriented opening on Friday, offering a variety of alternative activities to skiing, including the Winter Bike Park and the Mon(t) Magic Family Park.

Tomorrow, Thursday, December 5, detailed information on open slopes and services for this weekend will be released.
