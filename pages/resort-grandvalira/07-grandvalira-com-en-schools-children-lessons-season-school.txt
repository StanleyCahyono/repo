Seasonal ski school 

Enjoy the Grandvalira Season Ski School. The program aims to offer a ski school service to the youngest members of the family throughout the season. It's a flexible, high-quality and continuous service. This is a small program with limited places, combining skiing and other disciplines with a playful atmosphere and respect for the environment.

Màgic Ski in Grau Roig 

Màgic Ski is a recreational ski club that teaches students to enjoy the mountains through respect for nature and for people. Values such as respect, cooperation and teamwork are fundamental pillars of our teaching.

What is the Màgic Ski?

Màgic Ski offers skiing lessons to children throughout the season. The club's base is in the Grau Roig sector, where the facilities are perfectly suited to little ones who have just started skiing. Older children, together with their instructor, who will accompany them for the entire season, can explore Grandvalira, learning and perfecting their technique as they go.
