SNOW-SURE SKIING ON THE STUBAI GLACIER

In winter, the Stubai Glacier is a snow-sure glacier ski area in Tyrol. The high altitude, wide pistes, snow park facilities, freeride options and family-friendly areas make the ski area attractive for skiers, snowboarders, families and sporty guests.

THE STUBAI GLACIER AT A GLANCE
The Stubai Glacier is located in the Stubai Valley in Tyrol, Austria, near Neustift im Stubaital.
The mountain experience reaches up to over 3,000 meters above sea level.
You can reach the Stubai Glacier from Innsbruck in around 45 minutes.
In winter, the Stubai Glacier is one of the most snow-sure ski resorts in Tyrol.
In summer, the Stubai Glacier is an excursion destination for panoramic views, hiking, family adventures and cooling off in the mountains.
TOP OF TYROL, ice ridge, high-altitude playground, hikes, events, restaurants, webcams and live information.
