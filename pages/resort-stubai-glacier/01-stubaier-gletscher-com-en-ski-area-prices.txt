Dogs on the Stubai Glacier

Dogs are welcome on the Stubai Glacier and may accompany you free of charge.

If your dog accompanies you, please note the following information:

Gondola lifts: Dogs are only allowed on a leash and muzzle. Muzzles can be borrowed at the valley station for a deposit of EUR 25.
Chairlifts: For safety reasons, dogs are not allowed on chairlifts.
Ski slopes: In winter, dogs are not allowed on the ski slopes for safety reasons.
Restaurants: Dogs are welcome, provided they are kept on a lead. Exception: dogs are not allowed in the dining room of the Restaurant Schaufelspitz.
Shops: Dogs are welcome, provided they are kept on a lead. 

Arrival at the Stubai Glacier by public transport:

Dogs travel free of charge on the Innsbrucker Verkehrsbetriebe/VVT lines and the public buses (STUBAI) - muzzle and leash compulsory (registered assistance dogs are an exception to the muzzle requirement).
