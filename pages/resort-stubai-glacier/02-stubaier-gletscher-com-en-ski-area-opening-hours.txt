OPENING HOURS STUBAI GLACIER
CURRENT OPERATING TIMES & DATES

The Stubai Glacier is open for around 10 months a year. From mid-October to May, you can enjoy skiing here, while from July to mid-September, the Stubai Glacier is a popular destination for families, hikers, and via ferrata enthusiasts. Check out the current opening hours here.

Opening hours for the Neustift advance ticket sales office
Our ticket office in Neustift (opposite the BP petrol station) is open from the end of September to May at the following times:  

Monday to Saturday	7:30 a.m. to 12 p.m. & 2 p.m. to 5 p.m. 
Sundays and public holidays	7:30 a.m. to 12 p.m. & 3 p.m. to 5 p.m.
