The countdown is on! In just a few weeks’ time, the 26-27 winter season will be in full swing, inviting you to put your skis back on. France’s mountain resorts are getting ready to welcome you in the best possible conditions for a winter full of adventure, skiing and, above all, fun!

November openings:

The major high-altitude ski areas in France open at the end of November! And because large ski areas don’t mean difficult slopes, they will be able to delight all types of public with areas adapted to every level.
