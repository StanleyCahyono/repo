From beginner skiing to extreme freeriding, panoramic slopes and fun areas for children, Courchevel offers a complete skiing experience in the heart of the Alps.

With 150 km of slopes, modern ski lifts, renowned ski schools and direct access to the 3 Vallées ski area, everything has been designed to ensure that everyone can enjoy the mountains in their own way.

Find out more about skiing in Courchevel below: ski passes, schools, piste maps, ski area, tips for beginners and much more.

FAQ

How many kilometers of slopes are there in Courchevel?

The Courchevel ski area offers 150 km of marked slopes for all levels. It is connected to the 3 Vallées ski area, offering 600 km of skiing.

Where can I buy a ski pass in Courchevel?

You can buy or top up your pass online, on the Les 3 Vallées mobile app, at ticket machines, or at ticket offices in the resort.

Are there areas for learning to ski in Courchevel?

Yes. Easy Rider areas, accessible lifts, and renowned ski schools provide the best conditions for learning at any age.

Can you ski in Courchevel without taking a shuttle?

Yes. All of Courchevel's villages are connected by a smooth network of slopes and ski lifts. No transfers are necessary during the day.
