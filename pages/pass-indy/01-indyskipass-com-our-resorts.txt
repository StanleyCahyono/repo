Leave behind the mega lines and the mega prices. The Indy Pass offers two days each across 300+ uncrowded, authentic ski destinations across the United States, Canada, Europe and Japan. These independent ski areas are incredibly diverse but all have one thing in common—each offers smaller crowds, lower costs, and endlessly authentic vibes.
