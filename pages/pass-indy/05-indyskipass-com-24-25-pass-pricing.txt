24/25 PASS PRICING
PASSES ON SALE MARCH 1

Passes go on sale March 1 at 10 AM MT to previous passholders and waitlist members. Passes will be available to the general public starting March 8 at 10 AM MT. To honor our commitment to preserving the less-crowded nature of Indy resorts, the number of passes for sale will again be limited, it is strongly recommended to secure a pass as early as possible.
