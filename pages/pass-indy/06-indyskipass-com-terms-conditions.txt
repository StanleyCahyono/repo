Types of Indy Passes

Indy offers several pass products on a limited basis, including:

The “Indy Base Pass,” “Indy Base Kids Pass”, and the “Indy Add-On pass” (collectively referred to as a “Standard Pass”);
The “Indy+ Pass,” “Indy+ Kids Pass,” and “Indy+ Add-On Pass” (collectively referred to as a “Plus Pass”);

The “Learn to Turn Pass”; and
The “Indy XC Pass.”

Each product has distinct benefits and restrictions as described on Indy’s website and FAQ.

Add‑On and Indy Employee Passes require additional verification and may not be used until verification is complete. Failure to fulfill verification requirements may render the passholder ineligible for associated benefits or reimbursements. Verification requirements for the Add-On Pass are listed here. Verification requirements for the Indy Employee Pass are listed here. No refunds are provided for rejected usage prior to verification.

Indy Kids and Indy+ Kids passes are only for children under 13 years of age and will automatically upgrade to an adult pass upon reaching the age of 13 years old. 

300 Resort Guarantee

Passholders may request a refund before December 1, 2026, if the number of Indy Pass resort partners is less than 300 by November 1, 2026. A passholder can also request a refund for an unused pass by December 1, 2026, if their favorite resort from the 25/26 season does not renew for 26/27.

Blackout Dates 

Indy Base Pass, Indy Employee Pass, and LTT Pass products are subject to Blackout Dates. Indy Base Pass Blackouts are listed here. Indy Employee Pass Blackout Dates are listed here. LTT Pass Blackout Dates are listed here. Passholders are responsible for reviewing Blackout Dates before arrival. No refunds or reimbursements are provided for inability to ski on a posted Blackout Date. Blackout Dates are subject to change. Indy Plus Passes have no Blackout Dates.

Use of an Indy Pass

A Pass is not valid for Redemption at Partner Resorts until Activation is complete. To confirm Activation, log in to your profile and ensure your account is noted as ‘ready to ski.’ Activation requires:

A qualifying headshot, subject to Indy Pass review;
Executed Waiver; and
Completed payment.

For Add-On and Employee Passes, activation further requires photo verification which may take up to four business days.

To redeem your Indy Pass, present a valid government‑issued photo ID or your physical Indy Pass at the Partner Resort’s designated ticket window. You are responsible for checking the Partner Resort’s dedicated page on our website for Redemption locations and procedures. Indy is not liable for damages arising from attempts to redeem at a location other than that specified on the Partner Resort’s page.

Some Partner Resorts require purchase of an RFID card for lift access. RFID card purchases are not covered by your Indy Pass and are at the discretion of the Partner Resort.

Resort Access and Travel Restrictions

An Indy Pass does not guarantee access to any Partner Resort. Access is subject to the Partner Resort being open and having capacity. Partner Resorts may limit or deny access due to circumstances affecting operations. Partner Resorts may impose additional terms, conditions, releases, or waivers as a precondition to issuing access, and compliance is required to obtain a ticket. Indy is not responsible for resort closures or inability to access a Partner Resort due to access limitations outside of Indy’s control.

Travel to certain Partner Resort locations may be restricted by government agencies and may affect your ability to visit specific resorts at specific times without quarantine or other measures. Indy is not responsible for inability to visit a Partner Resort arising from governmental travel restrictions.

You may suffer injuries, illnesses, or other circumstances that prevent you from using your Pass. You are not entitled to a refund due to inability to use your Pass arising from such circumstances. Indy retains sole discretion to address Extraordinary Circumstances. In no event will reimbursement or a subsequent‑season pass be provided without: (a) a verified doctor’s note; (b) no more than two (2) Redemptions during the current Season; and (c) a written request received by April 1 of the Season for which accommodation is sought.

Reservations

To help avoid overcrowding, some Partner Resorts require Reservations. Partner Resorts requiring Reservations are listed here and are subject to change. Indy will make reasonable efforts to keep Passholders informed of which resorts require Reservations, but you are responsible for confirming requirements with the resort you plan to visit. Partner Resorts may cap daily Reservations. Only one Reservation per Pass may be made per day. Indy is not responsible for failure to obtain a Reservation or for capacity limitations at Partner Resorts.
If you make a Reservation and fail to redeem a ticket on that date without canceling within the Partner Resort’s timeframe, you will forfeit one day’s lift ticket at that resort. Indy Pass limits redemptions to one redemption per day. If you forfeit a redemption, you can not use your pass elsewhere in the same day.

Limitations and Restrictions on Pass Redemptions

Each Pass is for the exclusive use of the named Passholder and may be redeemed only once per day. Only one Indy Pass may be purchased per person, per Season. Passes are non‑transferable and non‑assignable. Pass benefits and Redemptions may not be resold or transferred. Violations may result in loss of benefits and may lead to criminal prosecution by Indy or Partner Resorts.

Indy Pass does not guarantee access to Partner Resorts’ events or special programming beyond the scope of a regular lift ticket (e.g., races, festivals, competitions). 

For Innsbruck Ski Resorts, Austria, passholders are entitled to two consecutive daily lift tickets total at any of the Innsbruck resorts listed on the Innsbruck Ski & City Network page. Additional information and restrictions are available on that page.

Passholders are prohibited from organizing, running, or participating in unauthorized snowsports lessons, guided tours, or other compensated services at Partner Resorts without the resort’s prior written authorization.

Indy Allied Program

Indy Pass holders are entitled to 25% off lift tickets on weekends and holidays between December 23 and March 10 of each Season and 50% off all other days at Allied Resorts. Passholder must present a valid Indy Pass or valid government‑issued ID to obtain this benefit. Allied Resorts are listed on Indy’s website.

Refund Policy

Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement or required by law, all pass products are non‑refundable and all sales are final. Once purchased, no refunds, reimbursements, credits, chargebacks, or other compensation will be provided, except as expressly stated in this Agreement.

Pass Protection Program

To be valid, Pass Protection must be purchased at the time of purchasing your Indy Pass, with no exceptions. Each Pass must have a separate Pass Protection policy to be eligible. Eligibility requires: (1) Pass Protection must be purchased at time of Pass purchase for the specific Pass; (2) zero Redemptions during the current Season; and (3) a written exchange request received no later than April 1 of the current season.

Once a pass is rolled over, no additional refunds or rollovers will be accepted without purchase of the subsequent year's Pass Protection. 

Additional information is available here.

Use of Likeness

By using an Indy Pass, you grant Indy and each Partner Resort the right to capture, use, and publish worldwide and in perpetuity, in any media now known or later developed (including online and social media), without approval or compensation, any image or performance of you (or of any individual for whom you are signing) while at a Partner Resort.

Automatic Renewal

All Indy Passes except for the Employee Pass and the Learn to Turn Pass are a subscription service that will automatically renew to the same pass type on a determined date each year using your default payment method and prior payment plan choice at the guaranteed lowest price. You may cancel at any time by following the instructions on the Indy Pass website’s cancellation page. 

Indy Kids and Indy+ Kids passes will upgrade to an adult Indy or Indy+ Pass if the passholder is will be over 13 years of age on March 1 of the Season, in which case passholders will be charged the then-current price for the Adult Pass. 

Add-On Passes will automatically renew but are subject to verification prior to use. A passholder that no longer qualifies for an Add-On Pass can upgrade to a different Indy Pass or request a refund of the renewal charge.

Learn to Turn passes will not automatically renew but Learn to Turn passholders will be given the option to upgrade to a different pass on February 25 each year.

Indy will send a renewal reminder fifteen (15) days in advance of your renewal date. The reminder will include the renewal date, the renewal charge, and instructions for cancelling your subscription.

Upon cancellation, or upon failure to tender payment at renewal, you may continue to use your Pass for the remainder of the Season for which you hold a valid Pass and you will not be charged for subsequent Seasons. You are not entitled to a partial refund upon cancellation, and you remain obligated under any previously agreed Payment Plan terms. 

Indy Pass limits pass sales each year to ensure that partner resorts remain less crowded. Thus, If you cancel, your spot may be offered immediately to a waitlisted passholder for the following Season. If you forfeit your spot, you may need to return to the waitlist to resubscribe and may not be eligible for discounts or early‑purchase options afforded to prior passholders.
