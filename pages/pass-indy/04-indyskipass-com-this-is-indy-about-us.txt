THIS IS INDY
DEVOTED TO SKIING. DEVOTED TO SKIERS.
We’re here because skiing matters. And so do people who ski.
IT'S BIGGER THAN US

Indy Pass is rooted in the belief that when people unite around something they love and believe in, good things happen. That’s why we’re not just another multi-resort pass product, but an actual living, breathing community of skiers and riders who love this sport and believe it should remain welcoming and accessible to all. 

MORE SKIING. LESS MONEY.

The Indy Pass unlocks 300+ independent resorts across North America, Europe, Japan, and South America. Never before has a multi-resort pass offered so much terrain, and so many options, for so little. Take your pass on a global powder quest, or pick your favorite region and let ‘er rip. And don’t forget to smile, because you’re getting an unparalleled skiing experience at an unbeatable price. 

THE KIDS ARE ALRIGHT

More than any other reason, Indy Pass exists because our children deserve the opportunity to forge a connection with the mountains that will stay with them for the rest of their lives. That has the potential to fundamentally change their lives. This has always been the promise of skiing. Now, it’s up to all of us to help uphold it.
