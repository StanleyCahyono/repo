WINTER IN ISCHGL AT A GLANCE

Ischgl in winter is more than just a holiday destination – it’s a real snow adventure at altitudes of up to 2,872 metres. Skiers, snowboarders, families and leisure-seekers can look forward to action, enjoyment and plenty of variety in the Tyrolean Paznaun region from 26 November 2026 to 2 May 2027.

From the Silvretta Arena with its endless skiing fun, to stunning winter walking trails, right through to the Silvretta Therme and legendary top events: there’s always something going on in Ischgl!

Silvretta Arena: boundless skiing fun up to 2,872 metres in the Tyrolean Alps and Switzerland.

Skiing & snowboarding: challenging runs, panoramic slopes and the legendary ‘Smugglers’ Circuit’.

Lifestyle & Leisure: Alpine cuisine, après-ski and unforgettable winter moments

Top Events: Top of the Mountain concerts, Formen in Weiß, Spring Blanc & other highlights

Ischgl is a popular winter sports resort with a season that lasts until the beginning of May. The ski resort is located at an altitude of 2,872 meters and offers 239 kilometers of slopes and snow parks.
