SILVRETTA SKI PASS IS YOUR TICKET TO COUNTLESS MOUNTAIN MOMENTS
IN THE WHOLE PAZNAUN REGION

The Silvretta Ski Pass is your ticket to 75 lifts and cable cars in Ischgl, Galtür, Kappl and See. Simply hop on, ride to the top and get to the starting points of the region’s best slopes!
The ski season in Ischgl/Samnaun starts at the end of November. The Silvretta ski pass is the ideal equipment for conquering the slopes until the beginning of May. 

valid at 75 lifts in Ischgl/Samnaun, Galtür, Kappl and See
For guests without a guest card from Ischgl/Mathon
No price reduction for closed lifts in Galtür, Kappl, See (off-season)
Children under 8 years accompanied by a parent travel free.
 

The company reserves the right to change prices, also on a daily basis, especially during events.
