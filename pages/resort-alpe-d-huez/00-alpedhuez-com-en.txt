Nestled in the heart of the Grandes Rousses massif, the resort radiates with its exceptional sunshine: more than 300 days of sun per year. Here, the light sublimates each panorama, from sunrise to sunset, offering a unique atmosphere between snowy peaks and green pastures.

In winter, the Alpe d’Huez deploys an exceptional ski area: more than 250 km of slopes, including the famous Sarenne, the longest black slope in the world with its 16 km of uninterrupted descent. Beginners, families or experienced riders, everyone finds their ideal playground among 135 varied tracks, served by modern and fluid lifts.

Thrill-seekers enjoy: freeride in the mythical corridors of the Pic Blanc, XXL snowpark, toboggan on rails, paragliding, ice climbing or even snowmobile… Alpe d’Huez is the mountain in all its power and generosity.
