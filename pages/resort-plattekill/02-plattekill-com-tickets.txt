RESCHEDULING TICKETS

If you are unable to use your tickets on the original date of purchase, you can change the tickets to a new date in the current season.   A $20/per ticket change fee will apply.  There are no refunds/credits for a change to a lower price day and any difference in ticket price will be charged in addition to this fee.

Tickets must be rescheduled  BY 5PM THE DAY PRIOR TO YOUR ORIGINAL BOOKED DATE or your tickets will be considered expired.   There is NO RESCHEDULING AFTER THIS TIME.

To reschedule: 
CLICK HERE enter the email associated with your order, login to your account and reschedule your ticket(s)

REFUNDS
There are NO REFUNDS for online ticket purchases and there is no guarantee on the number of trails/lifts open for the date for which you are purchasing.
Plattekill will not refund or discount tickets based on conditions and/or trail count.

PLEASE NOTE

Children must be at least 3-feet tall to tube and are not allowed to ride in adult laps. Children must sit in their own tubes. Tubes do have handles and adults can slide together with children.

Tubing tickets are not transferable from person to person.  Everyone must be ticketed to enter tubing area.

We do NOT OFFER REFUNDS for online ticket purchases of tubing tickets for any reason.

ALL RATES AND DATES ARE SUBJECT TO CHANGE WITHOUT NOTICE.
Plattekill is not responsible for lost lift tickets or lost/stolen property.

RESCHEDULING TICKETS

Tubing tickets unable to be used on the original date you purchased may be changed to a new date in the current season. A $5/per ticket change fee will apply.

Tickets must be rescheduled BY 5PM THE DAY PRIOR TO YOUR ORIGINAL BOOKED DATE or your tickets will be considered expired. 

There is NO RESCHEDULING AFTER THIS TIME.

To reschedule: 
– visit shop.plattekill.com, enter the email associated with your order & set up a password
– login and reschedule your ticket(s)
– Questions?  Call us  (607) 326-3500 X 116
