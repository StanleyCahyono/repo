FAMILY MOUNTAIN

Greetings from Plattekill Mountain,
Discover BIG MOUNTAIN skiing across 41 exciting trails and 1100′ of vertical, and the best part is you’ll enjoy it all in a laid-back family atmosphere.  We’re the last family-owned and operated ski mountain in the Northern Catskills of New York offering skiing, snowboarding and snowtubing fun, plus great events and après too!
DISCOVER THE FAMILY-RUN SKI MOUNTAIN DIFFERENCE!!!

We look forward to welcoming you here this winter.

EVENTS

We've got a whole bunch of events planned this winter from Demo Days to Karaoke Nights to apres ski parties and a whole lineup of live music in between! Come and join the fun on and off...the slopes!
