Rescheduling Rentals:

Rentals unable to be used on the original date for which you purchased may be changed to a new date in the current season, however all rescheduling must be completed BY 5PM THE DAY PRIOR TO YOUR BOOKED DATE.  There is NO RESCHEDULING AFTER THIS TIME – your rental will be considered expired.

To reschedule:
– visit shop.plattekill.com
– enter the email associated with your order & set up a password
– login and reschedule your rental(s)

SEASON RENTAL RETURNS:

The lodge is open for returns:
Monday – Friday 10am-3pm
Saturday/Sunday 9am-3pm

All rentals must be returned by April 30 or a $25/per rental set late fee will be charged. 

Rentals unreturned by May 15 will result in charges for equipment replacement with the credit card used for the season rental purchase.
* $250 unreturned skis or board, $100 unreturned boots, $75 unreturned poles or helmet
