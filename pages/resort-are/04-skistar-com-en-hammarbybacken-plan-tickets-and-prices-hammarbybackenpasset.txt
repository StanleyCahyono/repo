Experience Hammarbybacken's range of activities in one session, go and be as active as you like.

What’s included in the HammarbybackenPasset

An action-packed day at Hammarbybacken – fill your time with as much as you want.

SummerSki including ski rental (available only 13–15)
New: Climbing park*
Mountain Coaster

* The climbing park can be used once per day and has a limited duration per visit. All activities are subject to availability, and waiting times may occur.

Some activities have specific safety requirements, such as height and age restrictions. The HammarbybackenPasset cannot be combined with other discounts.

In case of rain and thunder

Some activities may need to close on short notice due to weather conditions to ensure safety.

Find opening hours and get notified about changes in the SkiStar app.

SummerSki

Now you have the opportunity to ski during both summer and autumn - in the middle of Stockholm. SummerSki in Hammarbybacken is an approx. 9000 m2 skiable surface in the form of artificial grass that mimics the feeling of skiing on snow.

SummerSki
Mountain Coaster

Experience Hammarbybacken’s slopes in SkiStar’s Mountain Coasters. Ride a coaster along an 800-metre track with a drop of around 100 metres from top to bottom.

Mountain Coaster
Experience Mountain Tube

Head down the hill in a rubber ring at full speed and feel the butterflies in your stomach. Mountain Tube is a fun attraction that suits everyone in the family.

Experience Mountain Tube
Climbing park

Together with Högt & Lågt, we have a climbing park at Hammarbybacken. The climbing park is a perfect activity for a group of friends, family or conference.
