Lift opening times
Lift opening times

Here you'll find the opening hours for the lifts, from the start to the end of the ski season.

In the SkiStar app you can see weather, open lifts and pistes.

Date season 2026/27
Ski area	Opens	Closes
Vemdalsskalet*	27 November	18 April
Björnrike	18 December	11 April
Klövsjö	18 December	4 April
Storhogna	18 December	4 April

*Vemdalsskalet is open Thursday-Sunday until 17 December. All opening dates are subject to snow availability.

Vemdalsskalet opens in conjunction with Ski premiere, Ski Test & Christmas Market at Vemdalsskalet.
