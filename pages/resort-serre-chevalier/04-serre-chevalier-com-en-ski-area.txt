6-day Galaxie or 6-day Monêtier pass?

We'll tell you all about it!

Serre Chevalier is made up of 4 sectors: Briançon, Chantemerle, Villeneuve and Le Monêtier Les Bains, and two 6-day Pass options are available to you:

✅ Pass Serre Chevalier Galaxie: access to the entire Serre Chevalier ski area.
✅ Pass Monêtier: exclusive access to the Monêtier sector.
Advantages of Pass Serre Chevalier 6 days and +:

👉 "Grande Galaxie" agreement (from 21.12.2025) get 1 day free on each of the following areas:

Alpe d'Huez (except from 21 to 28.03.2026) / Les Deux Alpes / Puy Saint-Vincent / Montgenèvre-Vialattea / Sestrières-Vialattea

Practical details:

Pass purchased at a Point Info Vente: present your Serre Chevalier Pass and Galaxie voucher at the ticket office of the partner resort.
Pass purchased from a vending machine or recharged online: pick up your Galaxie voucher at the Serre Chevalier Point Info Vente, then go to the ticket office in the partner resort.
Pass purchased online (excluding recharging): receive your Galaxie voucher by post and present it with your Serre Chevalier Pass at the partner resort ticket office.

🎟️ Other exclusive benefits:

-10% off a 2-hour admission to the Grands Bains du Monêtier (on presentation of pass and proof of purchase - excluding school vacations & weekends).
-25% off adult day passes at La Grave (on presentation of pass and proof of purchase at the La Grave ticket office).

What is the Family Pass?

The Family Pass is designed for all families, whether traditional, blended or simply made up of friends and family sharing time together.

Do you ski for at least 6 days and are you a group of at least 4 skiers?

Group composition :

1 or 2 adults aged 21 to 64 inclusive
Children aged 6 to 11 and/or juniors aged 12 to 20

👉 If so, take advantage of the Family Pass!

This pass is also valid for the Giant Tyrolienne experience at altitude and allows you to benefit from the advantages of the Serre Chevalier Pass 6 days and more.

Handiski and reduced mobility package

The Serre Chevalier Vallée Briançon ski area and all its staff have been working for several years to make skiing and mountain activities accessible to people with disabilities

Find out more about prices and the list of equipment here

What are the group rates for children under 6 and over 75?
For children under 6, free passes are available online or at info sales points.
Passesfor over 75s are also free and are available at info sales points.
For group rates for 20 people or more, please send a request by e-mail to scv.groupes@compagniedesalpes.fr

What is the Discovery Pass?

This package offers you 5 or 30 lift passes, depending on the options selected below:

5-pass Discovery Pass, valid for 1 day
30-pass Discovery Pass, valid for 6 days
Valid for the entire ski area (lifts not counted)
