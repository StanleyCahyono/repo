The ski area in figures :

- 410 hectares of marked ski area

- 60 ski lifts, including the emblematic Serre Chevalier cable car

New for the 2023/2024 season: the PONTILLAS gondola takes over from two previous lifts, providing fast access to the Méa plateau at an altitude of 2,251 metres.

- 80 runs (13 black, 28 red, 25 blue and 14 green)

- 80% of the runs are located at an altitude of over 2,000 metres for optimum snow cover.

For even more fun in Serre Chevalier :

Fun zones with a new area: EasyBoarder on the Méa plateau (Villeneuve)

Protected areas for peaceful family skiing

Discovery areas to get your first taste of skiing

Nature areas for a trip to the land of sensations, in the middle of larch forests, typical of the Hautes Alpes.

The ski area stretches from Briançon to Monêtier les Bains, linking the town and the 4 villages of the Guisane valley.
