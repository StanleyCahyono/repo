WHAT IS AN EPIC DAY PASS?

The Epic Day Pass is a modern alternative to traditional lift tickets, with better access, more flexibility and up to 65% less than buying a lift ticket at the window. It’s like a flexible punchcard for daily lift access that also comes with the benefits of being a Pass Holder, like additional savings on food, lodging, lessons and rentals.

You don’t need to use the days consecutively or decide when and where you’ll visit in advance. Just select the number of days and resorts that you want to visit, then show up and hit the slopes when you want. 

Available for a Limited Time
As our Thank You for committing to ski or snowboard with us in advance, we offer big savings and more flexibility. This deal isn’t available later in the season, so make sure to lock in your Epic Day Pass while they’re still on sale!

Planning to visit during a holiday?
To ensure a great guest experience on the slopes, a handful of the most popular days (around major holidays) are designated as “Restricted Peak Dates” with access limited to certain Passes. If you plan to visit on any of these dates, please ensure you select “Yes” on the Peak Dates selector when building your Epic Day Pass.
