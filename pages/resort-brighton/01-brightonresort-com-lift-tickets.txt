WHY CHOOSE A BRIGHTON WINTER

There are plenty of places to ride in Utah. There’s only one Brighton. Come for the snow, stay for the night laps, find your people in the lift line, and leave a slightly better rider than you showed up. That’s pretty much how we like it.

WE TAKE WINTER SERIOUSLY

Not ourselves. Winter. Brighton averages 500+ inches of Wasatch snow each year, backed by high elevation, snowmaking, and a grooming crew that works hard to make the season as long and rideable as possible.

GOOD PEOPLE. GOOD LAPS.

Brighton is the kind of place where families, pros, first-timers, lifers, and after-work warriors end up sharing the same chairs. Different reasons for being here. Same reason for coming back.

RIDERS WELCOME. ALWAYS.

Snowboarding didn't have to earn a seat at the table here. Brighton embraced it early, and that creative, expressive riding culture still runs through the terrain, parks, style, and people who call this mountain home.

PROGRESSION HAPPENS HERE

You don’t have to be the best rider on the mountain. You just need somewhere to go next. Brighton’s mix of terrain gives everyone from first-timers to lifelong riders plenty of room to keep progressing.

WE STAY UP LATE

Most mountains call it around 4, but that's when we turn the lights on. Ride 200+ illuminated acres across 32 lit runs and 5 lifts, six nights a week.

COME AS YOU ARE

First timer. Lifelong local. Two planks. One plank. Tiny human in a dinosaur suit. If you’re here to have a good time on the mountain, you’re one of us.
