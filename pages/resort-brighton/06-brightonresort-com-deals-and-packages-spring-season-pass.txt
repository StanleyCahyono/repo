SPRING PASS PERKS

Make the most of spring with added benefits that go beyond lift access.

UNLIMITED RIDING

Ride as much as you want from March 27 through Closing Day—no blackouts, just all-day access.

MELTDOWN ACCESS

Ride The Meltdown from April 20 through Closing Day. Spring laps, stacked features, and top-to-bottom park riding.

NIGHT RIDING

Keep the day going with access to Brighton’s night riding operations.

BOYNEREWARDS

Earn points when you ride, dine, and shop across Brighton and other Boyne Resorts.

Link your pass to your card for quick and easy payments across the resort.

Spring turns, soft snow, and stacked park lines await. The Meltdown runs April 20 to Closing Day—your bonus season starts here.
