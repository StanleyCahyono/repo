WHAT COMES WITH YOUR PACK
10 TICKET PACK PERKS

A ticket pack is built around the fact that riding is better with people. Ten days, no names attached, used however the season shakes out.

FULLY TRANSFERABLE

The days are not tied to you. Hand one to a friend flying in, cover a kid’s day off school, or put the whole crew on the hill at once.

NO BLACKOUT DATES

Valid any day we are open. Holidays, weekends, powder mornings, and nights under the lights all count the same.

EVERYONE GETS A TURN

Ten tickets can go to ten people on one day or one person across ten days. Nothing has to be decided when you buy.

DAY & NIGHT, ONE TICKET

Each ticket runs up to 12 hours, so a storm morning and a lap under the lights can come out of the same day.

RIDE ON YOUR SCHEDULE

Days do not need to be used back to back. Hold them for a storm cycle, a visit from out of town, or the stretch of the season you like best.

BOYNEREWARDS

Earn points when you ride, dine, and shop at Brighton and across Boyne Resorts, then redeem them for digital reward cards to use around the mountain.

All tickets are valid for the current winter season only and expire at the end of the season.
