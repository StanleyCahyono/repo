LIFT TICKETS MADE EASY
GET TO THE GOOD PART

Better rates when you plan ahead, fewer stops when you get here, and options when plans change. Because we’d rather get you on snow.

BUY EARLY & SAVE

The earlier you buy, the more you can save. Lock in your lift tickets ahead of time and unlock up to 50% off window rates.

SKIP THE TICKET WINDOW

Already have a Brighton Card? Reload it with your new ticket or pass and head straight to the lifts. New here? We’ll set you up with one at checkout.

PLAN A LITTLE. SAVE A LOT.

If your schedule has some wiggle room, so does the price. Weekdays, non-holidays, and early or late season dates can mean lower rates and more room on the mountain.

STICK AROUND AWHILE

The more consecutive days you add to your ticket, the more you can save. Because one Brighton day has a habit of turning into three.

PLANS CHANGE. WE GET IT.

Plans went sideways? Easy Change lets you move the full value of your lift ticket to a credit voucher. No penalties. No wasted ski day.

LEARN MORE ABOUT EASY CHANGE

EARN BOYNEREWARDS

Earn points when you ride, dine, and shop at Brighton and other Boyne Resorts. Redeem them for digital reward cards to use across the mountain.
