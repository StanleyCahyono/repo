WHY CHOOSE A PACK OR GO CARD
MORE DAYS, LESS PLANNING

Packs and Go Cards sit between a single lift ticket and a full season pass. You get a better rate than buying day of, the freedom to ride when conditions line up, and no pressure to use every day at once.

Better Than The Window Rate

Buying your days in advance costs less than buying them one at a time at the ticket window. The more days you load, the less each one costs.

No Blackout Dates

Your days are good on any open day of the season, including holidays, weekends, powder mornings, and nights under the lights. Ride when the mountain is best, not when a calendar allows it.

Straight to the Lift

Go Cards load onto one RFID card and stay there. Skip the ticket window, head for the chair, and start your day where a day should start.

Yours, Or Your Crew's

Go Cards are personal, built for riders stacking their own days. Ticket packs are fully transferable, so you can hand days to family, friends, or a group coming into town.

Ride On Your Schedule

There is nothing to book in advance. Hold your days for a storm, a visit from out of town, or a stretch of blue sky, and use them as the season unfolds.
