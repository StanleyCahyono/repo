WHAT COMES WITH YOUR CARD
GO CARD PERKS

A Go Card is more than a stack of tickets. It changes how your day starts and what it costs you to get up here more often.

DIRECT-TO-LIFT ACCESS

Your tickets live on one RFID card. No ticket window, no daily redemption, nothing to pull up with cold hands. Walk to the gate and go.

UP TO 40% OFF

Loading your days in advance costs meaningfully less than buying them one at a time at the window, and the savings grow with every day you add.

NO BLACKOUT DATES

Valid any day we are open. Holidays, weekends, powder mornings, and nights under the lights all count the same.

ALL DAY, ALL NIGHT

Each ticket runs up to 12 hours, so a storm morning and a lap under the lights can come out of the same day.

RIDE ON YOUR SCHEDULE

Days do not need to be used back to back. Hold them for a storm cycle, a visit from out of town, or the stretch of the season you like best.

BOYNEREWARDS

Earn points when you ride, dine, and shop at Brighton and across Boyne Resorts, then redeem them for digital reward cards to use around the mountain.

GO CARDS VS. TICKET PACKS

Two ways to ride, built for different needs.

While both products offer flexibility, Go Cards and Ticket Packs are designed for different types of riders.

Go Cards

Personal, single-rider product
Non-transferable
Tickets are pre-loaded onto one RFID card
Go straight to the lift, no ticket window required
Best for solo riders and repeat visits

Ticket Packs

Shareable across multiple riders
Tickets can be used by different people on the same or different days
Tickets must be redeemed or assigned per rider
May require visiting the ticket window
Best for families, groups, or hosting friends
TICKET DETAILS
Choose 3, 4, or 5 All-Day lift tickets, all loaded onto one Go Card
Scan and ride — tickets redeem automatically at the lift gates
Valid Opening Day 2026 through Closing Day, April 2027
Ride on your schedule — tickets don’t need to be used on consecutive days
Designed for one rider (non-transferable)
Up to 40% savings compared to 2026/27 full-day window lift ticket rates
