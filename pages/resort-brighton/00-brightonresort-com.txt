MOUNTAIN BIKING

Brighton’s bike park offers lift-served riding in Big Cottonwood Canyon, with progressive terrain for beginners through intermediate riders. From flowy green trails to more technical blue runs, it’s a place to build confidence and spend a summer day on the mountain.

WEDDINGS, BANQUETS & MORE
HOST AN EVENT

Weddings, banquets, showers or something in between—Brighton is where people come together. Just 35 minutes from Salt Lake City, our mountain spaces offer a scenic escape without the long drive. With year-round venues, local catering, and the peaks of Big Cottonwood as your backdrop, it’s an easy choice for unforgettable gatherings.

THERE'S NO PLACE LIKE HOME
THE BRIGHTON SIDE OF LIFE

We’re not like other resorts, and we don’t want to be. Our terrain and welcoming vibe just hits different, making it the place where all riders – from first-timers to pro cliff-huckers – feel right at home. Here, skiing and riding stay true to what they should be: pure, accessible fun for all.
