Season Pass Presale Winter 2026/27

Starting 27 November 2026, the presale for the Ski Arlberg season passes for the 2026/27 winter season begins.

Show presale prices
PRESALE
Buy your season pass and experience limitless skiing pleasure at Ski Arlberg

From 27 November to 10 December 2026, season passes will be available at reduced presale prices in the webshop and at selected ski pass offices.

Presale Prices
Season pass Ski Arlberg	Presale Price
Adults	€ 840,00
Children (2011-2018)	€ 345,00
Youths (2007-2010) / Seniors (1953-1962)	€ 676,00
Family Bonus from the 3rd child ²
¹ Personal pickup and ID required when purchasing a season pass (only at ski pass offices for families). The family bonus applies to families with at least three children. Children and youth must belong to the same household as their parents. Partners must live in the same household. Proof must be provided through official photo ID and residence registration forms.
² For Ski Arlberg season passes, the third and younger siblings (children or youth born in 2007 or later) receive a free season pass, provided at least two older siblings and one parent purchase a Ski Arlberg season pass for the same period. All tickets must be bought at the same time and place.
³ Pickup also possible after 10 December 2026 (however, the purchase must be made by 10 December 2026).
All prices in euros, including VAT. A € 5.00 deposit applies for the Ski Arlberg chip card. The Ski Arlberg general terms and conditions apply. Prices and information are subject to change. The chip card remains the property of Ski Arlberg. The full tariff overview is available at www.skiarlberg.at. Season passes are valid throughout the entire Ski Arlberg area.
