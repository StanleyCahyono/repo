Ski ticket prices

Group deal

For every 20 paying participants with the, your group receives one free ticket, provided a collective purchase is made with a participant list on company or club letterhead. Additionally, the bus driver, if carrying winter sports equipment, receives a free ski ticket, or, if without equipment, a complimentary ascent and descent in a cable car (e.g., Bergbahn Oberlech, Rüfikopf).

Payment

The following payment methods are accepted for ski tickets:

Cash
EC/Maestro or credit card (Euro/Mastercard, Visa, Diners Club, American Express)
Bank transfer in advance
Chip cards

All ski tickets are issued on the reusable Ski Arlberg chip card.

Deposit fee: €5.00
Refund available at our cash desks if the card is not kept for your next visit.

Collection

Ski tickets can be collected and paid for at your chosen cash desk.

Parking options
In Lech at Schlosskopf (for cable car users with a valid ski ticket)
Zürs at Trittkopf

offers free daily parking for cars and buses.
