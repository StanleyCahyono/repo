Winter is Coming: Ski Opening Dates in Austria
8 Sept 2026

Which Austrian winter resorts are the first to open and how long can skiers enjoy the pistes? Check out the must-know dates in a nutshell.

Austria’s ski season is just around the corner, with the country’s first slopes set to welcome skiers and snowboarders from early October . From high-altitude glacier skiing in the autumn to the wider opening of Austria‘s mountain resorts as winter gets underway, there are plenty of opportunities to get on the snow early.

Here’s an overview of when Austria’s leading ski resorts are set to open for the 2026/27 season – and how long skiers can enjoy the pistes.

Please note: Weather and snow conditions can shift dates at short notice.

October 2026

Zillertal
Early October  2026 (Hintertux Glacier) – 11 April 2027

Carinthia
3 October 2026  (Mölltaler Glacier) – 30 May 2027

Zell am See-Kaprun
10 October 2026 (Kitzsteinhorn) – May 2027

Sölden
Mid-October 2026 (glaciers) – end of April 2027

Stubai Valley
Mid-October 2026 – mid-April 2027

November 2027

Kitzbühel
6 November 2026 – 4 April 2027

Obergurgl-Hochgurgl
19 November 2026 – 18 April 2027

Obertauern
20 November 2026 – 2 May 2027

Ischgl
26 November 2026 – 2 May 2027

Tiroler Zugspitz Arena
27 November 2026 – early April 2027

Saalbach-Hinterglemm
27 November 2026 – 4 Apri 2027

Silvretta Montafon
27 November 2026 – 11 April 2027

Schladming-Dachstein
End of November 2026 – early April 2027

December 2027

St. Anton am Arlberg
2 December 2026 – 18 April 2027

Lech Zürs
2 December 2026 – 18 April 2027

Serfaus-Fiss-Ladis
4 December 2026 – 4 April 2027

Ski Juwel Alpbachtal Wildschönau
4 December 2026 – 4 April 2027

St. Johann in Tirol
4 December 2026 – 29 March 2027

Grossarltal
4 December 2026 – end of April 2027

SkiWelt Wilder Kaiser Brixental
5 December 2026 – 4 April 2027

Hochkönig
5 December 2026 – 4 April 2027

Kleinwalsertal
Early December 2026 – early April 2027

Innsbruck
12 December 2026 – 25 April 2027

Holiday Region TirolWest
19 December 2026 – 14 March 2027
