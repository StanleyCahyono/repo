WHY HIRE YOUR SNOW GEAR AT PERISHER?

Planning a trip to Perisher and wondering whether to bring your own gear or rent on the mountain? Hiring ski and snowboard equipment at Perisher comes with a range of benefits that can enhance your experience, save you time, and even improve your performance on the slopes. Here’s why renting your snow gear at Perisher is the smart choice.

Convenience Right at the Slopes

Why carry heavy ski and snowboard gear when you can hire everything you need right in resort? Perisher has multiple rental locations across the mountain, including Bullocks Flat, Perisher Valley, and Smiggin Holes, so you can easily pick up and drop off your gear without transporting it from home.

 

Access to High-Quality and Well-Maintained Equipment

Perisher’s rental gear is professionally maintained and regularly updated, ensuring you receive equipment that’s tuned, fitted, and ready for the mountain. Unlike off‑mountain rental options, which may require you to leave the slopes and travel back to town if something breaks, doesn’t fit, or needs adjusting, Perisher Sports has expert fitters right on the mountain. If anything feels off or needs swapping during your day, support is only steps away, meaning more time riding and less time troubleshooting.

 

Try the Latest Gear and Find the Right Fit

Renting lets you experience Snowsports at your level. Whether you’re a beginner seeking forgiving equipment or an advanced rider looking for top-tier performance, Perisher Sports has options to suit every ability level and snow condition. Trained staff will also help you find the ideal fit for comfort and confidence.

Perfect for First-Timers and Families

If you’re new to snow sports, renting is a cost‑effective way to try skiing or snowboarding without committing to expensive gear. It’s also ideal for families, especially kids who quickly outgrow their equipment, making renting a hassle‑free and budget‑friendly option. 

Hassle-Free Travel

Flying or driving with bulky gear can make your trip a hassle. By hiring equipment at Perisher, you avoid baggage fees, transport challenges, and potential damage. Everything you need is available on‑site, so you can travel light and stress‑free.

Save Money and Storage Space

Buying and maintaining snow gear can be costly, particularly if you only visit the slopes occasionally. Renting means you don’t need to invest in expensive equipment, tuning services, or storage space, making it great value for casual skiers and boarders.

Easily Swap Gear 

One of the biggest perks of renting from Perisher Sports is the flexibility to swap between skis and a snowboard once per rental booking free-of-charge. With multiple rental locations across the resort, switching setups is simple. Whether you want to carve morning groomers on skis or hit the park on a snowboard in the afternoon, you can adapt to conditions or just try something new.

Make the Most of Your Perisher Experience

Hiring your snow gear at Perisher maximises convenience, performance, and overall enjoyment. With high‑quality equipment, expert advice, and the freedom to adjust your setup, you can focus on making the most of your time on the mountain.

So next time you head to Perisher, skip the hassle and rent your gear on the mountain. You’ll be glad you did!

How To Book Your Ski & Snowboard Rentals

Booking ski and snowboard rentals at Perisher is quick and easy. Reserve your gear online in advance for maximum discounts, to save time upon arrival and secure your preferred setup. Gear can be picked up from one of Perisher Sports’ convenient rental locations, including Perisher Valley, Smiggin Holes, and Bullocks Flat.
