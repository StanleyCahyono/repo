OFFICIAL OPENING DATES OF THE TIGNES RESORT

Looking for a mountain getaway? Here are the opening dates for Tignes, the ski resort where you can enjoy the pleasures of the slopes. With slopes at an altitude of over 3000 m, you can hit the slopes from November to May, to the delight of snow lovers.

Find out all about when the Tignes – Val d’Isère ski area is open in summer and winter. Glacier, ski area, Lagon, Tignespace, Aventure Park, summer activity centres… You’ll find everything you need to make great memories in the mountains, surrounded by the people you love.

WINTER 2026-2027
FROM 21 NOVEMBER TO 2 MAY 2027

Winter sports fans are in for a treat! Tignes was one of the first resorts to open in France. The Perce-Neige funicular provides quick access to the Grande Motte glacier, the highest point in the resort at 3656 metres.

In spring, enjoy skiing in pleasant sunshine and soft snow. The resort remains open until early May. During the Easter holidays, the snow cover remains optimal for a guaranteed spring skiing adventure. Ski again and again!

SKI AREA

Progressive opening of the Tignes ski area from 21 November* and then the link ski area with Val d’Isère from 28 November*.

*subject to favorable snow conditions.

SUMMER 2026
FROM 20 JUNE TO 6 SEPTEMBER

Enjoy summer skiing on the Grande Motte Glacier, the Bike Park linked to Val d’Isère, Lake Tignes and its water sports centre, and all the activities on offer!

MY TIGNES ACTIVITIES

With the My Tignes activity programme, you can enjoy a wide range of activities for all the family: tennis, mini-golf, archery, lift access, trampolines, etc. The My Tignes card activities start from June (progressive opening of all activity sites).

THE BIKE PARK

Head to the trails of the Tignes Bike Park from Saturday 27 June until 30 August 2026.

SUMMER SKIING ON THE GRANDE MOTTE GLACIER

In 2026, enjoy summer skiing at an altitude of over 3 000 metres on the Grande Motte Glacier from 20 June to 19 July.
