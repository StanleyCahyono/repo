A SKI PASS FOR EVERYONE IN TIGNES
SUMMER SEASON 2026

For a fortnight, a week, a day or even à la carte… put together your very own Tignes package. The diversity of our offers serves a single purpose: to ensure you have the ski holiday of your dreams in our resort!

For children, seniors, tribes, handicapped skiers and even pedestrians… there’s a lift pass for everyone, so you can hurtle down the slopes of Tignes, enjoy the breathtaking views or take advantage of the mountain restaurants.

At Tignes, we do everything we can to make your holiday as easy as possible. As proof, you can of course buy your ski passes from our advisors, but also directly online, or in just a few minutes from our express purchase terminals. All you have to do is enjoy the slopes!

FREE SKI PASS

At Tignes, the Tignes – Val d’Isère ski pass is FREE for children under 8 and at the child rate up to and including 18.

ALL THE ADVANTAGES OF TIGNES SKI PASSES
SKI PASSES TO SUIT ALL SKIERS… AND ALL FAMILIES!

At Tignes, we think of everyone: families, young people, seniors, ski enthusiasts and day skiers. Here, ski passes aren’t just tickets to the slopes: they’re real keys to one of the most beautiful ski areas in the Alps, with special conditions so that everyone can make the most of their stay

FAMILY-FRIENDLY SKIING

Because the mountains should be fun for everyone, Tignes offers free ski passes for children under 8. And that’s not all: the children’s rate applies up to and including the age of 18 (an exclusive offer that eases the budget for large families or families with teenagers). A resort that thinks about your holidays… but also your wallet!

AND FOR DISABLED SKIERS
Holders of a disability card and their accompanying person each receive a 50% discount on the purchase of their pass at the standard rate (skier or pedestrian).

Please note that both passes must be purchased at the ticket office at the same time and must have the same duration, the same validity and cover the same ski area.

PRICES DESIGNED FOR ALL AGES
Senior rate from age 65, so you can continue to enjoy the pleasures of the slopes at a low price
Free ski passes from the age of 75, because there’s no age limit for enjoying a day on the slopes
AN EXCEPTIONAL SKI AREA

With more than 300 km of linked pistes between Tignes and Val d’Isère, modern ski lifts and guaranteed snow from November to May, you’re choosing much more than a ski pass: you’re choosing a recognised, innovative, high-altitude resort that welcomes everyone.
