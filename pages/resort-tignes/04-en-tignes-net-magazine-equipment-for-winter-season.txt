HOW TO CHOOSE THE RIGHT SOCKS FOR THE WINTER SEASON?

OK, you’re ready: brand new outfit, waxed skis, helmet, gloves and boots, everything has been checked to attack this new ski season in perfect conditions.

HOW TO CHOOSE YOUR SOCKS?

To choose the right pair of ski socks, you need to ask yourself a few questions.

1. WHAT IS MY SKIING LEVEL AND INTENSITY?

If I ski every weekend, if I’m an instructor for example, I wouldn’t choose the same socks as for an annual family ski holiday. In most cases, ski sock brands classify their models according to a degree of practice: the socks are thus adapted by their materials and construction. It is essential to choose socks that are adapted to your needs… Clearly, first-price socks will not be suitable for regular use, as they are offered in materials that are not very resistant and lack technical construction.

2. WHERE AM I GOING TO SKI, IN WHAT WEATHER CONDITIONS?

Depending on the time of year, the altitude of the slopes and their geographical location, temperatures will not necessarily be the same. This cold criterion must be taken into account when choosing socks, because the calorific value varies from model to model and from one material to another. Socks that provide a lot of warmth to the feet are very much appreciated in very cold weather, and will literally change your life!

3. WHAT TYPE OF SPORT WILL I BE DOING?

Winter in the mountains isn’t just about downhill skiing!

Depending on the activity you practice, you can adapt your pair of socks: cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, Nordic walking, sledding and many others! The technical constructions of the different models can be adapted to your needs: flat seams, moisture wicking, heat retention, non-compressive, mixed materials… There are many different technologies, and each sock model is designed to meet a certain number of criteria.

Now that you know which pair of socks is right for you, you need to know how to care for them. Ideally, you should wash them by hand, as that’s how they retain their characteristics for the longest possible time… If you’re in a hurry, machine washing at 30° is fine, but definitely not the tumble-dryer: it’s the sworn enemy of socks in general!

Last but not least, take your usual shoe size: it’s important that the sock fits your foot perfectly to avoid any feeling of rubbing. Most models are unisex and come in a wide range of sizes.
