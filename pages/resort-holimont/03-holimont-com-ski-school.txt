WHAT TO EXPECT
Confidence, Not Just Instruction
We’re not just teaching you how to ski. We’re building confidence, sharing joy, and making winter your favorite season. With small group sizes, experienced instructors, and a low-pressure vibe, HoliMont Ski School is the best place to learn, grow, and have a blast doing it.

PRIVATE COACHING & CLINICS
Private coaching & clinics that meet you where you are.

For over 60 years, HoliMont has invested in growing confident, lifelong skiers and snowboarders. From our youth race teams to adult clinics to adaptive instruction, we believe snow sports should be fun, accessible, and growth-oriented—for everyone.
