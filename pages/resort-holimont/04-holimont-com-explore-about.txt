For over 60 years we’ve been committed to creating an environment for generations of families and friends to adventure and build memories together.

Generations of members have been drawn in by the amazing skiing, only to fall in love with the warm, community atmosphere that makes HoliMont the kind of place you never want to leave.

And while a sense of community can’t be manufactured, snow’s a different story. Our patented snowmaking system creates 700 vertical feet of wall-to-wall, top-to-bottom snow. Combine that with an average of 180 inches of natural snow per season, and you’re guaranteed a great run every time.

HoliMont’s Three Uniques:

Family-Centered – Generations of HoliMont families have made memories here. The HoliMont community and all of our social events are geared to family.

Great Snow and No Lift Lines – Our grooming, snowmaking, and no waiting make for endless laps. And we do the same thing in the summer on dirt.

Freedom and Independence – Your kids are safe to explore hundreds of acres with their friends.
