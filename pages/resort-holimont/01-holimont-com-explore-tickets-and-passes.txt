10-Day Weekday Experience Pack

Looking for the most ski time at the best rate per day? The 10-Day Experience Pack offers more freedom than single tickets and more flexibility than membership.

WHAT’S INCLUDED:
10 weekday ski/ride visits (transferable days available in March)
Skip busy weekends and enjoy open trails during the week
Use across the season or bundle with other visit types
IDEAL FOR:
Seasoned guests with flexible midweek schedules
Parents, retirees, or those between memberships
Skiers who come often but not all season long
BUY NOW
5-Day Weekday Experience Pack

Just visiting HoliMont or only planning a few weekday trips? The 5-Day Experience Pack is the perfect introduction to our slopes, giving you midweek access without pressure, long lines, or long-term commitment.

WHAT’S INCLUDED:
5 weekday ski/snowboard visits during regular operating season
Access to all groomed terrain
A "starter" Pack made for trying out our community
IDEAL FOR:
Casual guests looking for a flexible entry point
First-time families testing HoliMont for a few days
Working professionals with limited weekday availability

2026 Bike Park Season Pass

Get a head start on next year’s mountain biking — and enjoy bonus access this fall. Our Bike Park Pass is your season-long ticket to HoliMont’s trails, flow zones, and summer scene.

WHAT’S INCLUDED:
Unlimited access to lift-served trails during the full 2026 season
On-site amenities including rentals, food, and camping
All-ages trail infrastructure and community summer events
IDEAL FOR:
Summer-only riders or multi-sport guests
First-timers getting a feel for the terrain
Passholders looking to lock in next year’s biking before winter
