Ski & snowboard rental

Where equipment meets expertise

More and more skiers choose to rent their skis, snowboards and equipment directly in Alta Badia. At the valley's ski rentals, you'll find professionals who help you choose the equipment best suited to your ability, your skiing style and the snow conditions, with expert advice designed to help you make the most of every day on the slopes.From the latest ski models to essential accessories, every piece of equipment is carefully prepared and adjusted to offer comfort, safety and performance from your very first run. Because choosing the right ski rental means starting your day with the equipment that's right for you.
