From the Sella Group to Sas dla Crusc - Santa Croce, enjoy Alta Badia with one sole ski pass

54 interconnected lift facilities, perfectly groomed slopes and the dreamlike Dolomites 

The Alta Badia ski pass allows you to ski comfortably on 130 piste kilometres stretching from the localities of Corvara, Colfosco, San Cassiano, La Villa and Badia to the mountain passes called Campolongo, Falzarego and Gardena. A total of 54 cable cars provides you with quick and comfortable transport, while state-of-the-art snow-making systems ensure the perfect conditions on the slopes - even when snowfall is scarce. From Piz Boé to Sassongher, from Sas dla Crusc to the Gran Risa: skiing in Alta Badia is a spectacular adventure. And with the price calculator of the Alta Badia ski pass you can make the most of your skiing holiday.

A new way to access the slopes

SmartPass

Your smartphone instead of a ski pass

With SmartPass, your smartphone becomes your ski pass: simple, immediate and always with you. An innovation that makes your time on the Dolomiti Superski slopes smoother and more effortless, so you can focus on enjoying the mountains.

Discover how it works
 

Online ski pass

Save time, save money and get straight onto the slopes

Benefit of a 5% discount by purchasing your one-day or multi-day ski pass online and no later than two days prior to use. After your purchase you’ll get a QR-Code which you can use to withdraw your My Dolomiti Card at the pick-up boxes of the ski pass offices. If you already hold a card it’s even easier as you can comfortably upload your ski pass from home and directly access the ski lifts once on-site, bypassing the queue at the cash desk. Moreover, since we are talking about a reusable My Dolomiti Card, you are also contributing to safeguarding the environment. Giulan. 

Your online ski pass
 

My Dolomiti Card

Rechargeable and personalized

The My Dolomiti Card is a personal and rechargeable card in credit card format, which comes with numerous benefits and eases your days on Alta Badia’s slopes. By uploading your tickets onto the card, you will have direct access to the ski lifts, enjoy a 5% discount on the ski pass, save your tracks and memorise your skiing performance. You can recharge the environmentally-friendly and reusable card comfortably online. Aren’t this some excellent reasons to get it?! 

Your My Dolomiti Card
 

Ikon Pass

The Dolomites in the world

Ikon Pass is a United States-based and global ski pass association, which also includes the Dolomiti Superski carousel and allows ski enthusiasts from all over the world to discover our ski paradise in the heart of the Dolomites. The Ikon Pass offers unlimited access to slopes and ski lifts in eighteen destinations in North America and Canada as well as up to seven days’ access in more than 40 international ski destinations – amongst them the largest ski carousel in the world, Dolomiti Superski.

Alta Badia: wherever you go, there's a ski pass office nearby 

You’ll find ski pass offices in every village of Alta Badia - Corvara, Colfosco, La Villa, San Cassiano and Badia - which are open every day of the week as of December and await you with qualified staff members. As an alternative you can also decide to purchase your ski pass online.
