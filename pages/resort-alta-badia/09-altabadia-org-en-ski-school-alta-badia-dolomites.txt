Seven ski schools, one skiing culture

In Alta Badia, skiing is part of every winter. The instructors of the valley's seven ski schools have grown up on these mountains and know every slope, every type of snow and every season. It is an expertise built over time and passed on with passion, experience and genuine care for every skier.

Whether you are making your first turns or looking to refine your technique, every lesson is tailored to your level, your pace and your goals, guiding you step by step. Every year, instructors take part in training and refresher courses to ensure the highest standards of teaching.

Among them are former athletes and coaches who have brought their experience from competitive skiing to the slopes of Alta Badia, making that knowledge accessible to skiers of all levels.

Freelance ski instructors in Alta Badia

If you are looking for a more personal approach, you can rely on Alta Badia's independent ski instructors. Through private lessons or sessions for small groups, they tailor every experience to your level, your goals and the way you want to enjoy your day on the snow.

From alpine skiing to snowboarding, they guide beginners and experienced skiers with expertise, care and a deep knowledge of the ski area, helping you enjoy the slopes of Alta Badia with greater confidence and awareness.

Safety on the slopes is part of the experience

Experiencing the mountains also means experiencing them with awareness. In Alta Badia, safety on the slopes is an essential part of every day on the snow. It starts with small gestures, respect for others and knowing the rules that help everyone enjoy the slopes with confidence. Whether it is your first day on skis or you are already an experienced skier, understanding the FIS rules, using the right equipment and adapting your skiing to the snow and slope conditions are the first steps towards enjoying every run safely and responsibly.
