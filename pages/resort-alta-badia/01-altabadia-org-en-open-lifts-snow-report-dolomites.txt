Everything you may need to know before strapping on your skis

The ski map, the lifts and the latest slope conditions: here you'll find all the information you need, updated in real time, to make the most of your day on the snow. For us, being there for everyone who chooses Alta Badia means providing clear, reliable and always up-to-date information.

 

04.12.2026 - 04.04.2027

In the mountains, the weather and snow conditions can change throughout the day. Here you'll find real-time updates on snow, slopes and lifts, so you have everything you need to plan your day on the snow, always at your fingertips. For us, up-to-date information also means greater peace of mind and safety for everyone enjoying the mountains.
