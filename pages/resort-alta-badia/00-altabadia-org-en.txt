Enrosadira Trail

10.10.2026

Autumn in Alta Badia sets the scene for a trail running experience through alpine trails, larch forests and landscapes transformed by the colours of the season.

More than a race, it is a journey to experience step by step, where effort, breath and rhythm become one with nature at the most intense moment of autumn.

Dolomites Low Emission Zone at Passo Gardena

To help preserve one of the Dolomites' most precious landscapes and improve mobility management, the Dolomites Low Emission Zone pilot project is being introduced in the Passo Gardena area. The initiative is the result of a collaboration between the Provinces of Bolzano, Trento and Belluno, together with the municipalities of the valleys surrounding the Sella Group.

From 22 September to 5 October 2026, the project will enter a digital testing phase.

Italian Competition and Market Authority
The commitments of Dolomiti Superski have been accepted

Dolomiti Superski has been informed by the Italian Competition and Market Authority of the outcome of the proceedings initiated last year. The Authority has deemed the commitments submitted by Dolomiti Superski to be appropriate. Starting 15 October 2026, eligible customers will be able to benefit from the measures provided for. Full details regarding the procedures and timelines will be communicated through Dolomiti Superski’s and Skiresort's official channels.
