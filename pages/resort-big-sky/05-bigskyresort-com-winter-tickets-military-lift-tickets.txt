MILITARY LIFT TICKETS FAQ
Open all
DO YOU HAVE A MILITARY DISCOUNT ON LIFT TICKETS OR SEASON PASSES?

We appreciate your service and are pleased to offer up to 40% off advanced online winter lift tickets for qualifying military members and their families aged 15 and up. For full details and eligibility, please see Military Validation page.

 

At this time, military discounts are not available on Big Sky Resort season passes, winter activities, or summer products, including the Freestyle Pass.

 

HOW DO I VALIDATE MY MILITARY ID?

Validate your military ID on our Military Validation page by entering your details into the form. You may be required to upload a photo of your unexpired Military ID, Dependent ID, or DD214. Once you have submitted the form, Sheer ID will email you a verification code to use during checkout.

Sheer ID military verification codes work for 1 ticket purchase. Each person must fill out the form to get their own unique code.

WHICH MILITARY STATUS IS ELIGIBLE FOR A DISCOUNT ON LIFT TICKETS?

Big Sky Resort proudly offers discounted lift tickets to our military service members.  To see if you qualify enter your information into the form on our Military Validation page. 

HOW MUCH DO MILITARY LIFT TICKETS COST?

Ski and snowboard lift ticket prices vary by day depending on the time of year. Active and retired military service members can save up to 40% on single-day lift tickets. You can find the current military lift ticket prices for ages 15 and up on our online store. Our online rates are cheaper than in person, so maximize your military discount by purchasing online in advance. 

DO I NEED SEPARATE MILITARY CODES FOR EACH PERSON?

Each person must verify their military status to get their own discount code. Military discount codes work for one person only. You cannot use one code more than once. Need discounted tickets for your family or group? Each qualifying person must complete verification separately. They will receive their own one-time discount code.  This ensures proper verification for all military discounts.
