Our seasonal programs offer the opportunity to improve your skills with other like-minded skiers. It's not just about skiing—you'll get the chance to build community on and around the mountain.

Join us for:

Welcome cocktail hour on January 6, 2027
Weekly coffee & pastries before each session
Expert instruction tailored to your goals and terrain
A supportive community of local skiers and riders
SEASONAL PROGRAM DETAILS
Seasonal programs will not be prorated for missed sessions.
Groupings of participants are based on each participant's skiing speed and the terrain they are currently skiing. Requests to switch groups must be made through the program coordinator.
Sessions will not be cancelled due to bad weather or conditions.
Sessions are non-transferable.
