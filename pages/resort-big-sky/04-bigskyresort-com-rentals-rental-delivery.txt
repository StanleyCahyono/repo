RENTAL DELIVERY

Skip the rental shop—get fitted for your ski & snowboard rentals in the comfort of your lodging. Free for guests in the Summit, Huntley Lodge, Shoshone, and Village Center hotels.

Request Rental Delivery

Save time by having your rentals delivered to your hotel or vacation rental in Big Sky! Book your rentals and then fill out our delivery request form.

Delivery is complimentary for guests staying in the Huntley Lodge, Summit Hotel, Shoshone, and Village Center hotels.

Delivery is $49/skier or rider. Servicing the Big Sky Mountain Village and Madison Base Areas.

RENTAL DELIVERY FAQS
Open all
HOW DO I SET UP RENTAL DELIVERY?

Make a rental delivery request through this form. An agent will respond 3-5 days before with delivery availability. Our direct line is (406) 995-5574.

WHERE DO YOU DELIVER RENTALS AND HOW MUCH DOES IT COST?

With Rental Delivery we can deliver rental equipment anywhere in Big Sky, just let us know where you are staying and we will come to you.

 

Delivery is $49 per skier or rider. Rental delivery is complimentary for guests of the Huntley Lodge, Village Center and Summit Hotel as well as for guests who have booked lessons or guides through Mountain Sports.

WHAT KIND OF EQUIPMENT DO YOU DELIVER?
With Rental Delivery we will deliver all of our rental and demo skis, boards, poles, helmets, and boots to your door.

WHEN DO YOU DELIVER RENTALS?
Rental Deliveries take place between 7:30 a.m. and 8:30 p.m. daily. We understand that traveling in the mountains can be unpredictable. If you are experiencing delays or need to make special arrangements, let us know and we will do our best to accommodate you.
