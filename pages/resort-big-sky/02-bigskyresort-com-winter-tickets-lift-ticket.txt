ARE LIFT TICKETS DATE SPECIFIC?

All winter lift tickets, Kircliff scenic tickets, and summer bike haul lift tickets are date-specific. 

 

For information on How multi-day winter lift tickets work or How to use your Freestyle season pass view these Help Center articles. 

HOW DO MULTI-DAY TICKETS WORK?

Our multi-day winter lift tickets give you the flexibility to use your tickets across a date range. The first number is the number of days you want to ski. The second number is the number of days you have to use your lift tickets. The start date is the time when your number of days starts—you don’t have to ski on the start date. 

 

For example, if you purchase a 4 of 5 day lift ticket starting on December 17, you have the flexibility to ski 4 of the 5 days between December 17-21. You can ski the days consecutively or skip a day.

HOW DO I PICK UP MY LIFT TICKET?

Lift tickets and season passes are loaded onto reusable Big Sky Resort Sky Cards. That can be used season after season. Scan your Sky Card at the lift gates to ride the lift. 

 

If you already have a Sky Card, reload the Sky Card at checkout and head straight to the lifts with your card. 

 

If you don’t have a Sky Card or need a new one, add a new Sky Card for $5 during checkout. When you arrive, can the QR code in your confirmation email to print your card any Sky Card Express Box. Find Sky Card Express Box locations and learn more about using your Sky Card on the mountain on our website. 

 

Big Sky Resort does not mail Sky Cards. 

CAN SOMEONE ELSE PICK UP MY LIFT TICKET FOR ME?
Someone else can pick up your lift ticket if you share the QR code in your confirmation email.

WHAT IS EASY CHANGE?

Easy Change is Big Sky Resort’s flexible change program for when your plans change after purchasing lift tickets, rentals, lessons, or activities online. Outside of 48 hours from the start date of your product, you can cancel your purchase for a full refund. Within 48 hours, we’ll convert the full dollar value of your purchase into a credit voucher valid for 365 days if documentation is provided for why you are unable to participate. Visit our Easy Change page for details.

DO YOU SELL OUT OF LIFT TICKETS?

Lift tickets for skiing, snowboarding, biking, and scenic riding, typically do not sell out. However, you will get the best price when you purchase lift tickets in advance, so it’s worth planning ahead.

 

Any lift ticket that is required to be purchased in-person will not sell out:

 

Big Sky Resort Season Passholder Discount Tickets (for blackout days)
Boyne Sister Resort Discounted Tickets 
Mountain Collective Discounted Tickets
Half-Day 12:30 PM Tickets (subject to availability during certain times of the season)
DO THE MAGIC CARPETS REQUIRE A LIFT TICKET?

You need a ticket to ride magic carpet lifts. 

DOES MY CHILD WHO IS UNDER 7 YEARS OLD NEED A LIFT TICKET?

All children, including those 6 and under, need a valid lift ticket and Sky Card to access the lifts year-round. A one-time $5 Sky Card purchase is required if you don’t already have one.

 

In winter, lift tickets for ages 6 & under are $1/day.  Parents with Big Sky Resort season passes can obtain a season pass for their child age 6 & under at the ticket window. For more info read the article "What season pass is available for kids 6 and under?"

In summer, bike haul and scenic lift ticket prices for ages 6 & under vary and can be found in our online store.
