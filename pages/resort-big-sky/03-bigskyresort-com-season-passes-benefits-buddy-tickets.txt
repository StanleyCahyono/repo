BIG SKY PASSHOLDER BUDDY TICKETS

Skiing is better with friends! Big Sky Resort Gold and Black Season Passes get discounted Buddy Tickets to share the Big Sky love. Use the instructions below to access your vouchers and share your Buddy Tickets online.

GOLD PASS

10 Buddy Tickets

BLACK PASS

4 Buddy Tickets

BOYNE PASSPORT

10 Buddy Tickets (valid at any Boyne Resort)

ACCESSING YOUR BUDDY TICKET VOUCHERS
Sign into your account in our online store.
Click on "Account" in the blue bar near the top of the page.
Under "My Account," select "Vouchers."
On the Vouchers page, you will see the vouchers connected to your account with their ID and expiration dates.
Select "email" and email it to your recipient, or give them the ID number attached to the individual voucher.

Find more detailed instructions in our Help Center.

PURCHASING A BUDDY TICKET
Get a Buddy Ticket Voucher ID number (instructions above) from your passholder friend.
Go to the Buddy Ticket product on the Big Sky Resort Shop.
Add your desired date(s) and proceed to checkout. Note that Buddy Tickets are good for one individual for one day each.
Assign your Buddy Ticket(s) to the appropriate group member(s). If you're buying tickets for someone not in your group, please give them the code so they can link their lift ticket to their profile.
Reload your Sky Card or add a new one.
Sign the waiver(s), then enter your Buddy Voucher Code, and click 'Validate'. If you are purchasing multiple Buddy Tickets, each ticket will need a unique voucher code.
Finish the checkout process.

If you choose to reload an existing Sky Card with your Buddy Ticket, you can go straight to the lifts on the day of your ticket. If you purchased a new Sky Card, you can pick it up from one of our Sky Card Express boxes.

More detailed instructions can be found in our Help Center.

Share the Big Sky love with your own Buddy Tickets. Get your Season Pass today.
