MILITARY LIFT TICKET DISCOUNTS

Verify your status and save up to 40% on lift tickets.

Big Sky Resort is proud to offer discounted lift tickets to military service members and their family members aged 15+.

Verified military members include active duty military, veteran or retiree, Reservists or National Guard, military families with an active military ID, and Gold Star families. Family members of veterans do not qualify for this offer.

Validate your status below and then use this link to purchase your tickets

1. VERIFY YOUR STATUS

Use the dialogue box below to enter your details. Big Sky validates your military ID using SheerID. Qualifying military members include active duty military, veteran or retiree, Reservist or National Guard, military families with an active military ID, and Gold Star families. Family members of veterans do not qualify for this offer.

2. RECEIVE YOUR DISCOUNT CODE

Upon verifying your military status, you will be given a one-time discount code to use on Big Sky's online shop. If you are looking to get discounted tickets for more than one qualifying person, a validation will have to be performed for each person, and they will receive their own code.

After 30 days, you can re-validate your ID for an additional code. We hope you take advantage of this offer throughout the season!

3. USE YOUR DISCOUNT CODE TO BUY LIFT TICKETS

With your discount code ready, shop Military Lift Tickets online. Add your tickets to your cart. If you need to purchase any other products, you can do so in this same transaction. Once you're ready, proceed to checkout. After you have assigned products and Sky Cards to members of your group, you will be asked to enter your discount codes to redeem your discounted lift tickets. The name on your validated ID must match the lift ticket holder. Finish checking out, and you're ready to go. We can't wait to see you on the mountain!

LIFT TICKET INFORMATION
LIFT TICKET CANCELLATION

Cancellations outside of 48 hours of the product's start date can be requested for a full refund. Within 48 hours, a credit may be given. See our Easy Change page for details.

HEAD TO THE LIFTS WITH A SKY CARD

If you already have a Sky Card from a previous visit, welcome back! You can load your new lift ticket or season pass onto the card at checkout, and you can head straight to the lifts when you arrive. If you’re not seeing your Sky Card in your account, see our Help Center for instructions on reconnecting it to your profile.
