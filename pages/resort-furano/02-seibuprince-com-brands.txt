MAUNA KEA BEACH HOTEL

For more than five decades, Mauna Kea Beach Hotel has stood firmly as the standard bearer of ho‘okipa (hospitality) on the Island of Hawai‘i. Developed by Laurance S. Rockefeller as the first resort hotel on the island, Mauna Kea Beach Hotel has welcomed generations of travelers to embrace the island’s spirit, the warmth of its people, and the rejuvenating capacity of the ocean that surrounds it.

THE WESTIN HAPUNA BEACH RESORT

The Westin Hapuna Beach Resort stretches along its namesake white-sand beach on the Island of Hawai‘i. The resort’s contemporary style, manicured gardens and all-ocean-facing guestrooms complement the magnificent Kohala Coast shoreline. Open-air dining venues, award-winning 18-hole Hapuna Golf Course, adult exclusive and family pools and this island of sunshine and sand, volcanoes and rainforests awaits discovery.

PRINCE WAIKIKI

Welcome to the Prince Waikiki, a deluxe hotel where every room features an ocean view
One wall of every guest room features a glass picture window, from which guests can enjoy the beautiful blue Pacific and the refreshing Hawaiian breeze. The hotel's infinity pool, with its view of the yacht harbor, and adjacent open-air club lounge are popular destinations. The Ala Moana Shopping Center is a mere five-minute walk away. The Prince Waikiki is conveniently located, with easy access to the popular Kakaʻako Farmers Market and the Ward Village, which is home to many fashionable shops.

THE GIBRALTAR BOWRAL

Located just a 90-minute drive from both Sydney and Canberra, The Gibraltar Bowral is perfect if you’re searching for Bowral accommodation.

The hotel offers premium accommodation with 78 rooms, including private balcony rooms, family rooms and suites. Guests can enjoy an 18-hole golf course, wellness facilities like a gymnasium and indoor swimming pool, dining at Marilo Restaurant and Harveys Bar, as well as well-equipped conference and event spaces, private wedding spaces and four on-site EV chargers for added convenience.

SANYO-SO

Sanyo-so is nestled at the foot of Mount Genji in Izu-Nagaoka. Built as a villa for Hisaya Iwasaki, the eldest son of Yataro Iwasaki (founder of the former Mitsubishi zaibatsu conglomerate), in a magnificent Japanese garden, Sanyo-so is a dignified and elegant Japanese-style residence built in the historic and prestigious Sukiya-zukuri architectural style.
