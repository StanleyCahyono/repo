Single and Multiday Passes

Whether you just want a half day on the slopes, or a multi-day holiday, you’ll need to purchase a ‘Mountain Access Card’ to use the lifts at Falls Creek. Pre-load your days skiing online onto your Mountain Access Card, and you’ll be ready to pass the automated gates to enter the lifts in a breeze.

A Mountain Access Card costs $6, however, these cards can be reloaded over the season and in future seasons. Replacement cards will also cost $6.

Handy Tip: Don't place your Mountain Access Card near your phone as this can interrupt the Mountain Access Card from scanning and the gate may not open.
