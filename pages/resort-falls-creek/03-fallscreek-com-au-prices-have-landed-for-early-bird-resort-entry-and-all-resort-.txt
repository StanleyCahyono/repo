Early Bird Resort Entry and All Resort Season Permits on Sale Monday 4th March
February 28, 2024
Get a head start on your winter planning. Check out the prices for the Early Bird Entry Permit or All Resort Season Permit which is back for winter 2024. On sale Monday 4th March!

As the snow season approaches, it’s time to start planning your alpine adventures. Whether you’re a loyal snow goer or planning your first trip to Victoria’s Alpine Resorts, getting your Season Resort Entry Permit sorted early can save you time and money.

Pricing for Resort Vehicle Permits 2024:

Falls Creek Early Bird Resort Entry Permit (Online Only) – $500
Falls Creek Resort Entry Permit (11th April onwards) – $599
All Resort Season Permit (Online Only) – $1078

Which Permit is Right for You?

Early Bird Resort Entry Permit: If you’re planning on visiting Falls Creek for 8 days or more, the Early Bird Resort Entry Permit is your best bet. Priced at $500, this permit offers great value and convenience for longer stays.
All Resort Season Permit: For the dedicated snow lovers who want to change it up for a day and try a new mountain in one of Victoria’s Alpine Resorts, the All Resort Season Permit is the perfect choice. Priced at $1078, this permit provides unlimited access to Falls Creek and other alpine resorts throughout the season.

Don’t miss out on these great deals! Visit our Resort Entry page for more information and to secure your permit for winter 2024. Start planning your snow adventures now and make the most of the upcoming snow season!

Visitors with questions regarding the Early Bird Sale or Vehicle Resort Entry Season Permits for the 2024 Snow Season can see the Resort Entry FAQs or alternatively email info.fallscreek@alpineresorts.vic.gov.au or call 03 5758 1200.
