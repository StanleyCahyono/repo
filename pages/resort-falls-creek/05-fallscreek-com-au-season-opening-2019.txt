WELCOME TO SNOW SEASON 2019

On Saturday June 8th Falls Creek officially cuts the ribbon to open season 2019. We’ve all been waiting patiently since last October to get back on the snow and now the time is finally here!

Strong pre-season snowfall and excellent snowmaking conditions have paved the way for 7 lifts to turn on day one of the season, two lifts more than the number of opening weekend lifts in the record-breaking 2000 snow season! Across the resort there is an average 47cm of natural snow and snowmaking areas are much deeper! With such an amazing base this weekend is set to be the biggest yet with great snow, loads of terrain and magical times to be had.

Head to the village Bowl from 6:30pm Saturday for a massive fireworks display to welcome the season with the rest of Falls Creek!
