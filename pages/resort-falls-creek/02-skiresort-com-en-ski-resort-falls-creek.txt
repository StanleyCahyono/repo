For skiing and snowboarding, there are 49 km of slopes available. 15 lifts transport the guests. The winter sports area is situated between the elevations of 1,500 and 1,780 m.

The Falls Creek ski resort, located in the Begong High Plains within the Alpine National Park, was established in 1951. It offers slopes for all levels of difficulty.
