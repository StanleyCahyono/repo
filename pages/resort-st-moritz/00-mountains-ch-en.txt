ENGADIN ST. MORITZ MOUNTAINS

As the mountain railway company, Engadin St. Moritz Mountains, we operate cable cars and mountain experiences on both the Corviglia and Muottas Muragl mountains. While the Corviglia is a true paradise for winter sports enthusiasts and bikers, the active yet more relaxed guests particularly appreciate the tranquil panoramic views and culinary delights on Muottas Muragl. Discover your personal favorite place in our two mountain worlds. 

SUMMER ACTIVITIES

With a wide variety of bike routes, hiking trails, trail running routes, children's playgrounds, downhill scooter and plenty of sun­­ – our mountains Corviglia and Muottas Muragl are a real summer hotspot. Everything you need to know for your perfect summer day in the mountains can be found here.
