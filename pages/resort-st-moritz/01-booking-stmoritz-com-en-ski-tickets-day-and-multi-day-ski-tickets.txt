SNOW-DEAL - DAY AND HALF DAY SKI TICKETS

Book early and benefit.

Buy your ski ticket now and benefit from the early bird discount thanks to the dynamic "Snow Deal"  pricing model. In addition, you can benefit from attractive family discounts.

Day and multi-day tickets valid for the "Entire Upper Engadine" include free transportation by public transport.

Snow sports instructors with a valid ID receive a 33% reduction on their ski ticket.

With a valid card you get a 50% discount on tickets (1 to 14 days) in the following ski resorts: Livigno, Bormio, S. Caterina Valfurva, Scuol, Samnaun, Val Müstair, Casaccia in the Val Bregaglia, Bivio, Bergün, Atzmännig, Wanlong Ski Resort (CN).

Book as a family

If you have 2 or more children and ski passes for 3 to 15 days or an ENGADINcard 365 / a PIZcard 365, you will receive a discounted family card. For this, please fill out this form, have it certified by your municipality of residence and upload it during the booking process. Alternatively, you can also upload your family booklet. A family is defined by all family members (1 or 2 legal guardians and their children up to the 18th birthday) living in the same household.
