PREPARE FOR THE MOUNTAIN

Clothing & Accessories

We offer a range of clothing and accessories to keep you warm, dry and comfortable in alpine conditions. Jackets, pants, helmets and goggles are available to rent, giving you the flexibility to add what you need to your ski or snowboard hire.

For a streamlined option, choose the Complete Bundle. It combines your skis, boots and poles or snowboard, bindings and boots with a jacket, pants, goggles and a helmet, everything you need for the mountain in one simple booking.

Please note that gloves are not included in rental packages, but are available to purchase for $30 from our rental locations.

Book Complete Rental Bundle
Pick Up and Drop Off

We offer convenient rental collection both on and off the mountain.

Rentals can be collected on-mountain at Cardrona Alpine Resort or Treble Cone Ski Area from 8.30am daily. Collecting on the mountain means you can get fitted and head straight to the lifts.

Off-mountain collection is also available at our town locations, 50 Cardrona Road, Wānaka and 37 Camp Street, Queenstown. Off-mountain collection is available the day before your booking from 12:00pm in Queenstown and 3:00pm in Wānaka.

All rental equipment must be returned to the same location where it was collected, unless otherwise arranged in advance.

Please note: Premium Rentals must be picked up and dropped off on the mountain.

We recommend arriving early during peak periods and allowing time for fitting.
