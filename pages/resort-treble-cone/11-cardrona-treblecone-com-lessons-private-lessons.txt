Why is a Private Lesson is right for you?

Private lessons are perfect for anyone who wants focused, one-on-one coaching tailored exactly to their goals. Whether you’re a first timer finding your feet or a confident skier or rider refining advanced skills, your instructor builds the session around you - your ability, your experience, and what will make the biggest difference to your day on the mountain.

With a private lesson, the time is yours. You can progress at your own pace, build confidence, have fun and focus on exactly what will help you improve each run. Many guests choose to book a private lesson early in their holiday so the rest of their trip feels smoother, easier, and more enjoyable.

Premium Private Lessons

Premium Private Lessons go beyond coaching, creating a seamless mountain experience from first lift to last run.

Premium Experience (for 2 people): A concierge-style experience for pairs who want priority access and personalised service throughout the day. Includes a table reservation and a priority car park. (Available at Cardrona only).

Premium Instructor Training Private (for 4 people). World-class coaching from qualified trainer, designed to help you increase your performance, or get you ready for your next exam. (Choose Cardrona or Treble Cone).

Premium Family Private (for 4 people): A tailored experience combining private coaching with added convenience and flexibility. (Choose Cardrona or Treble Cone).

What’s included:

Bespoke concierge-style planning before your lesson and assistance through the rental process

Priority chairlift access

Private locker (Premium Experience or Premium Family only)

Add-on friends & family at a discounted rate (Premium Family or ITC Private only)
