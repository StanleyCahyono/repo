Who is the Adaptive Programme for?

Our adaptive programme is designed for guests with physical, cognitive or sensory disabilities.

Adaptive private lessons with a qualified adaptive instructor, offering individually tailored one-on-one coaching and a plan that matches your learning style

Group lessons with volunteer support ideal if you or your child enjoys learning in a group environment but would benefit from some additional assistance

Volunteer support sessions designed for guests who can already ski or ride but would like extra on-snow support (complimentary for SSNZ members)

With the right support in place, you can expect a safe, encouraging environment, greater independence on the mountain, and the thrill of moving confidently on snow.

You’ll leave feeling capable, included, and excited to keep progressing.

MULTI-DAY PROGRAMME

Ride Tribe Adaptive

Ride Tribe Adaptive is a multi-day programme designed for anyone age 7+ who wants to grow their skills and confidence on snow.

Running over five weeks, this programme blends structured skill development with mountain adventure and plenty of fun. Guests build their skiing or snowboarding ability while learning alongside the same instructor and group each week.

With a consistent team and supportive coaching, skiers and riders develop confidence, independence and strong mountain skills, all in an environment that feels encouraging and enjoyable.
