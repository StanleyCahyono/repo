SUMMARY

Sunday 04/10

We are Open today with a Sunday Bluebird banger on the cards!

We will have firm snow conditions underfoot to start with warm temps increasing throughout the day making for some classic springy riding conditions!

The forecast for today is for a fine day and light winds so come on up for a rip!

We have the ANC Big Air Finals on today! This will be some great viewing to see our local athletes and international athletes compete.

The Olympic Pipe will have a delayed opening today - As we let it soften and salt it before opening!

And congrats to our Cardrona x TC Ice Hockey Team the Penguins for bringing the cup home last night in their game against NZSKI!

Friendly reminder, we have switched to our spring Pines Shuttle and bus schedule!

Road hazard advisory: Cardrona and Treble Cone access roads

Access to and from our mountains is via alpine roads, which present hazards including ice, snow, rockfall, landslides, steep gradients, and sweeping corners. These hazards are signposted wherever possible; however, alpine road conditions can change quickly and without warning. It is essential that you check the latest weather and road reports before you travel, drive to the conditions — including reducing speed where required — and fit snow chains when conditions call for it.

In areas identified as high risk for rockfall or landslide, please obey all "no stopping" signage and keep moving through these zones. Please follow all posted signage, speed limits, chain requirements, and any instructions from Cardrona/Treble Cone/RealNZ staff. Some hazards, such as rockfall and landslides, are largely unpredictable, and residual risk remains despite the mitigations in place.
