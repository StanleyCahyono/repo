6-PACK TICKETS
25-26 6-PACK TICKETS|BUY NOW

On Sale Now! West Mountain’s popular 6-Pack Tickets (Buy 5 Tickets, Get One Free) for winter skiing and tubing and summer aerial treetop (high ropes course) make great gifts! 6-Packs are fully transferable to family and friends and are available in Digital and Physical form. Skiing and Tubing 6-Packs are on sale from Nov. 1-Feb. 1. Aerial Adventure 6-Packs are on slae Nov. 1-July 1.

Ski/Ride Winter 6-Pack Details:

On sale Nov. 1-Feb. 1 for the 25-26 season. Our fully transferrable ski/ride 6-pack tickets are available in Digital and Physical form. Digital tickets will be emailed to you (please see details above). If purchasing physical ticket books, please indicate when checking out if you would like them mailed or if you would like to pick them up at the front desk. Please note that 6-packs can take 7-10 business days to arrive in the mail, and 2-3 business days to be processed for pick up at the front desk. Each physical ticket must be redeemed at the ticket window upon arrival and do not require a reservation in advance. Digital tickets can be booked online. Tickets are valid during the 25-26 winter season.

Tubing Winter 6-Pack Details:

On sale Nov. 1-Feb. 1 for the 25-26 season. Our fully transferable tubing 6-pack tickets are available in Digital and Physical form. Digital tickets will be emailed to you (please see details above). If purchasing physical tubing tickets, please note that they will be mailed and that they can take 7-10 business days to arrive in the mail, and 2-3 business days to be processed for pick up at the front desk. Each physical tubing ticket requires a reservation in advance by calling the front desk at 518-636-3699 ext. 0. Digital tickets can be booked in advance online. Tickets are valid during the 25-26 winter season.

Aerial Adventure Summer 6-Pack Details:

On sale Nov. 1-June 1 for summer 2026. Our fully transferable aerial adventure 6-pack tickets are available in Digital and Physical form. Digital tickets will be emailed to you (please see details above). If purchasing physical aerial park tickets, please note that they will only be mailed and that they can take 7-10 business days to arrive in the mail, and 2-3 business days to be processed for pick up at the front desk. Each physical aerial park ticket requires a reservation in advance by calling the front desk at 518-636-3699 ext. 0. Digital tickets can be booked in advance online. Tickets are valid during the 2026 summer season.

** NEW **

In addition to physical 6-Pack tickets, we will be offering Digital 6-Pack tickets that can be redeemed online and pre-reserved for a day of your choosing in the future. The 6 digital vouchers will be sent to you via the email provided as the "purchaser" to be distributed to whom the "purchaser" chooses. Instructions on how to use the digital vouchers are provided to the guest on the voucher in the "To Redeem Online" section. These instructions should be read and followed very carefully to prevent any error or inconvenience to yourself or chosen guests.
