PRIVATE RENTALS
PRIVATE TUBING/AERIAL (HIGH ROPES) ADVENTURE PARK RENTALS

Rent out the Tubing Park (in the winter) or the Aerial Treetop (High Ropes) Adventure Park (in the summer) for your next company event. Tubing and Climbing can be a great way to build relationships while having fun! Whether you need internal team building, have a cause for celebration or simply need to take a laugh break. Large groups of 100 or more  can rent out the Tubing Park or Aerial Adventure Park exclusively for your Group  for two hours! If you don’t have 100 people in your group, no problem. You can reserve the tubing park or aerial treetop adventure park for a corporate event with less than 100 for 2 hours, but pay for 100. Please allow an hour for chairlift transport to the Aerial Treetop Adventure Park and back to the lodge. We require payment at time of booking and we do not issue refunds.

Contact a Group Sales Representative at west@westmountain.com or 518-636-3699 ext. 316. Allow us to plan your next event.
