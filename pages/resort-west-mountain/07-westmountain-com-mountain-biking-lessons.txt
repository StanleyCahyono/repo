SUMMER GUIDING SERVICES
GUIDING SERVICES | RESERVE IN ADVANCE AT WEST@WESTMOUNTAIN.COM

West Mountain offers Private and Semi-Private 2-hour aerial treetop adventure guiding services to all ability levels, ages 10 and older. Anyone receiving as  aerial treetop adventure guiding service must purchase a ticket online at time of lesson booking. Please contact west@westmountain.com to book your aerial treetop guiding service at least 24 hours in advance. We will take a credit card number at the time of confirmation to hold your lesson time. Your credit card will be charged if you do not show.  We do not issue refunds.

Private Aerial Treetop Adventure Guide (one person and one instructor)
2 Hour: $90
Price does not include ticket.

Semi-Private Aerial Treetop Adventure Guide (two-five people from same family and one instructor)

2 Hour: $70 pp
Price does not include ticket.
