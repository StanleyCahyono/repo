INDY PASS TICKET DETAILS:

To redeem an Indy Pass ticket (Holiday & non-Holiday) you have to present the Indy Pass or photo ID at the ticket window upon arrival, so we can look you up in the Indy Pass system and give you your complimentary tickets (2 per Indy Pass Holder).
