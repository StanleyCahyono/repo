SKI TICKETS & SKI PASS PRICES
WINTERSKI TICKETSSki tickets
Find here all relevant facts about the ski ticket categories and ski pass prices. Ski tickets are valid at all lifts in the Ticket alliance ALPIN CARD - Skicircus Saalbach Hinterglemm Leogang Fieberbrunn, Schmittenhöhe in Zell am See and Kitzsteinhorn Kaprun! Here everyone will find a suitable ski ticket for a perfect day on the slopes in Salzburg: one-day and multi-day tickets, group tickets and much more. Tickets can be purchased directly at the ticket counters and are also available at several hotel reception desks, sport shops and ski schools. Easy purchase: get your ski tickets online.
