WINTERSki resort
Almost endless pistes, snow-covered slopes and a fascinating landscape. The Skicircus Saalbach Hinterglemm Leogang Fieberbrunn offers skiing fun at the highest level. With 270 kilometres of ski slopes, it is one of the largest ski areas in Austria. Beginners, families and experts can use 140 km blue, 112 km red and 18 km of black slopes. With 70 cableways and lifts, more than 60 cosy ski huts, numerous snow parks, a freeride park, floodlight slopes and toboggan runs, Salzburg's skiing region is one of the most diverse and best ski areas in the Alps.
 

Ski ALPIN CARD: 1 ticket for 3 premium regions and up to 408 kilometres of slopes!
With the Ski ALPIN CARD - the ticket association with the Schmittenhöhe in Zell am See and the Kitzsteinhorn Kaprun - you can, in addition to the extensive offer of the Ski Circus Saalbach Hinterglemm Leogang Fieberbrunn, enjoy the fabulous views of the Zeller See and breathe high Alpine air on the glacier.

SKI RENTAL SKI SCHOOLS
By merging Saalbach Hinterglemm Leogang with Fieberbrunn, we created a real mecca for freeriding: The Skicircus offers a freeriding park for freeride enthusiasts, numerous ski routes with different levels of difficulty, information point, avalanche transceiver search fields & check points and much more. Whether you're a freerider, skier or snowboarder - after a day of exercise on the slope a relaxed stop is necessary. In the Skicircus Saalbach Hinterglemm Leogang Fieberbrunn, more than 60 ski huts are available for a relaxed timeout. The ski hut keepers indulge their guests with local delicacies and true hut classics.
ACTION & FUN IN THE SKICIRCUS

Apart from classic skiing and snowboarding sports on the perfectly prepared slopes, the Skicircus offers even more opportunities for alternative fun in the snow guaranteeing unforgettable moments during your skiing holidays. Please note: During pre-season & off-season, not all offers can be provided!
