Activities Report
Sat. October 3rd & Sun October 4th

The vibrant colors of autumn are putting on a show across the mountain, just in time for our Leaf-Peeping Gondola Ride weekends! This weekend, the Northwoods Gondola will be open for Scenic Skyrides on Saturday and Sunday, 10am – 4pm. Take in the scenery, enjoy a packed lunch, and explore hiking opportunities at the 3,200 ft summit of Bear Mountain. While you’re here, don’t forget to swing by the Gore Mountain Retail Shop to gear up for winter, or grab a bite at Open Pit Grille!

Gondola Skyrides will be running through Harvest Fest weekend, happening Oct. 10th – 11th. Enjoy the mountain around peak foliage colors and celebrate the fall with an artisan vendor market, live music and entertainment, food and drinks, and more for all ages! Admission is free at this annual, favorite event.

FALL 2026 OPERATIONS
Come enjoy the fall foliage on our Scenic Skyrides! Our Northwoods Gondola will be open for Skyrides on Saturdays and Sundays, September 26th – October 11th. Hiking trails are open from the main base area as well. Got your 2026/27 Season Pass? You can start using it now for free access to scenic skyrides!

SAFETY & POLICIES

ANIMAL POLICY
While we love our pets, we only permit service animals/service animals in training on our property. No other animals will be permitted. We appreciate your cooperation. Click here for more info on ORDA’s Service Animal Policy.

SAFETY & POLICIES

CHAIRLIFT SCENIC SKYRIDES
All chairlift passengers must be at least 52″ tall, able to sit up on their own, and be able to raise and lower the restraint bar under their own power. There is no food service or restrooms at the top of the skyride.
