Convenient Jacket & Pants Rentals

Gear up like the pros with the all-new full-outfit Gore-Tex winter jacket and pant rental options. Test out the Gore-Tex technology material without having to splurge on a brand-new jacket or pants. Play in style and comfort at The Beast with Gore-Tex's "Keep you dry" guarantee.

Gore-Tex rentals will make going on a ski vacation the easiest that it's ever been. No need to overpack your suitcase with a snow jacket and snow pants or purchase your own if you don't have them. Reserve your jacket or pant rental online and a fully cleaned and inspected outfit will be ready there at the Snowshed or K-1 Lodge rental shop for you to pick up!

Gore-Tex rentals are available in Women's XS-XXL and Men's Small-3XL. Sorry, Youth sizes are currently not available for rent.

Gore-Tex Jacket

This durable, partially insulated GORE‑TEX Pro jacket is the next level of on-mountain outerwear. This shell is entirely seam-sealed with 3-Layer GORE-TEX Pro fabric plus strategically placed comfort mapped Primaloft® Silver insulation. It delivers professional performance through all weather conditions with breathability, style, comfort, and safety.

Fit: Relaxed fit, intended to be layered with a base layer and mid-layer insulation
3L GORE‑TEX Pro: fabric provides 100% waterproofing, windproofing, and breathability
Primaloft® Silver Insulation: is comfort mapped in critical areas to deliver comfort against the chill
Adjustable Hood: powered by Cohesive Drawcord System, fully adjustable storm hood fits over most helmets
Gore-Tex Pants

Constructed with 3L GORE‑TEX Pro fabric and comfort mapped PrimaLoft® Silver insulation, these pants will keep you protected from the elements, comfortable, and ensure enjoyable riding from dawn to dusk.

3L GORE‑TEX Pro: fabric provides 100% waterproofing, windproofing, and breathability
Primaloft® Silver Insulation: is comfort mapped in critical areas to deliver comfort against the chill
Anti-Scuff Cuffs: reinforces the bottom lower and inner leg for increased durability
Fully Taped Seams: waterproof and increases durability
Thigh Vents: on the exterior and interior enable quick ventilation - heat dump and moisture management
Cleaning & Sanitation

All Gore-Tex outerwear will go through a 12-point inspection before and after usage to keep the gear fresh and clean for all guests. Gore-Tex rental outerwear will be tagged with a QR code to ensure the gear has gone through all necessary sanitation stages. Every single garment, every single time.

How do I purchase a season pass?

Winter season passes can be purchased online at killington.com/passes. Summer season passes can be purchased online a killington.com/summer-passes.

Is there parking at the resort?

Free parking is available at all Killington Resort base lodges. In the winter, premium parking is available at K-1 & Snowshed Lodges.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome in outdoor areas and on select pet-friendly trails during the non-ski season. Service animals are welcome year-round. Please review our Pet Policy for full details. Please note that animals are not permitted on gondolas or chairlifts at any time.
