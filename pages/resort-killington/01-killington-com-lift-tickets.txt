LIFT TICKET PICKUP — QUICK, EASY & TOUCHLESS

How to Pick Up Your Ticket

When you purchase a new One Pass RFID card, follow these steps for a smooth lot-to-lift experience. If you've already reloaded an existing One Pass, you may proceed directly to the lift — no kiosk visit required. Check your reload status before you go.

Step 1: Save your order confirmation email with a QR Code. The QR Code is what you'll need at one of our kiosks to obtain your ticket(s) for contactless pickup.

Step 2: When you arrive at the resort you'll find kiosks conveniently located at each base lodge. Snowshed and Ramshead kiosks are outside, adjacent to the ticket window and are open 24 hours. Kiosks at K-1, Bear and Skyeship are located inside the lodges.

Step 3: Using your phone or printed email, scan the QR Code from your confirmation email at the kiosk. Child (6 and under) and Super Senior (80+) tickets must be picked up at a ticket window on the first floor of our K-1 Lodge.

Step 4: The kiosk will print all tickets in your order in the form of RFID cards. If you purchased only day tickets, all are included in this one card. You may head straight to the lift with your card for subsequent days.

Step 5: Put your RFID card ticket somewhere safe — a zippered pocket works best — and head for the lift. Be sure to save your RFID card so you can easily reload it for your next visit.

VERMONT & NEW HAMPSHIRE DAYS
Discounted Lift Tickets for Vermont & New Hampshire Residents

Vermont and New Hampshire residents get the biggest savings on The Beast — on Tuesdays, Wednesdays, and Sundays. Online purchase required. Present proof of residency at a ticket window on arrival.

Step 1: Purchase your Vermont or New Hampshire Day ticket online.

Step 2: Present your order confirmation and a VT or NH Driver's License — or similar state-issued proof of residency — at any ticket window on arrival. Utility bills and college IDs are not accepted as proof of residency.

A visit to the ticket window is required even when reloading a ONE PASS. No ticket windows at Bear Mountain Monday through Thursday.

MILITARY LIFT TICKETS
Discounted Lift Tickets for U.S. Military

All Active Duty, Retired, and Honorably Discharged members of the U.S. Military are eligible for discounted Killington lift tickets. Tickets must be purchased online. Verify military status through SheerID at checkout.

BUY MILITARY LIFT TICKETS

Eligibility

All Active Duty, Retired and Honorably Discharged members of the U.S. Military.

The discount is valid for the eligible member only. It cannot be used to purchase discounted tickets for non-military friends or family members.

How do I purchase a season pass?

Winter season passes can be purchased online at killington.com/passes. Summer season passes can be purchased online a killington.com/summer-passes.

Is there parking at the resort?

Free parking is available at all Killington Resort base lodges. In the winter, premium parking is available at K-1 & Snowshed Lodges.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome in outdoor areas and on select pet-friendly trails during the non-ski season. Service animals are welcome year-round. Please review our Pet Policy for full details. Please note that animals are not permitted on gondolas or chairlifts at any time.
