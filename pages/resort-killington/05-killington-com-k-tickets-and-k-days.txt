Are K-Tickets Right for You?

K-Ticket Vouchers are built for flexible skiers. If your schedule shifts, your voucher moves with you — valid on any day of the 2026/27 season. They're one price for all ages, which makes them easy for groups with a mix of adults and kids.

They're not the right fit for everyone. K-Tickets cannot be re-sold, canceled, returned, or extended. If you've locked in specific dates and need cancellation protection, date-specific lift tickets may be a better match — those are covered under our lift ticket cancellation policy and include age-based pricing for youth and seniors.

One more thing to know: kids 12 and under ski free with the purchase of a 5+ day adult date-specific ticket. That offer does not apply to K-Ticket Vouchers.

K-Ticket Voucher Details

Purchase an unlimited number of 1-day K-Ticket Vouchers to use yourself or share with friends and family any day during the 2026/27 season.

After purchase, vouchers are available in your online account. Redeem them for yourself, assign them to an account member, or share them with anyone via email.

K-Ticket Vouchers are only available for purchase through December 25, 2026.

Once a K-Ticket Voucher is redeemed or assigned to a person's One Pass RFID card, it cannot be transferred to someone else.

We're no longer offering the buy 7, get the 8th free K-Ticket voucher promotion.

Are K-Days Passes Right for You?

K-Days Passes are built for skiers and riders planning to visit Killington for two, three, or four days during the 2026/27 season. The four-day option offers the best value at $111 per day.

Planning to ski or ride Killington more often? Consider an Unlimited Winter Pass for unlimited access throughout the winter season, plus exclusive passholder perks and discounts.

K-Days Pass Details
K-Days Passes are assigned to one skier or rider at time of purchase. They cannot be transferred or shared. All days must be used by the person the pass is assigned to.
A recent photo is required on file. Check your photo status in the Group Members section of your online account. Photos are stored in lift access systems only — they will not be printed on RFID cards. Photos cannot be taken on-site.
At checkout, choose to reload the pass onto an existing One Pass RFID card or purchase a new card to pick up from a kiosk.
Days may be used any time during the 2026/27 season and do not need to be used consecutively.
Available for purchase through October 15, 2026.
K-Days Passes cannot be upgraded to a season pass.

For the most up-to-date information on Killington Resort's operating hours, visit our Mountain Report.

How do I purchase a season pass?

Winter season passes can be purchased online at killington.com/passes. Summer season passes can be purchased online a killington.com/summer-passes.

Is there parking at the resort?

Free parking is available at all Killington Resort base lodges. In the winter, premium parking is available at K-1 & Snowshed Lodges.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome in outdoor areas and on select pet-friendly trails during the non-ski season. Service animals are welcome year-round. Please review our Pet Policy for full details. Please note that animals are not permitted on gondolas or chairlifts at any time.
