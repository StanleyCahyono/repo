LIFT TICKET PICKUP — QUICK, EASY & TOUCHLESS

How To Pick Up Your Ticket

When you purchase a new One Pass RFID card, follow these steps for a smooth lot-to-lift experience. If you've already reloaded an existing One Pass, you may proceed directly to the lift — no kiosk visit required. Check your reload status before you go.

Step 1: Save your order confirmation email with a QR Code. The QR Code is what you'll need at one of our kiosks to obtain your ticket(s) for contactless pickup.

Step 2: When you arrive at the resort you'll find kiosks conveniently located at each base lodge. Snowshed and Ramshead kiosks are outside, adjacent to the ticket window and are open 24 hours. The Killington Grand Resort Hotel is inside the lobby across the room from the front desk.

Step 3: Using your phone or printed email, scan the QR Code from your confirmation email at the kiosk. Child (6 and under) and Super Senior (80+) tickets must be picked up at a ticket window on the first floor of our K-1 Lodge.

Step 4: The kiosk will print all tickets in your order in the form of RFID cards. If you purchased only day tickets, all are included in this one card. You may head straight to the lift with your card for subsequent days.

Step 5: Put your RFID card ticket somewhere safe — a zippered pocket works best — and head for the lift. Be sure to save your RFID card so you can easily reload it for your next visit.

EQUIPMENT REQUIREMENTS

What to Bring & Ride
Gear & Clothing
Helmets are required for all riders. Full-face helmets are strongly recommended and are available for rent.
Closed toe and heel shoes are required. No sandals, flip-flops, or other similar footwear permitted.
Bike Requirements
All bikes are required to have front and rear hand brakes. Disc brakes are strongly recommended.

All bikes must have front suspension at minimum. No rigid frames are permitted. Full suspension bikes are recommended and available for rent.
Bikes must have a tire diameter of 3" or less. Bikes with larger tires will not fit in our racks securely.
Only one rider per bike is permitted on lifts and trails.
Tandem bikes and front and rear mounted child seats are not permitted.
E-Bike Rules
Pedal-assist e-bikes with front or full suspension are allowed on the Ramshead and Snowshed lifts and downhill trails.
E-bikes are not allowed on the K-1 Express Gondola.
For uphill travel, e-bikes are only permitted on the Off the Top route with a valid trail pass.

K-1 Gondola Operations & Maintenance

This summer, the K-1 Gondola operated primarily on Friday through Sunday, shifting from last year’s Thursday through Sunday schedule to provide additional time for planned lift maintenance.

The work is part of Killington’s broader $1.3 million investment in lift maintenance projects across the resort. This summer marks the first phase of a larger, two-phase project on the K-1 Gondola, with crews completing targeted maintenance on key components, including acceleration and deceleration systems, drive and brake systems, and other reliability-focused upgrades.

Phase 2 of the project is planned for summer 2027 and will require the K-1 Gondola to be closed for the majority of the summer while more extensive work is completed. Together, these improvements are an important investment in the long-term reliability of the gondola and the guest experience for years to come. Additional details about summer 2027 operations will be shared as plans are finalized.

For this season, the K-1 Gondola will continue daily fall operations through October 12.

REGIONAL RIDING

90+ Miles Beyond the the Park

In addition to everything we offer at Killington Bike Park, the Killington region offers over 90 miles of non-lift serviced mountain bike trails. Plan to spend a few days with us to get the full Killington mountain bike experience.

Here are a few of our favorite local networks, whether you're looking to get an early morning pedal in before hitting the park or want to take a day to earn your downhill.

Killington Mountain Bike Club — Two separate but interconnected networks. Sherburne Trails and Base Camp Trails are located right in the town of Killington.

Pine Hill Park — Mostly technical singletrack in Rutland. 15 miles west of Killington.

Woodstock Area Mountain Bike Association — Three unique trail networks in Woodstock. 20 miles east of Killington.

How do I purchase a season pass?

Winter season passes can be purchased online at killington.com/passes. Summer season passes can be purchased online a killington.com/summer-passes.

Is there parking at the resort?

Free parking is available at all Killington Resort base lodges. In the winter, premium parking is available at K-1 & Snowshed Lodges.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome in outdoor areas and on select pet-friendly trails during the non-ski season. Service animals are welcome year-round. Please review our Pet Policy for full details. Please note that animals are not permitted on gondolas or chairlifts at any time.
