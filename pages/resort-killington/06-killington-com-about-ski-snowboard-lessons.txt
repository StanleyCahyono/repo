About Ski & Snowboard Lessons
Find Your Program
From First Turns to Expert Skills

This is where skiers and snowboarders of all abilities—from the first day to the 50th season—come to level up. Killington Mountain Sports School exists to nurture progression, turning seemingly impossible goals into easily achievable steps. Get the most out of Killington with a ski or snowboard program suited to fit your unique needs, no matter your ability level. When you join one of our award-winning lesson programs, you will refine your skills, gain confidence, and have fun on the mountain. 

Here at Killington, we know there’s always more to learn, whether it’s your first day on snow, or your hundredth. The Killington Mountain Sports School offers lessons for every level and seasonal programs to show you the best of east coast skiing and riding.

Pick Your Path. Book Your Spot.

Questions? We’ve got answers.

What are the resort operating hours?

For the most up-to-date information on Killington Resort's operating hours, visit our Mountain Report.

How do I purchase a season pass?

Winter season passes can be purchased online at killington.com/passes. Summer season passes can be purchased online a killington.com/summer-passes.

Is there parking at the resort?

Free parking is available at all Killington Resort base lodges. In the winter, premium parking is available at K-1 & Snowshed Lodges.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome in outdoor areas and on select pet-friendly trails during the non-ski season. Service animals are welcome year-round. Please review our Pet Policy for full details. Please note that animals are not permitted on gondolas or chairlifts at any time.
