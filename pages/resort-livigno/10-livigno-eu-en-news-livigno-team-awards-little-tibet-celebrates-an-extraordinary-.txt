LIVIGNO TEAM AWARDS: LITTLE TIBET CELEBRATES AN EXTRAORDINARY SEASON

04/04/2025

The entire community came together to celebrate its champions amidst medals and Crystal Globes.

Livigno, 04/04/2025 – It was a true celebration that lit up the evening in Livigno for the Livigno Team Awards, a special event dedicated to the athletes who proudly carried the name of the town to the top throughout this

extraordinary, success-filled season.

Maurizio Bormolini, Flora Tabanelli, Miro Tabanelli, Jole Galli, Michela Moioli, and many others: an exceptional team that made this past winter truly unforgettable. Their achievements sparked excitement and pride among the large crowd that gathered to cheer on and celebrate the champions of the Livigno Team.

Thunderous applause was reserved for the brightest stars of the Livigno Team, such as Jole Galli, who made a stunning comeback this season after last year's injury. She claimed two World Cup victories, earned a World Championship bronze medal, and finished fourth overall in the Ski Cross standings—a remarkable track record that fuels high hopes for the 2026 Winter Olympics, when the discipline’s medals will be awarded right here in Livigno.

Sharing the same Olympic dream is Maurizio Bormolini, another athlete born and raised in Livigno, who delivered a truly flawless season. He clinched the overall World Cup title in Parallel, along with the Parallel Giant Slalom World Cup title and the World Championship crown. His total dominance in the discipline was clear, and during the Livigno Team Awards, Bormolini shared powerful and inspiring words with the many young athletes gathered below the stage—leaving a lasting impression on the next generation of champions.

A special chapter, and extra ovations, were reserved for siblings Miro and Flora Tabanelli, who made history by winning the X Games together, establishing themselves on the international stage as two of the top talents in their respective disciplines.

Flora, in particular, stunned the world during what was essentially her first season among the elite. With three victories, numerous podiums, and an extraordinary level of consistency and versatility, she claimed the Big Air World Cup title, achieved a sensational win in the overall Freestyle World Cup, and captured World Championship gold in Big Air at the St. Moritz World Championships. At just 17 years old, she’s already a phenomenon—an athlete destined for greatness who has ignited dreams across the entire Italian freestyle movement.
Big applause also went to Michela Moioli, newly crowned World Champion, who couldn’t attend in person as she was competing in Canada for the final World Cup event of the season—but she joined the celebration with a heartfelt video message.
Beyond the stars, the evening was filled with affection and recognition for all the athletes, with a particularly emotional moment dedicated to Paolo Rodigari, who was honored as he officially marked the end of his competitive career.

A day of celebration, certainly—but one that aligns with clear goals for growth and development, as emphasized by Luca Moretti, President of Livigno Next: “The Livigno Team has two souls: on one hand, it rewards excellence in all its forms, and the trophies and medals here tonight are proof of that once again. On the other hand, it embraces sport as a powerful tool for social promotion and for strengthening the community. Seeing so many young athletes gathered near the stage, celebrating our champions and dreaming of one day becoming part of the Livigno Team, perfectly captures this dual spirit.”

The Livigno Team continues to grow, welcoming key additions such as Gregory Barnaby and Giorgia Priarone in triathlon, and Andrea Pusateri in para-cycling. These are athletes of remarkable caliber, ready to bring new trophies to Livigno’s already impressive collection. But beyond the results lies a strong human dimension. These three athletes have fully embraced the spirit of Livigno—not just as a launchpad for future success, but as a true sporting family. A place to grow, to inspire the next generation, and to be part of a community that lives and breathes sport as a way of life.
