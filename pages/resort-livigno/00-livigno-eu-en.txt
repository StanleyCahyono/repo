LIVIGNO
ALPINE ENERGY AT THE HIGHEST LEVEL
Your summer adventure
begins at 1.816mt
Performance, nature, freedom.
Everything you’re looking for is in Livigno.
There is a place that becomes the starting point for those who want to push further: among high‑altitude trails, speed‑shaped flow lines, and the legendary passes that made cycling history. Welcome to Livigno, land of sport and Olympic venue, where the legacy of major international events continues to inspire athletes, travelers, and anyone seeking their own dimension at altitude. Over 1,500 km of trekking routes across panoramic ridges and silent valleys. 3,200 km of bike trails spanning road cycling, flow trails, and downhill in the bike parks. A high‑altitude plateau perfect for training, chosen by Olympians and international teams. But Livigno is also a world for families, Italian style, shopping along the Fashion Mile, and gourmet experiences blending alpine flavors with contemporary creativity.

YOUR SUMMER IN LIVIGNO
Experience the warm season in every way you want.
TREKKING
High‑altitude paths, shifting light.
Steps finding their rhythm.
Panoramic routes, quiet valleys, trails opening toward the horizon. In Livigno, trekking is an invitation to move, breathe, and go beyond—between easy walks and technical itineraries across 1,500 km of freedom at altitude.
DISCOVER MORE
ROAD BIKE
Legendary climbs, thin air.
Curves that demand precision.
High‑altitude roads, iconic gradients, landscapes that change at every turn. Livigno is a privileged starting point for those who want to challenge the great legendary Alpine climbs and live cycling as true performance.
DISCOVER MORE
MTB
Bends shaped for speed.
Flow turning into pure movement.
3,200 km of fluid lines, berms, and constant rhythm. Livigno’s flow trails are designed for those who love to glide, accelerate, and find their own flow through forests, clearings, and high‑altitude views.
DISCOVER MORE
DOWNHILL
Drops, speed, gravity pulling.
A descent that fires every instinct.
Drops, jumps, rock gardens, and technical lines. Livigno’s Bike Park is the ideal playground for those seeking pure adrenaline and wanting to push to the limit on sculpted descents and rider‑ready tracks.
DISCOVER MORE
HIGH‑ALTITUDE TRAINING
Altitude, breath, growing power.
Performance at 1.816mt
In Livigno, the body works better, deeper. That’s why it’s the training destination for athletes and international teams preparing, training, and improving endurance, power, and recovery in a unique natural environment.
DISCOVER MORE
EVENTS
Summer that sparks the rhythm.
Days become a stage.
Music, sport, culture, and shared moments transform Livigno into a plateau of collective energy. Open‑air festivals, family activities, and evenings under the stars animate the summer, creating a calendar of events that brings together community, guests, and nature in one vibrant experience.

Alpine spirit,
Olympic spirit
Livigno carries with it an Olympic legacy that continues to shape its sporting character. The Olympic and World Championships hosted here have left a lasting mark: world-class facilities and a culture of excellence. A valley where sport is an integral part of its identity.
