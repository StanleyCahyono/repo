MUSIC AT HIGH ALTITUDE: LIVIGNO CELEBRATES FERRAGOSTO WITH THE LIVE PERFORMANCE OF SIMON&MAC

13/08/2025

On the occasion of the Ferragosto celebrations, Livigno will host a concert by the duo Simon & Mac, taking place on Friday, August 15 at 9:00 PM in Piazza del Comune.

 

Livigno, 03.07.2025 – The duo is known for their captivating pop style and their ability to attract a diverse audience through a fusion of traditional accordion, electric drums, and modern pop/EDM, creating a one-of-a-kind Alps-EDM show. They are currently on tour across some of the most prestigious Alpine destinations and, after the success achieved during Alpenfest 2024, they are ready to return to Livigno with their “Simon & Mac Live Show,” promising an evening full of energy and entertainment.

The event, with free admission, is a special occasion for both residents and visitors to celebrate Ferragosto surrounded by music and the unique atmosphere of Livigno, a place renowned for offering original and unforgettable experiences.

The live show will take place in Piazza del Comune, starting at 9:00 PM
