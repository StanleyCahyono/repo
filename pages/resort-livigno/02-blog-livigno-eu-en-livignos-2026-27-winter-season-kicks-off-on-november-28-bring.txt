LIVIGNO’S 2026/27 WINTER SEASON KICKS OFF ON NOVEMBER 28, BRINGING INNOVATION, HIGH-QUALITY EXPERIENCES AND OLYMPIC SPIRIT

LIVIGNO 28/09/2026

An ever-evolving ski area, upgraded services and an increasingly international offering make Livigno one of the Alps’ leading destinations.

 

Livigno is preparing to kick off the 2026/27 winter season on November 28, reaffirming its position as one of the most comprehensive and dynamic Alpine destinations on the international stage. The start of the new season reflects a path of continuous growth, where innovation, quality and the enhancement of the local area come together in a consistent and integrated way.

With over 115 kilometres of slopes, an elevation of 1,816 metres, and some of the most reliable snow conditions in the Alps, Livigno offers a broad and varied skiing experience designed to meet the needs of a diverse audience, from families and winter sports enthusiasts to the most demanding skiers.

The 2026/27 season also comes at a particularly significant time for the destination, as Livigno continues to build on the legacy of its Olympic experience. This milestone has helped strengthen Livigno’s international positioning, further consolidating its identity as a modern, sport-focused destination with a strong commitment to the ongoing development of its services. Guests will therefore be able to experience the mountains through the very places that have hosted and welcomed some of the leading figures in international sport for training and competition.

In line with this ongoing development, investment in the ski area continues, with targeted upgrades to lift systems and infrastructure aimed at delivering an increasingly efficient experience. This evolution extends across the entire tourism ecosystem, with accommodation and services focused on high quality standards and increasingly well equipped to meet the needs of a growing international clients.

Among the initiatives confirmed for the 2026/27 season is the return of Skipass Free, available from November 28 to December 19, 2026, and from April 10 to May 2, 2027. The offer includes a complimentary ski pass for stays of at least 4 ski days and 5 nights in a hotel, or 6 ski days and 7 nights in an apartment, with direct booking through participating accommodation providers or via the official website, livigno.eu. The initiative makes access to the ski area even more straightforward and attractive.

The offer extends across a total skiable area of more than 100 km², with over 30 kilometres dedicated to cross-country skiing, all set within a natural environment of outstanding scenic value.

Against this backdrop, Livigno continues to stand out as a destination that successfully combines Alpine tradition with a contemporary outlook, offering a winter experience that brings together sport, hospitality and quality, driven by a consistent commitment to ongoing development.
