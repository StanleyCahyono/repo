Hit the slopes with us from December 12, 2026 to April 24, 2027

This winter, set your sights on a memorable holiday. Les Arcs / Peisey-Vallandry Mountain Area awaits you in the heart of the Alps with 200 km of ski slopes, 16 high-altitude activities and 6 unique educational areas. 70% of the ski area is located above 2,000 metres, ensuring excellent snow quality throughout the season. Thanks to the Vanoise Express cable car, discover the resort of La Plagne and enjoy the Paradiski experience, the second largest ski area in the world with 425 km of slopes.

Les Arcs is a Flocon Vert-certified destination and the mountain area is B Corp certified: enjoy a more responsible holiday experience. Take advantage of access to this unique destination in France thanks to the train + funicular combo from Bourg Saint Maurice. Fully committed to your satisfaction, the Domaine de Montagne teams are ready to welcome you this winter. Are you?
