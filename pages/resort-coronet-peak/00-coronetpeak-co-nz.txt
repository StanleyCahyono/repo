CORONET PEAK

Just 20 minutes from Queenstown, Coronet Peak is your local legend known for its rollercoaster terrain, wide open trails, and unforgettable views. Since 1947, we’ve been serving up Good Times from sunrise corduroy and legendary après, to Night Ski under the stars. Close, iconic, and made for making memories.

QUEENSTOWN’S MOST EXCITING TERRAIN
CORONET PEAK. BUILT TO THRILL.

Coronet Peak is built for thrill-seekers and cruisers alike. Our rollercoaster terrain delivers wide-open groomers, natural rollers, gullies, steeps and mellow zones perfect for every kind of skier and rider.

‍

Whether you're learning the ropes, chasing performance, or lapping with the crew, the Peak delivers variety, views, and velocity. With world-class snowmaking and a vibe like no other, this is Queenstown’s most playful mountain.

SUMMER

We may be a snow lovers dream over winter, but we keep the good times rolling through to summer. Here at The Peak, we’re proud to be home to the Southern Hemisphere’s longest maintained mountain bike single-track, measuring 1,200 meters from top to bottom. We’re an alpine adventure playground for all.
