**Notes
“Shift” passes are valid at either Song Mountain or Labrador Mountain for one 8-hour lift ticket per week, Mon thru Fri, or Sat from 4 pm till closing, for the entire season. No makeups for missed weeks.
“Saturday” or “Sunday” passes are valid EITHER ALL Saturdays or ALL Sundays, ALL season, and are mountain and day specific.
“Weekday” passes are valid on one chosen day, Mon-Fri, the same day ALL season, and are mountain and day specific.
Individual Once-A-Week Passes do not include lessons. Add $175 to include 4 lessons, running for 4 consecutive weeks beginning the first full week of January on your respective day.
Private Lesson 6-Pacs are good for 6 private anytime lessons. They do not expire.
Rental 6-Pacs are good for 6 complete rental packages for the season and are non-transferable. Ski Package = skis, boots, and poles. Board package = snowboard and boots.
