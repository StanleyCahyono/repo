May 3, 2026 - 04:15 pm

Last chair has been called, and Copper Mountain is now closed for the 2025-26 winter season. Thank you for being a part of a memorable year on the slopes! While snowfall didn’t always cooperate, there were still plenty of great days to be had across the mountain. You all showed up, made the most of every run, and helped make it a season worth celebrating.

We're already looking forward to next winter, with hopes of more powder days than we can handle and classic Colorado bluebird days peppered in. 

Until then, the fun doesn't stop. Join us this summer for golf at Copper Creek, downhill bike park laps, and a full lineup of great summer activities and events culminating with the Union Peak Festival in late August.

We look forward to seeing you this summer and again next winter!
