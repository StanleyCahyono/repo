Season pass - Magic Pass

The season pass for Saas-Fee and Saas-Almagell is officially included in the Magic Pass.

This pass provides access to the mountain railways and ski slopes in Saas-Fee and Saas-Almagell during the winter season.

Magic Pass

Information
Decisive for the children and youth fare is the date of birth (not the year of birth).
Including ski bus Saas-Almagell to Saas-Fee with SaastalCard
Multi-day ski passes must be redeemed on consecutive days and the skiing days are not freely selectable.
Group rate applies for groups of 20 or more people: 10 per cent off standard ski pass prices. No free passes.
Family hit

Children under 5.99 years are free of charge.
Children up to 8.99 years free of charge when purchasing a minimum 6-day pass of a parent with SaastalCard (season & annual passes excluded)

Information

Decisive for the children and youth fare is the date of birth (not the year of birth).
When purchasing a parent's online ticket (6-day pass), an identity card and the valid SaastalCard must be presented at the ticket office. Afterwards, a free ski pass will be issued to the child (check of the identity card ).
The family hit is not valid with season or annual tickets.

Discounts and rebates are not cumulative.

Ski pass insurance and refunds

Ski passes in the Saas-Fee, Saas-Grund and Saas-Almagell ski areas are automatically insured through Europ Assistance. This means winter sports enthusiasts can enjoy even greater safety and carefree skiing fun.

Additional insurance
Additional ‘SnowAssist Light’ insurance can be taken out for emergency transport.

Ski pass

Buy your ski pass online and load it directly onto your SwissPass or an existing KeyCard. Don't have a KeyCard? Then book one and simply pick up your ski pass at one of the pick-up stations at the Alpin Express, Spielboden or Hohsaas mountain lifts.
