Rentals Pricing

Swain offers both ski and snowboard rentals. Ski rentals include skis, boots, and poles. A snowboard rental includes a snowboard and boots. We now also offer ski bikes and twin tip skis. Ski bikes require a demonstration for your first time use, and is included in the price.

Please note: Poles are not guaranteed with rentals and are available on a first come first serve basis.

Group Tickets
Discount Packages for Groups

Get your friends and family together for your next trip to Swain and save big! Swain is the place to be for family and friends and offers great discounts on lift tickets, lessons, rentals & lodging for groups of 15 or more skiers.

Reservations must be made in advance for group trips and single payment (cash, check or credit card) is required to receive group pricing.

For more information, please call 607-545-6511, email groups@swain.com, or submit our inquiry form linked below. Don’t forget that every group trip to Swain earns you additional Swain complimentary lift tickets. Get one free lift ticket per 15 people for every visit!

Discounts Available for School Clubs!

Swain has one of the largest school club programs in all of New York state. We welcome all school groups & colleges interested in coming for the day or multiple field trips.
