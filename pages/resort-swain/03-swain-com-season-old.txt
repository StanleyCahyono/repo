Enjoy unlimited skiing & boarding, as long as you want, whenever you want. But there’s more gained from becoming a season pass holder at Swain than just infinite time on the slopes!

Additional benefits include discounted lift tickets to share, free and discounted lesson packages, great deals on lodging PLUS all of the free and discounted lift passes to our reciprocal partners! Not to mention, the warm welcoming ski & board community that knows how to kick back and have fun.

There’s more to a Swain Season Pass than just our awesome community and hometown vibe.

Swain Season Passes include access to many of our reciprocal partner mountains PLUS a host of VIP benefits, including:

Discounts on dining and retail
15% lodging discount
Discounted lift tickets for your friends
Lesson discounts for your friends!

Discounts at Stokoe Farms
Plus, discounted reciprocal lift tickets to other mountains such as Holiday Valley, Whiteface Mountain, Hunt Hollow, and more! 
Season Pass Use Policy
Season Passes are non-transferrable and are only to be used by the individual named on the pass.
Family Rates are for parents or guardians and their children. Dependents are under the age of 22 at time of payment, living at home or attending college full time.
Dependent persons are those claimed on your Federal Tax Return for 2024.
Ages 6 & Under on December 1, 2025, are $15 with a paying adult
Ages 75 & Up at time of payment are $15 anytime, photo ID required
College students must present or send a copy of photo college ID before Student Pass will be issued
Homeschool students must present a copy of an IHIPP to be eligible for a discounted season pass.
