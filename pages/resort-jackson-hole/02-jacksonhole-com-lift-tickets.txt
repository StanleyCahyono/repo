How To Ski If Full-Day Tickets Are Sold Out

1. Purchase a half-day lift ticket (12-4 PM).

Head to the online store, pick your lift ticket product and select "1/2 Day" in the Number of Days dropdown box to see availability. The dropdown will look like the image below:

Please note: half-day lift tickets may also sell out.  

2. Sign up for a ski or snowboard lesson. 

Please call 877-482-1466 and have your order number confirming your lesson purchase ready when calling, to secure lift tickets for sold-out dates.

Explore Lessons

Please note: lessons may also sell out.  

3. Bundle lift tickets with lodging through JHRR.

Call Jackson Hole Resort Reservations at 866-265-4620 to see if they have lift tickets available if you book your lodging through them.

Please note: JHRR may not always have extra lift tickets.

4. Use your Flex 5 ticket*

The Flex 5 allows you to ski any 5 days within a 7-day period at any point during the season, including sold-out days, with no reservation needed.

*Flex 5 Blackout Dates: Dec. 19. 2025 - Jan. 4. 2026 and Feb. 9-22, 2026

Lift Ticket Details
It is recommended that lift tickets and passes be carried on the left side of the user's body in an area that is free from items that will disrupt the scanning process.
For your safety, delays in opening lifts may occur due to weather and avalanche mitigation.
All prices are subject to change without notice due to seasonal dates, weather and business conditions.
Lift Ticket Ages
Teen: 13-18
Youth: 5-12
Seniors: 65 years and over, photo ID required.
Adults: 19 to 64 years of age.
Children 4 and under ski free.
Guests can cancel up to 7 days before their arrival for a full refund. Cancellations within 7 days of the ticket date will not be refunded unless a documented extenuating circumstance, which will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis with Guest Services.
Lift Tickets are not transferable.
Prices and availability subject to change
Those with a reservation at Piste but no lift ticket may purchase a $20 Bridger Gondola ticket at the ticket window with proof of reservation.
You must complete your purchase to secure your ticket. Simply adding to your cart will not hold the ticket for you.

If you have any further questions or concerns, please call 877-482-1466 or e-mail us at info@jacksonhole.com

From The Blog
Snowiest Winter Ever at Jackson Hole

That's a wrap for the record-setting 2022-23 winter season at Jackson Hole. 595" of snow fell making this winter the snowiest, deepest ever recorded in Rendezvous Bowl! These past months have raced by, fueled by fresh flakes cascading onto our slopes and ample time sliding on snow. The "Teton trickle" felt more like a firehose at times, and the sun was most-elusive. March was miraculous and we though it best to put how absurdly snowy last month was into perspective (read more).

60" in 6 Days

Update 3/4/23: We continue to get pounded by snow here in the Tetons. It seems like mother nature can't stop and won't stop. Since initial post on 2/28/23, we've received another 46" of high-grade Teton blower. The skiing has been incredible, and the snow doesn't seem to be stopping anytime soon!

Unending powder continues to hit Jackson Hole this week. Tuesday morning greeted skiers and riders with 16 inches of powdered sugar covered slopes. We're currently at 137% of our annual snowfall average and the entire mountain is skiing great.
