Jackson Hole Announces 2026-27 Opening Day and Early Bird Ticket Sale
Jackson Hole has announced their opening date for the 2026-27 ski season while launching its annual Early Bird Lift Ticket Sale.
by Nolan Deck
August 10, 2026
 X FacebookMore
Jackson Hole Mountain Resort.

Jackson Hole Mountain Resort has announced their opening date for the 2026-27 ski season while launching its annual Early Bird Lift Ticket Sale, giving skiers and snowboarders an opportunity to lock in discounted tickets before winter arrives. The sale began today, Monday, August 10th, and runs through August 31st. The promotion offers its best combination of pricing and availability for the upcoming season.

Jackson Hole Early Bird Tickets Offer Major Savings

Early Bird pricing is available for tickets throughout the 2026-27 season, including holidays and peak periods. The largest discounts will be available on select non-peak dates, with savings reaching up to 50% on single-day lift tickets and up to 65% on multi-day tickets of three days or more.

Limited quantities are available at each pricing tier. Single-day tickets start at $81 on select dates, while qualifying multi-day tickets start at $57 per day. Visitors are encouraged to purchase early, as buying sooner provides access to the lowest available rates and a wider selection of dates.

Jackson Hole Opening Day Set for November 27

Jackson Hole Mountain Resort will officially open for the 2026-27 winter season on Friday, November 27th, 2026. The Wyoming resort is known for its substantial snowfall and challenging big mountain terrain. Jackson Hole averages 458 inches of snow annually and features 4,139 feet of continuous vertical rise across 2,500 acres of in-bounds terrain.

The mountain offers terrain for beginners, intermediates and experts, although Jackson Hole has built its reputation around its extensive advanced and expert terrain.

“There are mountains you visit, and there’s the one you never stop thinking about. For generations, Jackson Hole Mountain Resort has been the mountain skiers and riders have measured everything else against, with legendary terrain, untracked powder days, and a wild western spirit unlike anywhere else. There’s a reason we’ve always been called The Big One, and this winter we’re inviting skiers and riders to come find out why.” – Jackson Hole Mountain Resort CEO Doug Pierini.

Air Credits Available With Vacation Packages

Visitors who book qualifying lodging and activities through Jackson Hole Resort Reservations can also receive an air credit worth up to $500 per person, with a maximum credit of $2,000 for a family of four. Blackout dates apply. The resort’s reservation service can package airfare, lodging, transportation, lift tickets and activities into a single vacation booking.

Jackson Hole will also continue managing daily mountain capacity during the 2026-27 season. Ikon Pass and Mountain Collective passholders will be required to make reservations.
