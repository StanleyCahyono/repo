Benefits Explained
Winter Benefits
4 WINTER DISCOUNTED BUDDY PASSES❄️
Reservations made after opening day, enjoy 35% off buddy passes. (Adult and Senior only). Reservations made before opening day, enjoy 50% discounts on 4 one-day JHMR lift tickets for friends or family to use.
EARLY SEASON SKI/SNOWBOARD CLINIC❄️
Complimentary Mountain Sports School Early Season Program to refresh your skiing and riding techniques. Online registration required. (Adult and Senior only).
50% OFF DAY TICKETS AT MOUNTAIN COLLECTIVE RESORTS❄️
Purchase unlimited 50% off 1-day in-resort rate lift tickets at the other Mountain Collective resorts.
OPTIONAL IKON BASE PASS ADD-ON❄️
$399 Ikon Base Pass add-on available at checkout. View Ikon Base Pass Guidelines.
Summer Benefits
SUMMER 2026 SIGHTSEEING PASS☀️
Complimentary Summer sightseeing lift access to the Aerial Tram, Bridger Gondola, and Sweetwater Gondola. Includes the evening gondola rides on the Bridger Gondola.
4 SUMMER 2026 EVENING GONDOLA TICKETS☀️
4 one-time evening gondola rides for friends and family on the Bridger Gondola. (Adult and Senior only).
30% OFF SUMMER 2026 BIKE PARK SEASON PASS AND DAY TICKETS☀️
Purchase a Summer 2026 Bike Park Season Pass or Bike Park day tickets at 30% off.
Year Round Benefits
10% OFF FOOD & BEVERAGE❄️☀️
Get unlimited 10% off at all JHMR dining locations.
10% OFF RETAIL/RENTAL/REPAIR❄️☀️
Get unlimited 10% off at JHMR retail, rental, and repair locations. Regular-priced items only (excludes current model year bike purchases).
Other Pass Benefits
JH SKI CLUB CARD❄️☀️
Local discount membership card for the 2026-27 season. Details on discounts here.
WINTER START BUS PASS❄️
START Bus Winter pass for the Winter 2026-27 season.

More Information
Season passes have limited availability. Winter 2026-27 passes may sell out at any time and will not go back on sale. There is no waitlist.
Season dates subject to change due to snow and business conditions.
All season pass pricing and products subject to change without notice.
Benefits are valid from purchase date through the end of the season.
Please contact the Season Pass Office at seasonpass@jacksonhole.com, call us at 855-869-3488 or use our chat bubble for more info
