Rental Information
Guests are strongly encouraged to book rentals online, in advance, to ensure availability on your desired date and streamline the rental pickup process.
The Shop at Windham Mountain Club is conveniently located on the 2nd floor of The Lodge.
Ski rental packages include skis, boots, poles, and helmet.
Snowboard rental packages include snowboard, boots, and helmet.
The Shop at Windham Mountain Club does not service or provide support for any rental equipment rented off-site.

The Demo Center

The Demo Center at the Shop at Windham Mountain Club offers a curated, on-snow experience designed for discerning skiers who want to invest with confidence. Featuring an exceptional selection of our top skis available to demo, guests can experience the craftsmanship, performance, and feel of each model before making a purchase. Our collection includes premier brands such as Van Deer, Stöckli, Volkl, Atomic, DPS, Blizzard, and Rossignol, ensuring access to the finest equipment tailored to your style and expectations. 

For more information, please call The Shop at 518-734-4300 ext. 1191.
