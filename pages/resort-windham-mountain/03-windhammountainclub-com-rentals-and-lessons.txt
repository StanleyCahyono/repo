RENTALS & LESSONS AT WINDHAM

GEAR UP & GO

Create peak moments on the mountain with hands-on lessons, seasonal programs, and easy access to rentals in The Shop. With tailored lessons and programs designed for every level, plus gear from skis and boards to boots and helmets, we got you covered for your seamless alpine experience.

RENTALS & THE DEMO CENTER
Ski & Demos

Located within The Shop at Windham Mountain Club, our exclusive rental location and The Demo Center makes it easy to find the right gear for your day on the slopes. Guests are strongly encouraged to book rentals online in advance to ensure availability on your desired date and streamline the rental pickup process. At The Demo Center, explore premium skis through our curated on-snow experience and find the right fit for your style.

LESSONS AT WINDHAM
The Ski & Ride School

Windham Mountain Club’s Ski and Ride School offers a variety of tailored experiences for guests of all levels. From first-time skiers to future elite performers, our private, one-to-one snow lessons, group lessons, and premium programs are designed around each guest's goals. With personalized instruction and expert guidance, you'll have everything you need to build confidence, refine your technique, and create mountain memories.

SAFETY AT WMC
Safety Information & Policies

Your safety is our top priority. Whether you’re a seasoned skier or a first-time visitor, ensuring a safe and enjoyable experience on the slopes is essential.
