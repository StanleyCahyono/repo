Windham Mountain Club is New York’s four-season mountain escape for generations of adventure. As a member here, you’ll discover a passionate community and world-class skiing, golf, dining, and adventure in the breathtaking wilderness of Upstate NY.

 

CONTACT US
A New Kind of Members' Club

Not your average ski resort. Definitely not your father’s country club. At Windham Mountain Club, this is mountain life, authentically lived. You’re joining like-minded community in inspired surroundings, where family, friends, and fellow members prioritize the slopes over status, exploration over the ordinary, and authentic warmth over stuffy tradition. Come be part of something special, where natural beauty, meaningful connection, and big-mountain skiing is in your backyard.

EXPLORE CLUB AMENITIES
Limitless skiing, no lift lines.

At Windham, every chair feels like first chair. Greet sunrise with a smile and experience the joy of untouched snow underfoot with zero lift lines. Experience having 54 trails of diverse terrain to yourself before lifts open to the general public, and enjoy members-only priority lift line privileges all day long.
