AUSTRALIA’S HIGHEST ALPINE VILLAGE

Unlike other resorts, Hotham’s village sits high among the mountain peaks at 1750m. Wake up to glorious panoramic views, step outside into the crisp alpine air, click into gear from ski-in ski-out stays, and get unbeatable first turns in - right from the top.

Signing off for the 2026 season

6 September - That's a wrap on winter at Hotham. Mother Nature made us work for it this season, but embracing the challenge is part of who we are up here. It’s what makes Hotham so special! 

Sunday 6 September will be our last day of skiing and boarding for winter

1 September - Our teams have given it all that they have to keep us skiing and riding as long as we can, but Mother Nature had other plans for us this season, and with some tough conditions in the forecast, our final day of skiing and boarding for winter will be Sunday 6 September. 

Epic Australia Passes Now on Sale for 2027

12 August 2026 – Vail Resorts today launched Epic Australia Passes for the 2027 Australian and 2027/28 Northern Hemisphere ski seasons at the lowest price of the year. Three Epic Australia Pass options deliver choice, unbeatable value and a range of flexibility for Australian skiers and snowboarders to access their next snow holiday.

The Storm Has Arrived!

We’re in the thick of the storm at Hotham, which has brought a mix of wild weather to the alps. But we've recieved 10cm of snow since temps dropped in the early hours of the morning, and it's still drumping.

11cm down, more incoming...

We've recieved a 11cm top-up over the past two days, and it’s about to get a whole lot better (and deeper). 

Gotcha Chair Opening Saturday

Gotcha chair is set to spin tomorrow, giving both intermediate and expert riders extra space to explore!

15cm Storm Total

Snow has made its return to Hotham! 15cm of the fresh stuff blanketed the resort in white, alongside guns firing!

Heavenly Valley Chair is SPINNING!

Hotham’s favourite lift is now spinning - opening the gateway to some of our steepest and most iconic terrain for advanced and expert riders.  

July 14: 26cm storm total + Road Runner Open

A total of 26cm has fallen since the storm rolled in & Road Runner Chair is now spinning!

July 12: 17cm and Village Chair Open!

It's still dumping AND fun laps on the Village loop await!
