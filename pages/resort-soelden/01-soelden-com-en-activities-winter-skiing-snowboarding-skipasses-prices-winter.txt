YOUR TICKET FOR THE WHITE CARPET

How many appearances in the Sölden ski area can I have? From beginner tickets, staggered day tickets to multi-day tickets and season tickets, you can satisfy your winter sports appetite in a very flexible way. The Ötztal Superskipass, which is automatically valid in all ski areas in the Ötztal from 3 days, provides even more variety.

OVERVIEW OF CASH DESK PRICES
1 AND 2 DAY SKI PASSES

Ski passes up to 2 days are only valid in the SKIGEBIET SÖLDEN.

*already discounted group price for adults - more information to the group rates.
The price quoted is the valid cash desk price. Discounted rates are available in the online ski ticket shop. The earlier you buy the ticket, the cheaper the price.
The cheaper online prices are available up to 5 days before the first day of skiing, after that (4 days before) the cash desk price also applies online.
